`[ ]` **Open — genuinely not fixed this session, by the dispatch's own explicit instruction.** `index.html`'s `<meta name="viewport">` carries both `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=no`, which together disable the browser's own pinch-to-zoom on every page — a WCAG 2.1 **1.4.4 (Resize Text)** failure: a low-vision user who relies on the OS/browser pinch-zoom to read small text cannot, anywhere in the app, including the marketing landing page. The two in-app canvases (Leaflet map, the Site Planner/Review SVG canvas) already implement their OWN pinch-zoom via touch/pointer events — B1168128's own header note and the mobile-pinch-fix `<script>` in `index.html` both confirm this is deliberate, to stop a two-finger gesture on the CANVAS from also zooming the whole page — so the fix is narrower than "delete the attribute": it needs a change that keeps the canvas's own pinch-zoom working while restoring the browser's native pinch-zoom everywhere else (e.g. on ordinary text/chrome), which is real design work, not a one-line deletion, and is why this stays flagged rather than attempted inline here.
