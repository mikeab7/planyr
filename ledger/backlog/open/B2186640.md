### B2186640 — Hard-deleting a plan is slow enough to time out, so a harness's "deleted" plan can be re-created by the app and linger `[sites / cleanup]` (bug) #db #cleanup

Found 2026-10-08 running V1502416 signed in. `sites_block_delete_live_group()` (BEFORE DELETE trigger) finds live group siblings with `where coalesce(s.data->>'groupId', s.id) = gkey` — an unindexed expression over the whole `sites` table with jsonb reads, per deleted row. Measured: a single-row `DELETE … WHERE id=…` via SQL and via the client timed out (60 s) on production while the table was idle of long transactions; client deletes issued while the app was open were also out-run by the app re-pushing its local copy (rows reappeared live). Soft-delete (`deleted_at`) is fast and hides the row from the app. Leftover trashed rows from the check: `zz-ga-{pied,coast,hsi,nrhp,cem,ch,tr,sb,sl,an}-7ggr` (test-account only, trashed).
- Fix direction: an expression index on `coalesce(data->>'groupId', id)` (or compare against the `group_id` column the trigger's own comments call a drifting mirror — decide), then purge the trashed leftovers. Needs a migration against production; not applied in this session.
- Verify: sandbox (EXPLAIN before/after + a timed single-row purge).
- Stopping rule: closes when a single-row purge of a trashed plan completes in seconds and the nine leftover rows are gone.
