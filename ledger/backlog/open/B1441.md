- **WHAT THE OLD BAR COST.** B1345's bar — byte-identical, or one unit of 255 on one channel — is a measurement of the FILE. It refused B1350's 424-node dock-door fold twice, the second time (PR #921) for a cause that turned out to be that Chromium does not rasterise a `<rect>` and a rectangular `<path>` to the same antialiased edge **at any zoom**, so no gate could ever have satisfied it.
- **THE CONSTRUCTION, and it is the substance of the item.** A raw diff cannot tell **the same ink moved a sub-pixel** (invisible) from **a line of ink removed** (a downgrade) — both read ~23/255, and refusing both is exactly what byte-identity did, at the price of refusing every representation change forever. So both renders are low-pass filtered **at two scales** before comparison: one near visual acuity (which bounds how far any single sample may stray) and one inside the eye's high-sensitivity band (which is what the eye actually receives after it integrates). The colour metric is **CIEDE2000**, not a channel delta — 10/255 in a dark blue and 10/255 in a light yellow are nothing alike to a viewer, which is the second reason "23/255" was never an answer to "can he see it".
- **THE BAR, three magnitudes:** detail ΔE00 ≤ **6.0** · perceived ΔE00 ≤ **1.0** (the classical just-noticeable difference; the figure usually quoted for ordinary viewing is ~2.3 and we deliberately took the strict end) · perceived frame-mean ΔE00 ≤ **0.10**. **Coverage is REPORTED but does not gate** — an earlier draft capped the share of the frame allowed to differ and it was removed before shipping, because for an antialiasing-class change the pixels touched scale with the total EDGE LENGTH IN THE DRAWING, i.e. with how much is drawn rather than how much changed; a dense plan would have failed for being dense.
- **THE VIEWING GEOMETRY IS STATED, NOT ASSUMED.** 20/20 acuity (1 arcmin), 600 mm viewing distance, 0.50 mm per CSS pixel (a 27″ 2560-wide panel at his measured dpr ~2.15) → **one CSS pixel subtends ~2.9 arcmin**. All three are parameters, every run prints the ones it used, and the two this repo cannot measure from a sandbox — his panel's physical width and how far he sits from it — are on `OWNER-TODO.md`.
- **WHAT SHIPPED.** `ui-audit/lib/perceptualDiff.mjs` (pure: sRGB→linear→Lab, CIEDE2000, separable Gaussian **in linear light** — averaging gamma-encoded values is not averaging light, and the whole claim rests on ink averaging back) + `ui-audit/verify-perceptual-parity.mjs`, which drives two builds at a zoom ladder and prints every number plus the geometry, never a bare verdict. The **engine is unchanged**: the same dependency-free PNG decoder (`lib/pngDiff.mjs`) B1345 built.
- **⛔ THE FIRST REAL USE REJECTED THE CHANGE IT WAS INTRODUCED FOR, and the bar was left alone.** B1350's fold came in at perceived ΔE00 **1.20–2.19** against 1.0. The one modelling parameter the verdict turns on (`PERCEIVED_ARCMIN` = 6, the half-period at the contrast-sensitivity peak; 12 would roughly halve every figure) was chosen **before** the measurement and deliberately not touched **after**. Picking a threshold to suit a result you have already seen is not a measurement — and a bar that rubber-stamps the first thing put in front of it would be worth nothing.
- **Guard.** `test/perceptualParity.test.js` (37): CIEDE2000 against **Sharma/Wu/Dalal's published test vectors** — that formula has more special cases than anything else in this repo (the hue-average wrap, the zero-chroma branch, the blue-region rotation) and a subtly wrong one would still return plausible small numbers, the most dangerous shape a metric can have — plus the discriminator itself (ink moved a sub-pixel PASSES, the same magnitude of ink REMOVED fails, and the two separate by more than 3×, with a companion assertion that a raw channel diff ranks them the wrong way round), and all three thresholds pinned by value.
- **The rule is written into `/CLAUDE.md` as PERCEPTUAL-PARITY**, naming that it supersedes B1345 for LOD-class changes, that the threshold is chosen before the measurement, and that every picture-altering change records a before/after crop.
