- **THE MEASUREMENT, on a seeded stub whose body is 7×18 px.** After press 1: **0 selection nodes, no panel** — the owner's report verbatim. After press 2: the road's path data **CHANGED** (`d` length 5778 → 5852), its box went 7×18 → 15×22 and its handle layer 0 → 15 nodes. **A double-click silently re-cut the road's alignment and burnt an undo frame.**
- **THE CAUSE.** `<g data-road-radius-flag>` paints AFTER the element pass, carried **no `data-feature`**, stopped propagation on pointerdown, set no selection and never called `isDoubleTap` — while its corner dot is a **7 px disc with an "!" on it**, marking a vertex and therefore painted ON that road's own body. On a road this size the review chrome is wider than the road. Its `onClick` ran `fixRoadRadiusFor`, which rewrites `pts`/`vtx` and pushes history.
- **THE FIX, and it is the rule applied rather than a special case.** The **DOT** sits on the road, so it IDENTIFIES AS the road (`data-feature`) and FORWARDS the press: single click selects, a pair opens Properties, right-click opens the road's own menu. The **LABEL PILL** sits in clear space on a leader, so it keeps the immediate one-click Fix and deliberately carries NO `data-feature` (it can overhang anything, and claiming to be the road there is the same mis-identification in the other direction).
- **⚠ THE DELIBERATE TRADE, stated rather than buried — this changes owner-facing behaviour.** The DOT no longer applies the fix on one click; the LABEL does. A click on your road may never silently re-cut it, and a dot bigger than the road it marks cannot be told apart from the road. The label unfolds only once the plan is zoomed in enough to act on the corner — which is the same zoom band where the dot stops being the biggest thing on the road — and the dot keeps its tooltip. **A deferral was built first and rejected:** holding the fix for the double-click window preserved the one-click dot, but it left a single click on a road still editing that road, which is the actual defect.
- **Guards:** `test/featureTarget.test.js` — the dot identifies as its road and forwards, the dot no longer calls `act()`, the label keeps the one-click Fix and claims no feature.
