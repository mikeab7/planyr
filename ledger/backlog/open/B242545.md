**WHY THIS IS NOT COSMETIC, verified (the brief asked).** The owner's Colorado site is in **Weld County**, which sits over the **DJ Basin / Wattenberg Field** and is the most heavily drilled county in the state — tens of thousands of wells. In **April 2017 a home in Firestone, Weld County exploded**, killing two people; the cause was traced to a **severed, abandoned flowline** from a nearby well that had been left uncapped and was leaking gas into the soil. Colorado's flowline registration, integrity-testing and mapping rulemaking followed directly from it. That is exactly why a Weld developer needs flowlines located before design — and exactly why an EMPTY oil & gas layer there is **dangerous rather than merely unhelpful**: on a due-diligence screen a blank reads as all-clear. Which is the whole reason the B1422 ×2 work below refuses to hide the row.
