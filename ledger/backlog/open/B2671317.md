### B2671317 — Colorado sites read "Couldn't check city limits" (no city-limits source routed in Colorado) [site-planner] (bug)
Status: Verify
Verify: live
Repro: Colorado site (350 S 6th Ave, Brighton, Adams County) shows "Couldn't check city limits · Adams County"; no city request ever sent. Texas and Georgia fine.
Root cause: `jurisdiction.js` routed Colorado to `countyCo` but `citySourcesForPoint` had NO Colorado branch (CITY_SOURCES are TX-only), so zero city sources were asked, `cityCentroid` stayed null and containment read "unknown". Not a regression from the source-pack split (#3020) or gisFetch routing (#3036); Colorado never had a city row in jurisdiction.js though the registry row `cityCo` (DOLA) existed for the map layer.
Fix: `JURISDICTION_SOURCES.cityCo` + Colorado branch in `citySourcesForPoint`. Other states (GA, TN, MS, CA, NJ, NY, TX) checked: all route city sources. Guard: test/coloradoCityLimits.test.js (sweeps every county-routed state for a city source; Brighton end-to-end).
Live check owed: Brighton badge shows Brighton or Unincorporated Adams County after deploy.
