`[ ]` **🔲 IN PROGRESS 2026-10-11 — built this session; moves to Done on a passing signed-in live check.**
- **What he sees:** a new box (double-click on blank paper then type, paste on blank paper) starts as wide as the text column, or the room to the right of where he clicked if that is less, never below a usable line (**I chose 240 wide** as the floor, and **720** as the ceiling so a Full-width page does not make a line too long to read). When he clicks out of it (another box, Escape, the page title) the box shrinks to its words, never narrower than a small grabbable minimum (**96**). A box he resized by hand, and every box that existed before today, is never shrunk. Re-entering a shrunk box does not widen it; typing past its edge grows it back to the start width BEFORE the words wrap. A press on the empty right-hand part of a wide box is a press inside that box — by design; Escape first (or click away) and it contracts.
- **Mechanism:** new node attr `fit` on `noteAnchor` (off by default, renders nothing when off → no existing document changes; cleared by any hand resize). `anchorStartPreferred`/`fitWidthFor`/`growWidthFor` in `lib/notesBoxResize.js` (pure, `test/notesBoxSelfSizing.test.js`); the shrink/grow writes are attribute steps with `addToHistory: false` (`autoSizeNoteAnchor`) so Ctrl+Z undoes his typing and never an invisible resize; a self-sizing box renders below the old 160 floor. Print/export read the stored `w`, so paper carries the settled width.
