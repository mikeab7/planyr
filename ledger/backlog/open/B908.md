### B908 — Scheduler indent→outdent round-trip leaves the task's Start/Finish shifted by one business day `[Scheduler]` (bug) #scheduler  *(owner-tested 2026-07-19 during the V327 live follow-up on Goose Creek; minted **B908**)*

But the outdent step also recomputed task 46's **Start/Finish one business day later** than before the round-trip (07/20/26–08/20/26 → 07/21/26–08/21/26), even though nothing about the task's own inputs (duration, predecessors) was touched by the user. - **Code-level investigation this session (no live repro possible):** both `indentTaskById`/`outdentTaskById` (`public/sequence/index.html:6281`/`6312`) end in `recomputeAfterStructureChange = rollupParentDates(cascadeDates(tasks))` — the same recompute used after any structural move. A task with an **FS (finish-to-start) predecessor and no `pinnedStart`** has its `start` re-derived every cascade pass as `addBD(pred.end, 1) [+lag]` (`constrainedStartFrom`, line 1918); when a task is briefly re-parented (indent), `rollupParentDates` (line 2209) can overwrite its **new parent's** own start/end to mirror the (now sole) child, and outdenting then re-derives the child's start from that parent-as-predecessor's mutated end — a plausible but **unconfirmed** mechanism for a same-day drift. Whether task 46 actually has task 45 as an FS predecessor (vs. a plain `pinnedStart` date that shouldn't move at all) can only be confirmed against the real stored task JSON. **Do not guess-fix this** against a mature, heavily-tested cascade engine (`test/schedulerEngine.test.js` et al.) without the real repro data — a blind change here risks a regression across every other predecessor-driven schedule.
