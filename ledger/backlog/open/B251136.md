- **STEP 1 — the path did not merely go unmeasured; it was UNREACHABLE BY CONSTRUCTION, and the reason is a seeding fact.** `SitePlanner.jsx` gates the Tier-2 re-raster on `overlayDocs.has(id) || storageKey.endsWith(".pdf")`. `overlayDocs` is populated only by dropping a file in-session, which no harness does; and `ui-audit/lib/planFixture.mjs`'s seeder emitted an `idbKey` (the RASTER's bytes) and **never a `storageKey`** (the SOURCE PDF's) — even though `rasterSpecOf` deliberately carries `pdfBacked` *because it gates this cost path*, with a comment saying exactly that. The fixture recorded the fact and the seed dropped it. Closed by an opt-in `pdfStorage` flag on `fixtureSite`/`fixtureSeed`, **defaulted OFF** so sixty-plus runs of recorded history are not silently perturbed — and every existing arm therefore under-measures the owner by exactly this path, which is now written down beside the flag.
- **THE THRESHOLD, MEASURED, NOT QUOTED.** `measuredThresholdPpf` bisects the app's own decision function rather than restating the constant beside it: on his overlay the gate opens at **0.9375 px/ft** and the 8192 px cap is reached at **1.1378 px/ft**. That first number is the finding hiding inside the finding — 0.94 px/ft is an ORDINARY working zoom (a 1,000 ft building is under a thousand pixels wide), so this is not a stress-test path, it is where he works.

- **⚠ A CORRECTION TO B227478's SCOPE, recorded here rather than by re-opening it (AUDIT-FIRST).** That item's disposal list reads "**B749's PDF re-raster on zoom** (§5 item 10, which had survived as the leading open lead — the same PDF, the same `pdfBacked` gate, available to both plans equally)." The re-raster's cost has two halves — PDF.js's render of the page content, and the PNG encode of the resulting canvas — and only the first is content-dependent, so **this understates the render half and reproduces the encode half exactly.** His real sheet is a full civil drawing; the numbers above are a floor.
