- **THE REFRAME, and it is the substance of the item rather than a preamble.** B1432 froze CONTENT and varied INTERACTION COUNT, and found nothing across 3,000 gestures. That stands. But a work session is not a constant scene: the owner DRAWS, TURNS LAYERS ON, OPENS PANELS, SWITCHES PLANS and EDITS, and all of those rise monotonically through a session and all reset on reload. So the hypothesis is not accumulation, it is **AMPLIFICATION** — per-frame cost as a function of what the session has filled up with — and "time since reload" is only the proxy. **This RECONCILES B1357's r = 0.93** rather than contradicting it: what changes during his session is how much is drawn, because he is the one drawing it.
- **THE TOP OF THE RANKED TABLE, by (measured cost × how much the axis rises in a session):**
  1. **Panel bodies rebuilt on every render** (`renderPanelBody`, `SitePlanner.jsx:15933`, called once for the docked panel and once per floating panel) — **MEASURED +34.4% of an identical gesture with four panels open (r = 0.914), +683 ms of it SCRIPT, on unchanged content** (`canvasNodes` pinned at 976 at every rung; layout and style roughly flat). Long-task total 0 → 645 ms. Owned by **B1351**.
  2. **The view is baked into every coordinate** (`f2p`, `SitePlanner.jsx:4299`, 141 call sites) — a pan re-emits ~1,200 nodes instead of translating one. Owned by **B1360**.
  3. **A forced synchronous layout in the registration effect** (`SitePlanner.jsx:2992`), whose cost rises with DOM size at flat `LayoutCount`. Owned by **B1359**.
  4. **`resolveElNeighbors` is O(n²) per EDIT** (`SitePlanner.jsx:23054`) — four full scans per element. B1352 was right to build it; the residue is that it is quadratic and now paid per edit.
  5. **`computeBuildingGrid` recomputed per building per FRAME** — fixed in this dispatch, see **B1437**.
