### B2671243 — Live preview when hovering (or arrowing to) a paste option, like Word `[Notes]` (feature) #notes #ui  *(owner ask, 2026-11-12: "In Word, if you hover over Keep Source Formatting or Merge Formatting or Keep Text Only, it shows you how they all look as you hover. I'd like that feature too." Net-new; builds on the paste-options menu of B2494096 / B2671211.)*

`[x]` **BUILT AND VERIFIED THIS SESSION.**

- Verify: **sandbox** for the mechanics (below) + a signed-in check on the deploy.

**WHAT HE GETS.** Hover an option in the paste-options menu, or move the keyboard highlight to it, and the pasted text shows in that option's form in place; move off the menu (or back to the applied option, or Escape, or press outside) and it is exactly as it was. Click / Enter / the letter commits.

**HOW (`NoteEditor.jsx` `previewPasteMode` / `restorePreview`).** The preview is the SAME transform a pick runs, applied as a transaction with `addToHistory:false` and a `pastePreview` meta that `onUpdate` and the version-snapshot timer skip — so no undo step and no save. The pre-preview document + selection are snapshotted; restoring is one exact replace (`revertToBase`). A pick takes the preview off FIRST, so its one undo step reverses from the applied state (Ctrl+Z returns to the paste as it landed, never to a preview). Never sets `uiEvent:"paste"` (that widens a table's box — a real edit). Picture is not previewed (async, stores an image); touch has no hover.

**⛔ THE FIRST BUILD FLICKERED, AND THE HARNESS IS WHY IT DID NOT SHIP.** The menu was anchored to the end of the paste; a preview changes the paste's height, so the menu slid out from under the pointer, `mouseleave` restored the paste, the menu slid back, `mouseenter` previewed again. The menu is now frozen to the APPLIED paste's end while previewing.

**GUARD.** `ui-audit/verify-notes-paste-preview.mjs` (34 checks, a required `build` step; a known-good arm — a hover on Merge must visibly change the live document — VOIDS the run): hover shows Merge / Keep Text Only in place; leaving restores deep-equal with the same caret and scroll; arrows preview; **undoDepth unchanged**; **the stored page is byte-identical after 2.5 s of hovering**; click / Enter / M each commit as ONE undo step and one Ctrl+Z returns to the paste; an outside press restores; Excel's Transpose and Values preview and restore. **Mutation-checked:** dropping `addToHistory:false` takes it to 5 red; dropping the `onUpdate` skip takes it to 1 red.

Tooltip label ("Keep Source Formatting (K)") and the "(Ctrl)" tab are unchanged. Said plainly: after a COMMIT, one Ctrl+Z undoes the pick (back to the paste as it landed) and a second undoes the paste — as the menu has always behaved; the preview adds nothing to either.
