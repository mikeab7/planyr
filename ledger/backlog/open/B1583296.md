**WHY IT'S ARCHITECTURAL.** Every resolver in `counties.js` (`resolveCounty`, `countyPolygonsCore.js`, `countyKeyForName`) answers at COUNTY granularity — one nationwide polygon per county (`public/geo/county-polygons.json`), one parcel-source key per resolved county name. There is no existing notion of "part of a county." Wayne County has no county-wide CAD/assessor GIS after three discovery routes (`docs/STATEWIDE-PARCELS.md`), so the only way to use Detroit's real, authoritative layer without silently misrouting the rest of the county (the exact Nebraska-shaped defect B1332016 already named, and the reason the wrong-scope rejection was correct) is a NEW resolution tier that runs BEFORE the county-level answer and can say "yes, inside this specific city" or defer.

**WHAT WAS BUILT.** `src/workspaces/site-planner/lib/cityScopes.js` — a new, small, pure module: a bundled City of Detroit boundary polygon (two rings — the outer city limit, and a HOLE excluding Hamtramck + Highland Park, two fully independent municipalities inside Detroit's footprint) and `cityScopeAnswer(lat, lng)`, a synchronous point-in-polygon-with-hole test returning the same `{key, name, state, nearEdge}` shape `counties.js`'s own county resolver already uses. `counties.js` calls it FIRST, before the nationwide county geometry, from both `geometryCountyAnswer` (feeds `candidateCountiesForPoint`/`countyForView`) and `countyIdentity` (the panel's "no parcel data wired here yet" message) — a Detroit hit resolves directly; everywhere else falls through unchanged to the ordinary county answer, which (since no `wayne`/`mi_wayne` entry exists) already, correctly, reports no source.

**BOUNDARY GEOMETRY PROVENANCE.** "City of Detroit Boundary," Michigan Geographic Framework, published by the City of Detroit's own ArcGIS Online organization (`OpenDataAdmin_detroitmi`, item `86b221bb68ca4364afe81d156e54f95c`, `contentStatus: public_authoritative`) — queried live from THIS sandbox 2026-09-12 (`services2.arcgis.com` is reachable here), simplified server-side and rounded to ~11 m precision (screening-grade, same class as the nationwide asset's own `NEAR_EDGE_DEG`). Full detail in `cityScopes.js`'s own header.

**A LATENT LEAK THIS SURFACED AND FIXED, not present before this item (found by testing against the real committed nationwide asset, not assumed):** `candidateCountiesForPoint`'s and `countyForView`'s "nothing matched a bbox or confident geometry — fall back to every configured source in this state" branches did not know a city-scoped entry can only ever answer inside its own limits, so once `mi_detroit` existed, a real Wayne County point outside Detroit (Taylor, MI) would have been handed `mi_detroit` as a candidate to QUERY (a wasted, empty-returning request) and — worse — `countyForView`'s nationwide nearest-center search would have named "City of Detroit" as the JURISDICTION for Taylor, a wrong answer more plausible-looking than the pre-existing nearest-center coarseness it replaced. Fixed by requiring every city-scoped `COUNTIES_MAP` entry to carry `cityScoped: true` and excluding that flag from both blind fallbacks (never from a real bbox/geometry match, which is unaffected). Caught and fixed in this same session, before shipping.
