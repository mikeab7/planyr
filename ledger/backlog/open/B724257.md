- **THE PICKER.** Hold Alt and click: resolves to the TOP of the hit stack at that point, exactly like a plain click. Alt+click AGAIN at the same point (within a few px) steps one deeper — the object underneath, then the one under that — wrapping back to the top so it can never dead-end. Click anywhere else and the next Alt+click starts over at the top: this is a way to REACH something, not a mode you can leave engaged by accident. A brief flash names how many objects are stacked here ("2 of 3 here — Alt+click again to go deeper").
- **BUILT ON THE EXISTING RESOLVER, not a second hit-test.** `lib/featureTarget.js` already had `featureStack`/`stackEntries` (the exact primitive `resolveDoubleClickTarget` and the B548065 "Behind this" menu use) returning every feature under a point, top-most first, chrome/handles skipped, deduped. Added `stackAtPoint` (a thin composition of those two) and `nextPickIndex` (the pure cycle rule — same-point-within-tolerance advances and wraps, anything else resets to 0). Wired via `handleStackPick` into the SVG root's EXISTING capture-phase press handler (the same one `notePress`/the double-click anchor already use), because a capture-phase listener is what lets the picker win over whichever feature's own bubble-phase pointerdown would otherwise take the press first.
- **THE BAND-VS-Z FIX: `EL_BANDS.back = -100`, the mirror of the existing `EL_BANDS.front = 100`.** An element's right-click menu now offers **"Force on top of everything"** and **"Force underneath everything"** (collapsing to one "Use the normal layer order" row once either is set, per PANEL-BREVITY — never three rows at once). `zOrder`/`drawElsZ`/`arrangePeers` needed NO changes beyond the one constant: `zOrder` already resolves `bandForce` generically, and `drawElsZ` already splits on a plain `zOrder(el) < BUILDING_Z` threshold sorted ascending, so `-100` falls into the "below" half and sorts first there (bottom of the whole drawing) for free, exactly as `100` already fell into "above" and sorted last.
- **PROVEN LIVE, not just in a pure-function test.** Seeded a pond geometrically containing a road on the real Richfield fixture (124-element dense plan) and drove real pointer events against the real built app: a plain click resolved to whichever real fixture element already painted topmost there; Alt+click same point → same top; Alt+click again → the seeded pond; Alt+click again → the seeded ROAD underneath it; Alt+click again → wrapped back to the top. Separately, forcing the pond's band to `"back"` and clicking a point covered by the pond's body but not its area label: a PLAIN click now resolves directly to the road, no picker needed.
- **ONE NUANCE FOUND AND NOT FIXED, named rather than silently left:** an element's LABEL/dimension chrome renders in a pass that does not individually respect that element's own forced band (verified by planting the road directly under the pond's centered area-label anchor — the label still won the plain click even with the pond forced back; offsetting the road away from the label's anchor made the plain click resolve correctly). This is pre-existing label-layout behavior (labels declutter globally, not per-object z), out of scope for this item, and does not affect the picker (which still reaches the correct feature by identity regardless of which sub-node was hit) or the common case (a body-vs-body occlusion, the reported one).
- **CHROME-NEVER-EATS-A-PRESS respected:** the picker only engages on a plain Alt+click (no Shift/Ctrl/⌘ — those are already claimed by vertex-insert and other gestures) in the select tool, and falls through to the ordinary background press untouched when nothing is under the cursor.
