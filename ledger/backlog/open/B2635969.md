`[ ]` **Open — needs an owner call.** Measured on planyr_production 2026-11-03 (`pg_constraint`): the only foreign keys on those two tables are `added_by`/`invited_by`/`user_id` → `auth.users`; none point at `teams`. Consequences seen: a deleted team's invite previews as open ("invited to the team"), and before `db/team_invite_orphan_guard.sql` an Accept inserted a membership into a team that does not exist.
