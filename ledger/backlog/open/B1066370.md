- **THE INSTRUMENT, shipped this session:** `src/shared/ui/clickDiag.js` (new) — a small, always-on, silent-by-construction diagnostic. Installed from `main.jsx`, dynamic-imported and deferred to `requestIdleCallback` (same shape as the always-on performance recorder beside it) so it costs the critical-path bundle nothing.
