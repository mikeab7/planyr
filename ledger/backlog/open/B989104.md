- **The "unauthorable" contract, mechanically enforced, not just documented:** `Tab`/`MenuTrigger` destructure `style`/`borderRadius`/`height`/`padding`/`fontSize` out of their props and DISCARD them — a caller literally cannot pass a number that changes the shape. Silently dropping a prop is itself a LOUD-FAILURE violation (a caller who thinks an override applied gets nothing), so both primitives call `warnLockedOverride()`, a dev-console `console.error` naming exactly which banned prop was ignored. The **build-time half**, since this JS codebase has no TypeScript to "type it" with: `ui-audit/locked-primitive-audit.mjs`, modeled directly on `design-drift-audit.mjs`'s regex-over-source shape — scans every `<Tab`/`<MenuTrigger` JSX call site in `src/**/*.{js,jsx}` and fails on any `style=`/`borderRadius=`/`height=`/`padding=`/`fontSize=` prop. Zero-tolerance, not a ratchet (two brand-new primitives, no debt to inherit).
- **The scanner needed TWO teeth-check fixes before it was trustworthy — both documented in its own header rather than quietly patched, per this week's own discipline about proving a new check before shipping it.** (1) A naive "up to the next `>`" regex stopped at a NESTED element's own closing `>` inside a prop's `{...}` expression (`leading={<span style={...}>}` — exactly the shape both primitives are meant to hold) and misread the nested element's `style=` as the outer tag's own — caught by the guard's own "real repo, right now" test flagging `AccountControl.jsx` on the first run. (2) Fixed via brace-depth tracking to find the tag's real end — then the SAME test flagged `width="13" height="13"` and `style={{...}}` on a nested `<svg icon>` inside `AppHeader.jsx`'s `Tab` usage, because a flat regex over the (correctly bounded) span still can't tell "this word appears in the tag" from "this is the outer tag's OWN prop". Fixed with a second depth-scoped pass (`topLevelBannedProp`) that only counts a match at brace-depth 0. `test/lockedPrimitiveAudit.test.js` pins both regressions as permanent fixture cases (13 tests) alongside the real-repo assertion (currently 0 violations).
