**THE CHOICE, stated plainly (the brief's own "pick one and say why").** Two options were on the table: shrink the card to its content, or fill the blank space with something worth saying. **Chose shrink-to-content.** A reassurance line (e.g. "N other open projects are current") only answers `GoingQuietCard`'s own specific shape — it has a "hidden total" (every OTHER open project) to reference. Several of the other cards sharing this exact bug (Schedule health, Jump back in, Pipeline, Needs attention, Pursuits, Since you were last here) have no comparable "N others are fine" narrative to add — a schedule card with one real schedule has nothing else to reference; it is just genuinely short today, not hiding anything. A content-driven height fixes every one of them the same way, with zero new copy anywhere on the dashboard (PANEL-BREVITY — nothing added beats something added when nothing needs saying).

**HOW IT WORKS, and why it can't fight the grid.** `DashboardCard.jsx` (the shell every card renders inside) gains a `sizeToContent` option: `height: "auto"` instead of `height: "100%"`, always capped by `maxHeight: "100%"`. A card opted in now takes only the vertical room its own rows need, never more — but it can never exceed the tile react-grid-layout reserved for it, and its content area keeps the same `flex:1 / min-height:0 / overflow:auto` it already had, so a card with MORE rows than fit still scrolls internally exactly as it did before this change. Crucially, **the grid's own per-card row span (`h`, in `dashboardLayout.js`) is never read, written, or touched by this** — react-grid-layout positions every tile with an absolute pixel transform computed purely from the STORED `x/y/w/h`, independent of what a card actually renders inside it, so a card rendering shorter than its tile never moves any other card, never opens a "hole" in the overall grid, and is never echoed back into the saved layout (nothing here can silently overwrite a user's or the default's chosen tile size — the exact risk the brief called out). `Dashboard.jsx` opts in only the list/number cards that have no reason to fill every pixel — `jumpBackIn`, `pipelineStatus`, `needsAttention`, `pursuitsTable`, `scheduleHealth`, `goingQuiet`, `sinceLastHere` — and leaves the fill behavior unchanged for `recentPlans` (its thumbnail grid measures its own box to choose a 2×2/other layout), `compsSummary` (its peer scale bar lays out against the card's available room) and `locationsMap` (a real Leaflet map needs a defined height to render into, not an intrinsic one). The opt-in is also gated on `dataReady`, so every card still shows the identical, stable-height loading skeleton — the shrink only ever applies to real content, never mid-load, so it can't reopen the "a card growing after a sibling already rendered its final content" click-safety hole `CardSkeleton` (B1218496) exists to close.
