- **WHAT THIS ITEM IS NOT.** It is not a licence to ratchet four baselines to make the red go away. Raising a baseline is a product decision about download weight and needs the same justification as any other; doing it to silence accumulated drift is exactly what `test/perfBudgetPolicy.test.js` exists to prevent. The two honest paths are **optimize back inside the band** (B1064 owns the real Site-route gap) or **ratchet with a stated reason per metric** — and someone has to choose, per metric.
- **STOPPING RULE (STANDING RULE #2 clause 5):** closes when a `main` push build goes green on step 16 — either by optimization or by a per-metric ratchet with its reason recorded in `ratchetLog`.
