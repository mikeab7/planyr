- Verify: live (real-data: his own device)
- **THE FINDING.** B2165120's migration is copy → verify → keep the original untouched. That needs the library's size AGAIN in free localStorage, and Chromium's per-origin cap is ~5.2M characters (measured here: 5,200,312 accepted, then `QuotaExceededError`). The owner's device was measured at **3.88 MB used** (docs/perf/PERF-EDIT-CYCLE.md §2); his plans are ~1.30 MB of JSON (145 rows, 77 soft-deleted, the largest 0.94 MB — read from `public.sites` 2026-10-08). 3.88 + up to 1.30 = ~5.18 of 5.20: **the split fits by a hair at best, and would leave him one edit from "storage full".** So the build REFUSES the split unless 250,000 characters stay free afterwards (`plan-store-migration-aborted`, reason `headroom`, with `usedChars`/`needChars`/`capChars`/`shortBy` in the event) and keeps the old layout, unchanged. **On a device like his the per-edit win may therefore not apply until room is made.** Whether it does is read from `client_errors` after his next load, not guessed.
- **THE DECISION (recommendation first).** (1) **Recommended: wait for B2200384** (retire the legacy entry after the waiting period) — it frees the same room, but a device that can't migrate today stays slow until then. (2) **Relocate the safety copy to IndexedDB** (gigabytes, no cap): write a verified copy of the whole library there, THEN split without keeping the original in localStorage. That frees the room now, but it breaks two of the rules he set for B2165120 — "leave the old entry in place, untouched" and the revert-to-old-build guarantee (an old build reads only localStorage) — so it is his call, not mine. (3) Free room elsewhere in the same tier: the version-history ring keeps a ≤ 0.7 MB localStorage copy of what IndexedDB already mirrors (`storage.js` `writeHistoryAll`); verifying the IndexedDB copy and trimming the localStorage one would free 0.4–0.7 MB — but that ring is the B126 data-loss net and its IndexedDB write is documented fire-and-forget, so it needs its own item and its own proof.
- **Stopping rule:** closes when the owner picks (1)/(2)/(3) and the chosen path has shipped, or when his own device is confirmed (by the `plan-store-migrated` event or an explicit "it is fast now") to have migrated without any of them.
