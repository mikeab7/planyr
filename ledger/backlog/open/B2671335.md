### B2671335 — Q (Callout) and other tool letters sometimes do nothing on the first press; two causes `[Site Planner]` (bug) #keyboard #site-planner #typing-guard
Owner (2026-11-24): "when I press Q sometimes for the first time it doesn't [arm the callout] … I have to double tap".
**Cause 1 (reproduced, deterministic):** type a value in a Properties number box and press Enter. `NumInput` commits and KEEPS the caret (NEW-1, deliberate), so FIELD scope treated the next tool letter as text and typed it into a number-only box. Fix: a number box holding nothing uncommitted (`data-num-pristine`) lets tool-arming keys through (`keyScopeVerdict` `pristineNumber`, `armsTool`) and is blurred; typed-but-uncommitted text still owns the keyboard; Delete/Backspace/arrows/paste never pass.
**Cause 2 (measured, 10-25% of fast Enter→Q presses under load):** the planner's `window` keydown listener was removed and re-added from a passive effect on every change of `sel/tool/els/multi…`. React flushes pending passive effects at the start of the next discrete event — mid-dispatch of that keypress — so the old listener was unregistered after the event was queued to it and the new one was added too late: the key was dropped. Listener log showed unreg/reg in the same millisecond as the dropped keydown. Fix: ONE stable listener registered with `[]` deps forwarding to `plannerKeyRef`, kept current by a LAYOUT effect.
Adjacent cases tested: Q right after load, after map click, after Escape, after Tab out of a box, Q twice (stays armed, no toggle), mid-draw of another tool, uncommitted typing keeps the text, tool letters m t l r e c v from a committed box.
Guards: `test/keyContract.test.js` (pristineNumber), `test/keyListenerStable.test.js`, `e2e/q-after-number-box.spec.js` (66/66 over 6 repeats at 4 workers; 12 → fail without the fix).
**Verify: live** (timing/race): after deploy, signed in on a throwaway plan duplicate, edit a Properties number, Enter, Q at once, repeat ~20 times; Callout must arm every time.
