### B2193344 — A mouse click on a "Start your site" option in the first moments after the strip appears is sometimes dropped (desktop, signed in) `[site-planner]` (bug) #site-planner #selection #testing
*(Found 2026-10-08 by the signed-in live re-verify of V1414592 — a measured observation, NOT yet diagnosed.)*

`[ ]` **Observation.** On planyr.io (build c0ca458), signed in as the test account, desktop Chromium: clicking "Trace your boundary" (and the other three options) right after the strip first appears left the strip up and the Select tool active — 4 of 4 harness cases in a row failed at ~0 ms after the strip appeared; a click 1.5–3 s later always armed Draw. A hand-timed trial (press 80 ms, strip settled 300 ms): 2 of 3 armed, 1 dropped; 1.5 s ×2 and 3 s ×2: 4/4 armed. The button node is NOT remounted by the press (identity survives; pointerdown→click all fire on the button) and `.click()` from page JS works, so the event reaches the handler path but the tool does not stay armed — consistent with a late `loadEpoch`/plan-load remount resetting the tool right after (see CLAUDE.md constraint 8's boot-framing history) but UNPROVEN. Phones (touch taps) passed in the same run, so the evidence is desktop-only so far.
`[ ]` **To do:** reproduce with a recorder that logs `tool` + `loadEpoch` + the strip's mount/unmount across a click taken 0–2 s after first paint; fix at the cause (a click that arms a tool must not be undone by boot).
