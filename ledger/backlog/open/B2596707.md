`[ ]` **🔲 OPEN — NOT shipped in the speed-round session, said loudly.** B2596705 (a) took ~33% off but the open is still 2.5–5× the budget on the throttled stand-in (≈ 2.5–5 s on a normal machine).
- **Measured:** `suggestFfe` = ~60% of the open's main-thread time (inclusive, CPU profile); each of its ~20 builds is ~70 ms (layout) / ~140–180 ms (boundary) in Node unthrottled after the geometry cache; what remains per build is the elevation half — the cone search over graded edge pieces (`openGroundZ` / `pieceMin`), `bandZ`'s sample buckets, the pit fill and the earthwork cells — which genuinely changes with the FFE.
- **Ruled out, with the reason:** fewer evaluations (a looser bisection tolerance, a secant step) changes the suggested FFE → changes output, forbidden; caching the earthwork-cell lattice the same way does not hold in layout mode (its extent grows with the slope reach, which depends on the FFE).
- **Next levers, in order:** (1) SPECULATIVE-PARALLEL bisection on a small worker pool — evaluate the midpoint and both next midpoints at once (the binary search's own next two steps, so the answer is identical) and the 5 refine points at once: ~20 sequential builds → ~9 rounds; needs the surface builder + DEM sampler in a worker (structured-clone the DEM once per worker). ⚠ Check whether CDP CPU throttling applies to workers before quoting a sandbox number, or the stand-in will flatter it. (2) a device-side cache of the suggestion keyed by its exact signature (`geoSig#cfgSig#pondShare`) so a re-open of an unchanged plan skips the search (IndexedDB, declared rebuildable in `storageCensus.js` — TIER-BY-REBUILDABILITY).
