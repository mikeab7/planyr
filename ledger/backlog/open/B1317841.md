- **THE REPRO, exactly as reported:** with Chambers' live CAD and the statewide TxGIO layer both blocked, the banner read *"Statewide parcel outlines are slow right now — clicking a lot still adds it."* Both halves were wrong — the outlines actually painting came from the Drive cache, not the (also-blocked) statewide service, and clicking a lot did not add it (that was B1317840's bug).
- **THE FIX, mechanical: `markDown` (the per-county live-outline hang-guard) now checks for a loaded Drive snapshot BEFORE falling back to the generic message, and shows `cacheFallbackNotice(county)` instead** — names the county, its snapshot's as-of date, and (now truthfully, per B1317840) that clicking still selects. Two race conditions specific to this banner, both closed: **(a)** the snapshot can finish loading a moment AFTER the live layer already gave up — the `onSnapshotChange` listener now re-checks the "already known down" signal and corrects the banner retroactively when that happens; **(b)** a DIFFERENT, non-snapshot-backed neighboring county failing afterward (or the statewide layer's own independent `requesterror`) used to unconditionally overwrite whatever banner was showing with its own generic text — both of those handlers now check whether a snapshot-backed county already has an accurate cache banner showing and, if so, stand down rather than clobber it. Measured live during development: without this suppression, the correct banner would flash briefly and then get overwritten by the generic one moments later as a sibling county's own layer also failed — an intermittent, hard-to-notice recurrence of exactly the reported bug.
