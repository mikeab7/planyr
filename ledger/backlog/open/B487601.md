- **THE COST, which is why this is filed at all.** The owner lost about an hour and reported it as data corruption. His Woods Road project held three plans that were near-identical on screen and near-identical in the switcher, and he could not tell which one he was editing. He deleted a plan believing it was redundant, edited roads in one believing it was another, and concluded the app had swapped his work. **It had not** — every write is his own, logged (`key:delete`, one entry per press). The defect is that the product gave him no way to tell three copies apart.
- **`Concept A (copy) (copy)` is not a name anyone can navigate.** `duplicatePlan` appends ` (copy)` to the SOURCE's name, so copying a copy compounds it. `newPlanSameParcel` calls `nextConceptForGroup` and gets a clean "Concept B"; duplicate does not. Renaming produces the same collision from the other direction — two plans a user renames similarly are indistinguishable.
- **Both operations produce it, and the fix has to cover both.** Not just duplicate: a rename can land on a name a sibling already holds, and nothing warns.
- **What is missing, in order:** (a) a duplicate should get a DISTINCT name from the group's own vocabulary, not the source's name plus a suffix; (b) the plan you are editing needs a cue that is visible while you are drawing, not only in an opened menu; (c) the switcher should distinguish siblings by something real — last edited, element count, a thumbnail — since the names alone do not.
