`[ ]` **BLOCKED ON B1156864 — do not start.** The owner types a building name on a deal and the app quietly creates or reuses it — two deals naming the same building on the same site resolve to ONE building record. Split the facts correctly: the BUILDING holds physical attributes (clear height, dock doors, trailer parking, year built, office SF); the DEAL holds economics (rate, term, free rent, TI, escalation, OpEx). Without this, clear height is retyped on every deal for the same building and a 32 vs 36 foot mismatch between two deals on the same real building is never caught.
