### B2671330 — Deed: locked deed ignored double-click; add Convert to overlay (and back) [site-planner] (bug)
Verify: live
Tags: #site-planner #deed #overlays

Owner 2026-11-24: "double click on a part on a legal description or deed doesn't work. And then also add an option to convert it to an overlay, so I can hide it."
Found: a LOCKED deed (kind encumbrance) took the double-click as select-only (B750/B935 locked rule), so its panel (where Unlock lives) never opened. Unlocked deeds, filled or not, with exceptions or a parcel over them, all open fine; PR 3057 (outline-only for unfilled shapes) does not touch deeds. Fix: a locked deed opens its Properties like a locked measurement (`featureDoubleAction`, `startMoveMarkup`).
Feature: "Convert to overlay" in the deed right-click menu and the deed panel renders the tract (boundary + exceptions) to a picture at its exact spot and scale as an ordinary sheet overlay (Overlays list, eye, opacity, lock, crop, reorder) named "<name> (deed)", removes the deed, tombstones its ids. The overlay keeps `fromDeed` (the markups), so "Convert back to deed" (overlay row ⋯ and overlay right-click) rebuilds the editable tract with new ids. Per-course labels are not drawn in the picture (they return on convert back).
Files: lib/deedOverlay.js, SitePlanner.jsx (convertDeedToOverlay / convertOverlayToDeed / addOverlayFile `placed`), DeedInspector.jsx, PlannerContextMenus.jsx, OverlaysPanel.jsx. Tests: test/deedOverlay.test.js, e2e/deed-overlay-convert.spec.js.
