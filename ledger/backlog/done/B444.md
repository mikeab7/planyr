### B444 — Selection-count text overlapped/garbled by the Merge button in the merge banner `[Site Planner]` (bug)  *(owner-reported 2026-06-24 as "NEW-3"; minted **B444**)*
`[x]` **Fixed + shipped (branch `claude/gallant-euler-sk8kwd`). Headless-verified the count label and Merge button bounding boxes do not intersect (`ui-audit/verify-merge-banner.mjs`).**
- **Repro (owner screenshot):** the banner read "4 parce**s** selected" — the red "Merge parcels" button appeared to sit *on top of* the count text.
- **Root cause:** NOT a flex-layout defect — it was a **symptom of B442's z-index bug.** The banner sat *behind* the SVG, so the canvas's own parcel area-labels and strokes painted *over* the banner text, garbling "parcels" → "parces". A flex row never overlaps its own items.
- **Fix:** resolved by B442's `zIndex:6` (the banner now paints on top, crisp). Hardened anyway: wrapped the count in a `<span>` and added `whiteSpace:nowrap` to the banner so the label can never wrap or be crowded by the buttons at any count. Headless check asserts the label's and Merge button's rects are disjoint with 4 selected.
