### B1369 — The note text read as "aligned to the right" on a wide monitor: the column was centred while the toolbar was not `[Notes]` (bug) #notes #ui  *(owner chat 2026-07-31, round three, provisional NEW-3, verbatim: "my stuff is aligned to the right for some reason." Minted with B1365. DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open / ⏳ Verify / Done for `align`, `textAlign`, `centre`, `layout`, `notes`: nothing covers the note sheet's own placement. Net-new.)*
`[x]` **Shipped — and the competing explanation was RULED OUT against the real data before a line was written (AUDIT-FIRST).**
- Verify: sandbox
- Origin: filed 2026-07-31 from chat (round three, NEW-3)
- **⚠ The two candidate causes were genuinely different fixes, so which one it was had to be settled, not guessed.** Candidate B was a stray `TextAlign` stuck on the owner's paragraphs — a DATA problem needing a migration. Checked directly against the stored documents in `notes_pages`: of three pages, **zero** carry a right, centre or justified alignment; every `textAlign` present is the extension's default `left`. Candidate B is refuted. This is layout, and only layout.
- **The cause:** the sheet was `maxWidth: 820` with `margin: 0 auto` inside a pane that starts after the rail. The toolbar spans the pane; the text floated to the middle of it. The gap between the two left edges therefore GREW with every extra inch of monitor — which is exactly what "my stuff is over on the right" describes, and why it did not reproduce at a laptop width.
- **The fix:** the sheet is left-aligned, and its left inset matches the toolbar's own, so the text column's left edge lines up with the first control's and stays there. A document's left edge does not move when you resize the window.
- Guard: `verify-notes.mjs` §21 measures both left edges at two window widths and asserts they line up AND that the text edge does not move as the window widens — the second assertion is the actual report.
