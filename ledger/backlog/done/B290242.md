### B290242 — `statePlane` could not read the app's own `co_`-prefixed county keys, so Denver resolved to the wrong zone `[Site Planner / coordinates]` (bug) #site-planner #coordinates  *(filed + shipped 2026-08-09, found while proving B290241 — the new test failed on the resolver, not on the formula. Minted from this branch's block.)*
`[x]` `statePlane.slugCounty` stripped `city|and|county|of` but not the `co_` ROUTING prefix, which is the form a plan actually persists.
- Verify: sandbox — proven by the same real-browser harness (Denver resolves through its county key).
- **The defect.** `counties.js` prefixes Colorado keys (`co_denver`, `co_weld`, `co_elpaso`) because Texas and Colorado both have an El Paso and a Jefferson, and that prefixed key is what a plan's `county` field carries. `zoneForCounty("CO", "co_denver")` slugged to `codenver`, matched nothing, returned null — and `resolveZone` fell through to the COARSE point envelope, which this module's own comment says cannot separate the interleaved Front Range counties. **Measured: Denver resolved to Colorado NORTH (2231) instead of Central (2232).** `coloradoRegions.js`'s own slug already stripped the prefix, so one county resolved to a regime and to no zone.
- **The fix.** One `.replace(/^co_/, "")`, matching `coloradoRegions.js` exactly. Mutation: drop it → 2 red.
- Worth knowing: this made `resolveZone` silently reachable only by its coarse fallback for every Colorado county, which is why it had never been noticed — the fallback returns a plausible zone.
