`[x]` **SHIPPED — a CHECK defect, not a product overflow.** Reproduced locally on unmodified main (6 sweeps: 4 failed with 9+3 violations, 2 passed). Cause, measured by tracing the real toolbar over time: the Schedule actions bar first renders from `markToolbarReadyFallback` (`ready` true, `settled` unset, `section` undefined) and ~1.5 s later (longer on a loaded CI box) swaps to the embedded scheduler's real report. In the fallback state `inbox`, `zoom` and `format` are `ghost` items (invisible reserved placeholders, absent from the More menu by design) and the plan gives their room up first, so they sit in neither bar nor menu. `auditSnapshot` read that as `item-dropped`. Which phase the sweep landed in depended on a fixed 1.5 s + 0.5 s sleep racing the iframe handshake → 8 failing sizes, then 4, then 0 on one commit. At 800/960 only, because only there does the plan give ghosts up.
- Verify: sandbox — a CI gate; proven by repetition and mutation below.
- **Fix, three parts, no threshold loosened, no width dropped:** (1) `PriorityToolbar` publishes `data-ghost-ids` and `data-toolbar-settled` (new `settled` prop, Schedule passes `toolbar.settled`). (2) `verify-width-sweep.mjs` replaces the fixed sleeps with a real settle: fonts ready AND every toolbar `settled` AND the chrome's layout (toolbar attrs, row and control boxes) identical for 12 consecutive frames, 30 s ceiling → a `never-settled` FAILURE, never a half-measurement. (3) `auditSnapshot` no longer calls a ghost "dropped" (invisible and unavailable, nothing a user has lost) — exemption is per-id, so a real item missing beside a ghost is still caught.
- **Proof:** 24/24 then 20/20 consecutive Schedule 800+960 sweeps clean on the fixed tree (before: 4 of 6 red); the full 189-state sweep 3/3 clean; `test/widthSweep.test.js` +2 cases (ghost not dropped; ghost exemption per-id). **Teeth, run against the real app:** dropping a real toolbar item → `item-dropped` ×6, FAIL; forcing one item 1300 wide → `outside-viewport` ×72 + `clipped` ×72, FAIL.
