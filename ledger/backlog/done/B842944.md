The reassurance sentence (`notesConflictKeptLine()`, `lib/notesStore.js`) now says "The version you don't keep moves to Version history — nothing is deleted." — true to the new mechanism — replacing "…is saved as a copy next to '{name}'." in both `ConflictReview.jsx` and `ConflictSideBySide.jsx`.
