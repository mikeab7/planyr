Minted **B1012560** from this branch's reserved block against `origin/main` c5da2c1. DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open/⏳Verify/Done for "schedule header", "toolbarCenter", "module tab clip", "row 2 split": B387/B917073/B885137 built and reshaped Row 2's 3-zone layout but none of them measured or fixed the equal-split allocation itself. Net-new.)*
`[x]` **SHIPPED — one root cause, two symptoms, one fix for both.** `AppHeader.jsx`'s Row-2 3-zone layout (rendered only when a workspace passes `toolbarCenter` — today, only the Scheduler) gave the LEFT (module tabs) and RIGHT (toolbar cluster) zones identical `flex: 1` (basis 0%), so the row always split its space exactly in half between the two regardless of what either actually needed (tabs ~448px, toolbar cluster ~313px — genuinely unequal). **Symptom 1 (narrow):** at any container width where the equal 50/50 split gave the left zone less than 448px, the tab strip silently overflowed with no visible sign anything was cut. Measured break point: **1108px**. **Symptom 2 (wide) — found only after the fix for symptom 1 was drafted, when the owner reported seeing the header "mess up" on his own large laptop, a WIDE viewport, which the initial narrow-width-only fix would not have touched:** the Grid/Split/Gantt centre group sat a **constant** ~133–135px off-centre at *every* width from 1280 to 2560 — the gap to its right was always bigger than the gap to its left, growing more visually obvious (more dead space) the wider the window, because equal side BOXES don't centre a middle item between two side groups whose real content is unequal width.
- Verify: sandbox
- **Fix: exactly ONE zone grows.** The LEFT (tabs) zone is `flex:"none"` — real content width, never grows or shrinks, so it is structurally the LAST thing to lose space (fixes symptom 1). The RIGHT (toolbar) zone is *also* `flex:"none"` on desktop — content-sized, never grows — which is the part the first draft of this fix got wrong: an earlier version gave the toolbar zone `flex:"1 0 auto"` (to keep it pinned right when wrapped), and while that stopped the narrow-width clip, it let the toolbar's own growth eat a share of the leftover width that belongs to centring the group beside it, producing an off-centre gap that **grows without bound** as the window widens — worse than the original bug at very wide widths. The CENTRE zone is `flex:"1 1 auto"` and is now the **only** zone that grows: it alone absorbs whatever width is left over and centres its own content within that box, which — because neither side zone competes for that leftover width — splits it in *exactly equal halves* on either side of the centre content (fixes symptom 2; proven to 0.0px difference across 1440/1600/1920/2560 in the live harness below). This is a deliberate choice of "centred relative to the two side groups' visible content" over "centred on the row's raw physical midpoint" — the two are NOT the same when the side groups are unequal widths, and only the first one produces equal gaps; said so explicitly rather than assumed. Row 2's pre-existing `flexWrap:"wrap"` (a prior session's stated intent that never actually engaged, because the old equal-share flex-basis of 0% made every zone look "tiny" to the wrap algorithm regardless of real content) now correctly engages below ~977px, sending the toolbar cluster to its own second line — kept flush right there by the row's own `justifyContent:"flex-end"` (a no-op on the single-line/wide case, where the centre zone's growth already consumes 100% of the line's slack) — while the module tabs stay complete on line one down to the 760px phone breakpoint.
