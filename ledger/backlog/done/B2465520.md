**Reproduced first, on the unfixed code, twice:** locally (`ui-audit/verify-schedule-anchored-popovers.mjs`, the pre-fix page 7beda29: after one real wheel scroll of 90 the popup sat exactly 90 away from its cell) AND on production signed in as the test account (build 92842ae, throwaway schedule): Health dot, Status and Split all FAIL "stays glued" with `gapΔ=90.0`.
**Cause:** `getBoundingClientRect()` was read ONCE in the click handler into a `position:fixed` portal and never again. ContactPicker (B1557008) alone had its own per-frame loop — a second copy of the idea that also did not know about the sticky header.
**Fix — one source of truth (`public/sequence/index.html`):** `useAnchoredPopover(open, anchorRef, place, onGone)` + the pure `anchorHidden(rect, area, occluders)` + `anchorSurroundings(el)`. While open it re-reads the anchor's real rect EVERY animation frame (a rAF loop has no event to miss — the B1557008 finding) and re-places the popup; the instant the anchor is fully outside any clipping ancestor, fully under the grid's sticky header (`data-grid-header`, now on the grid header and the Master table's first `th`) or fully behind a pinned column, it calls the caller's close — **a plain close, never a commit** (Owner: `finishEditing`, which keeps only what was already written through). A cell only PARTLY out stays open (spreadsheet behaviour). First placement happens in a layout effect, before paint.
