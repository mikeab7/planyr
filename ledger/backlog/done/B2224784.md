`[x]` **✅ Done — merged in #2241 (f9bc844); live-verified 2026-10-08 signed in as e2e@planyr.test on planyr.io in light AND dark at 315°, V1643888 PASSED (42/42, build 5fc90a1, `ui-audit/verify-building-panel-v2.mjs`).** The Loading section is ONE list under the wall picker: a row per wall (or per linked dock pair), badge with the compass letter(s) + the role word BESIDE it (`dock` / `rear` / `ends` / `sides`), the wall's layers outward as chips (`Court 135′`, `Trailer 50′`, `Trailer ×2`, `Buffer 15′`, `Walk 5′`, `Parking 8 rows`, `Road 24′`), then a dashed `+`. The old "Outside the dock walls" / "Outside the rear & end walls" lists, their Add-layer rows and the Corner bump-outs stepper are gone. Rows in order: dock, rear (single-load only), each end — the rear is NEVER grouped with the ends and END walls are ALWAYS separate rows (no link option for ends, nor for the two long walls of a no-dock building). A cross-dock pair is one `N·S dock` row with a chain toggle (`same` ⇄ `split`, `b.dockStacksLinked`, absent = linked); re-linking makes the second wall match the first (stack, trailer rows, bump-outs and bump-out size) in one undo frame. A chip opens an inline editor under its row (one at a time; chip again or ✕ closes): Depth, Rows for car / trailer parking (typed count = one commit, one undo frame, THIS wall only), Remove (a dock wall removes the layer AND everything outside it; any other wall just that layer). `+` offers the catalog for THAT wall only (dock: court / trailer / buffer / sidewalk / road; other walls: sidewalk / car parking / buffer / road; nothing after a road) and opens the new layer's editor. Every edit takes the row's `sides` (`setWallLayerDepth`, `setTrailerRows`, `removeWallLayer`, `addWallLayer`, `toggleDockLink`, `setEmployeeParkingRows(b, n, sides)` in `SitePlanner.jsx`) — the fan-out across every dock / end wall is gone. Pure rules: `lib/wallRows.js`. Collapsed summary `{Single-load|Cross-dock|No docks} · {n} doors`. Sandbox-verified: `test/wallRows.test.js`, `e2e/building-panel-v2.spec.js` (315°: dock · rear · ends · ends, 8 rows on the rear only with both ends untouched and one undo; cross-dock same → split → buffer on one side only → chain back), the older building-panel / assembly specs moved onto the new rows (`panelPlus` in `e2e/helpers.js`).
