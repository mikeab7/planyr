### B33 — Doc Review redline shapes (rect/cloud/text) are unselectable except at corner points `[Document Review]` (bug) — minor, high
`[x]` 🔧 fixed 2026-06-15 (branch `doc-review/followup-minors`): `hitTest` is now shape-aware — rect/cloud select across their whole body (point-in-box ± tolerance), text selects across its rendered label box, and measures select on nearest vertex OR segment (so a line body selects, not just its endpoints). `hitTest` measures distance only to stored vertices; rect/cloud store two corners and text one point, so clicking an edge or body never selects them (hard to select/delete redlines). Fix: shape-aware hit testing (point-in-rect, distance-to-segment, text bbox).
