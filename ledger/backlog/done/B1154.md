- **Root cause, exactly as the owner's brief traced it.** `hitEditPath` found an edge with `projToSeg(fp, path.pts[i], path.pts[i+1])` — the straight CHORD between consecutive control points — with a tolerance of about the strip half-width, on the stated reasoning that "a click anywhere on the pavement hits the centerline". That reasoning holds only while the centerline IS the chord. The pavement is generated from `roadDenseCenterline` → `roadCenterline`, which builds a CURVED alignment from each vertex's treatment, and on a bend the chord cuts the corner. **Measured on the owner's own plan:** the apex of `e38duuwgj`'s 120 ft return (a ~91° bend on a 40 ft road) stands **35.8 ft** from the nearest chord against a **20.5 ft** tolerance — unreachable at any zoom, because both terms scale together. So no edge hit, no menu item, and the road's own element menu opened instead. Straight runs still worked: precisely the "works on some portions, not others" he described.
- **Fix — hit-test the road the renderer DRAWS.** `roadGeometry.js` gains `roadCenterlineTagged`, which returns the dense polyline PLUS, for each dense segment, the control-point segment that owns it; `projectToPolyline` and `projectToRoadCenterline` sit on top and return `{ index, pt, d }` — `index` being exactly the `edgeIndex` `insertRoadVertex` already expects, and `pt` a point ON the drawn centerline. `roadCenterline` is now a thin wrapper over the tagged builder, so its output is unchanged byte for byte (asserted on four of the owner's real roads).
- **All three paths corrected together, so they cannot disagree on a curve.** `editablePath` hands the road path its own `projectEdge`, which `hitEditPath` uses in place of the chord loop — that one seam serves the right-click menu, the Shift-click LEFT-click insert, and the hover insertion hint (`onCanvasVtxMoveCapture`), so the dot appears exactly where a right-click would actually take. The tolerance is untouched, so a click anywhere on the pavement still lands. `startRoadBranch` (B1067's "branch a road from here") got the same correction: it projected onto the chords too, which is how a branch came to be welded to a point the road does not pass through — the other half of B1155.
- **Guards (`test/roadCurvedJunction.test.js`, 17 cases).** A click on the outside of a 120 ft bend, further from BOTH chords than the half-width, now returns an edge hit at the clicked distance; the returned insertion point lies on the drawn centerline to 1e-6; the hit maps to a segment `insertRoadVertex` accepts and splices there; and a SHARP road matches the old chord projection to nine decimal places on index, point and distance — the "byte-identical on an uncurved road" requirement, asserted rather than assumed. Plus the degenerate cases (2-point road, 1-point road, null input).
- **Sandbox evidence.** `npm test` 6520 green · lint 0 errors · build green · `ui-audit/verify-road-split-curved.mjs` Part B drives a real click + right-click on the owner's Tsakiris plan through the built app: **before — "Add control point": NO; after — YES**, with the insertion hint shown.
