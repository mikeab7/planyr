### B70 — Curbs: derived auto-placed bands with visual / calc / dimension rules `[Site Planner]` (feature)
`[x]` Curbs are derived auto-placed thin bands — not user-placed, draggable, or deletable; they float to stay attached to their surface as rows are added/removed. Default = 6″ mono curb ("mono" = single monolithic pour, no gutter pan), occupying 0.5′ of plan-view width. **Placement:** terminal (the outermost band of every section gets a curb on its far/back edge); sidewalk transition (a curb between a sidewalk and the parking/drive it abuts — sidewalk behind, pavement dropping at the curb face); never a building-side curb. **Three-way representation, one rule for all sections (standard / truck court / trailer alike):** always *drawn*; always in the *calculations* (area, yield, paving SF, the width feeding any math → 18′ stall + 24′ aisle + 0.5′ = 42.5′; a 135′ court = 135.5′); never in the *displayed dimension* (reads 42′, 135′, 185′ — to the face of curb; the 0.5′ lives only in geometry + calc).
> Inspection first (what already existed): **roads already implemented the exact three-way contract** — a road's footprint includes its 6″ curbs (`h = travelW + 2·curb`), the curbs are drawn as lines, area uses `w·h` (curb included), and the on-canvas dimension shows `travelW` (curb excluded). So the *pattern* was proven; the gap was that **parking, paving/truck-court, and trailer carried no curb at all** (no draw, no calc, no rule). Built only that gap, generalizing the road contract to the other paved types.
> Done 2026-06-15 (branch `claude/nice-wozniak-2k2v9c`). A single derived-curb model (`curbEdgesOf(el, allEls)`, a **module-level** helper — no `cfgOf`/component scope, so it's safe inside `renderElPx`; cf. B68) is the one source feeding both the drawn band and the math: terminal curb on the edge pointing **away** from the host building (so never building-side); a sidewalk-transition curb on the inner edge when a sidewalk/landscape strip sits between the pad and its host; both depth ends for a free-standing field. The element's `w/h` stays the **face-of-curb** size, so (a) the displayed dimension already excludes the curb with no change, and (b) the curb is derived on top and *floats* to the terminal edge as rows change — no stored geometry. **Calc:** `curbAreaOf` (Σ edge-length·width) is added to the element's paving/parking/trailer bucket and thus to impervious — so a 200′ bay reads 8 500 sf (eff. 42.5′) and a 300′ court reads 40 650 sf (eff. 135.5′), while the on-canvas label still reads 42/135. **Draw:** a thin band whose width *scales* (so the 12″ option visibly doubles the 6″), kept legible by its stroke when the 0.5′ is sub-pixel at low zoom. Roads untouched (excluded from the new model; they keep their own curbs). Build + tests pass.
> Deferred nuance (kept minor, not blocking): the sidewalk-transition and free-field-both-ends cases are geometric heuristics (same-host + inboard; ±depth ends) rather than an explicit stored curb edge; polygon (irregular) pads are out of scope for the auto-curb. Revisit if a future case needs a hand-placed/overridden curb edge.
