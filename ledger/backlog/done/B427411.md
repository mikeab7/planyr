- **THE SCALE — four steps, and the numbers are NOT invented.** `pill 999 · sm 6 · md 8 · lg 12`. `8` was already the single most common radius in `MapFinder.jsx` (9 of its 23 sites) and `shared/ui/controls.jsx` already declared `{ control: 8, pill: 999, panel: 12 }` — a partial scale that never spread past that file and the Notes workspace. A rounder-looking 10/14 was drafted first and dropped: it would have been a FOURTH opinion, and it broke `test/notesModule.test.js`, which regex-parses those digits and cross-checks seven Notes components that hand-copy them. Adopting what most of the chrome already agreed on makes this a consolidation rather than a restyle, and the `controls.jsx` ↔ Notes contract survives untouched.
- **THE NESTING RULE is a function, not a number at a call site.** `nestedIn(outer, gap)` returns the concentric inner radius. Its special case is the one that was actually broken: inside a PILL, a control running the height of the bar is itself a pill — the search bar is a 42-tall pill and its Go / Select-parcels buttons were boxy chips floating inside a fully round container, which is exactly the "different edge shapes for the Find a site one" the owner described.
- **EVERY PLACE CHANGED.** `MapFinder.jsx` (all 23 sites) · `shared/ui/AppHeader.jsx` · `app/AccountControl.jsx` (the "Michael Butler" chip he named — 99/10/7 → pill/lg/sm) · `components/AuthPanel.jsx` · `shared/ui/CloudSyncBadge.jsx` · `shared/ui/ProjectBreadcrumb.jsx` · `components/JurisdictionBadge.jsx` · `components/LayerPanel.jsx` (8 sites) · `src/index.css` (the four tokens + a Leaflet override).
- **⛔ AND ONE THAT ONLY A COMPUTED-STYLE SWEEP COULD FIND: Leaflet's own stylesheet.** `.leaflet-touch .leaflet-bar a:first-child { border-radius: 2px }` has specificity (0,3,1) and outranked a two-class override; the zoom control kept its own corners no matter what the component said. Fixed by matching specificity, not by `!important`.
- **DELIBERATELY LEFT ALONE, as asked.** (a) The **seven Notes components** that hand-copy `{ control: 8, pill: 999 }` — they already agree with the scale numerically, and `test/notesModule.test.js` guards that agreement; repointing them is a separate, mechanical change with its own risk and no visible benefit.
