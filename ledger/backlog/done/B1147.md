**AUDIT-FIRST — what each pond actually did, and why the owner's diagnosis is half the story.**
The owner's theory ("the label is too wide to be within the pond") names the TRIGGER correctly. It does not
explain the disappearance, and the two symptoms are not two bugs. Measured against the real rings on the
pre-fix build (`verify-pond-label-fit.mjs`, all five zoom steps):

| | northern `e1454719dshobp` | southern `e1454718dshobp` |
|---|---|---|
| drawn size | 374 ft × 1,083 ft, 6.11 ac | 672 ft × 1,520 ft, 17.03 ac |
| pre-fix, every zoom step | **leadered OUTSIDE** | **leadered OUTSIDE**, and **GONE** at the closest step |

So BOTH ponds took the same branch. The old chain tested the wide `footprint … ac · … sf` line against the
pond's BOUNDING BOX; the northern pond's box is 374 ft wide and the line does not fit at working zoom or
below, so it was pushed out. The southern pond's box is wide enough to pass at inspect zoom but not once
zoomed out, so it followed. What made the southern one *disappear* is what happened NEXT: out on the paper
the label joins the general collision pool, and the old chain's last resort there was to say nothing. One
trigger, two endings — and the ending that says nothing is the defect class this repo keeps fixing.

Two further facts the bounding-box test hid. A bounding box **overstates** an irregular pond's room (these
two fill 66% and 73% of theirs), so a label could be told it fitted when the interior could not hold it.
And the 20.20-ac parcel badge's centroid lands INSIDE the southern pond — a fixed obstacle (B951) sitting
on the only anchor the label was ever allowed to use, because the old chain had exactly one position.

**The fix — an ordered ladder, measured against real room.**
- New shared pure module `lib/labelFitLadder.js` (see B1148). `interiorFitter(ring)` rasterises the ring
  and enumerates the maximal inscribed axis-aligned rectangles, answering "does this label fit, and WHERE"
  against room that exists. `labelForms(lines)` turns a priority-ordered line list into the ordered rungs.
- The ladder, first that FITS the measured interior: **(a)** the current single line · **(b)** STACKED —
  acreage and square footage on their own lines, the owner's ask · **(c)** ABBREVIATED — keep the acreage,
  drop the square footage · **(d)** OUTSIDE with a leader back to the pond · declutter/hide remains a
  COLLISION decision only, never the end of a width test.
- **ASPECT-AWARE by construction.** Rungs are TRIED in order but CHOSEN by measured fit, so stacking (which
  is narrower but TALLER) is never preferred on principle. Verified on a long-thin pond, which keeps the
  single line, and a tall-narrow one, which stacks (`test/pondLabelFit.test.js`).
- A label may also SLIDE within the interior to clear an obstacle — which is what lets the southern pond
  keep its anchor region despite the parcel badge parked on its centroid.
- The pond footprint line is now authored as a reflowable `{parts, sep, keep}` spec (`footprintLabelLine`),
  shared by both call sites. Same words, same two facts; it is simply no longer pre-joined into a string
  with no rungs left to take. `Holds … ac-ft usable · …′ rim to floor` and the `+X ac · +Y sf` added-ground
  line got the same treatment.
- `mustLabel` marks a pond: it may never end a frame unnamed. Its outside placement walks around and
  outward until it clears, and commits rather than going silent.
- Five copies of the same ring-bounding-box loop (three of them inline IIFEs) collapsed to one
  `ringSpanFt` / `ringMinSpanFt` — smaller, and one thing to fix. This is the optimization that pays for
  the new module's bytes: `perf-bundle-audit` is back inside every ceiling.

**Result on the real plan (after).** Both ponds labelled at every zoom step. Eight of ten steps place the
label INSIDE the outline; the two that leader out are the northern pond at wide zoom, where the pond draws
narrower on screen than its own name — rung (d), correctly, with a leader. The exported PDF sheet names
both ponds (PDF-PARITY, asserted on the composed sheet SVG, not inferred).

**No regression.** Tsakiris / Concept A and Bain / Concept A pond rings (also pulled from production) are
asserted corner-by-corner to stay inside their outlines, and the live harness confirms Tsakiris on the real
page. Its label now takes the STACKED rung — the wide line never actually fitted its interior, only its
bounding box.
