- **What was wrong:** the outline layer asks one request per 512-px cell and does not page, so a cell holding more lots than the server's `maxRecordCount` is answered with the first N and `exceededTransferLimit`, and nothing is said. Measured over each whole county on the exact Leaflet cell grid (`ui-audit/scan-outline-density.mjs`, `test/fixtures/tnOutlineDensity.json`): **Shelby** has 18 of 1,911 zoom-16 cells over 1,000 (busiest 1,411, midtown/downtown Memphis); **Hamilton** one at 1,066 (East Ridge / Brainerd) and one at 999 — pass 2 had recorded "busiest 884" from a different grid. Every other wired source is under its cap (Hickman 852/1,000, Montgomery 891/1,000, Williamson 899/2,000, Rutherford 1,000/2,000, Chester 553/2,000, Davidson 1,078/2,000 at its z17 floor; the Comptroller layer's 16 city cores max 880/2,000).
- **Fix:** `displayMinZoom: 17` on `tn_shelby` and `tn_hamilton` (both `COUNTIES` and `COUNTIES_MAP` — `displayMinZoomOf` reads the latter), the Davidson precedent. At 17 the busiest cell holds 533 / 523. The price, stated plainly: Select parcels in those two counties starts drawing one zoom level closer, with the existing "zoom in to see the lines" hint.
- **Red-proof:** `test/tnOutlineDensity.test.js` fails for exactly these two on `origin/main`; the Node sweep's B fails on the Memphis town lot there.
- **Not done, and why:** paging (`fetchAllFeatures`) would remove the floor but is a new capability for the worker transport and ghost layer — filed as the alternative, not built; the floor is the established mechanism.
