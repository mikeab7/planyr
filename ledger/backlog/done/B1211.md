**The report.** *"When I click on the parcel and the setback shows up and it says side twenty five feet — I feel like there's too much information almost, or it's just too much."* Four chips reading "Side · 25′" and "Rear · 25′" compete with the buildings, the property line and the dimension text; on a lot with one uniform setback they are also redundant with each other.

**What shipped.** The chip is a **control, not a headline**:
- **Quieter.** Numerals 10.5 → **9.5**, weight 700 → 600, plate 16 → 15 tall with a smaller radius, and the border keeps the setback colour at **half opacity** so it still identifies the line without shouting. The plate stays the neutral white drafting plate it always was.
- **The role word is now conditional** (`chipRoleWords`, pure + tested), two rules in order. **(a) UNIFORM PARCEL** — every chip shows the same value ⇒ no chip shows a role word: the number is identical on every boundary, so the role adds nothing to it, and the panel is where roles are read and set. This is the owner's own case: his four chips now read `25′`. **(b) REPEATED ROLE** — otherwise, within a role whose runs all share one value only the most legible run (longest anchor edge) keeps the word and its siblings drop to the bare value — the "don't say Rear · 25′ three times down one boundary" rule. A role whose runs carry **different** values keeps the word on all of them, because there the role is the only thing distinguishing two numbers.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** the chip is still a click target, still opens the inline editor, still takes an Alt-click segment override, and still carries `data-export="skip"` — the export path is byte-for-byte unaffected.
