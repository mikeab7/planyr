### B1316 — `movePage` / `moveSection` / `moveNotebook` were fully implemented, fully unit-tested, and REACHABLE FROM NOWHERE `[Notes]` (bug) #notes #ui #testing  *(filed 2026-07-31 from an owner review of B1290; minted with B1310. The sharpest finding of that audit.)*
`[x]` All three were exported from `lib/notesModel.js` and exercised across a dozen cases in `test/notesModel.test.js` — and **no component called any of them.** The tests passed and proved nothing a user could do: a page could not be reordered, moved to another section, or moved to another notebook; a section could not be reordered or moved; notebooks could not be reordered.
- Verify: sandbox
- Origin: filed 2026-07-31 from chat (NEW-4)
- Wired to a **⇅ Move** control on every row, opening an inline panel (never a dialog): ↑ Up / ↓ Down for reorder, plus a destination list — sections for a page, notebooks for a section. Reorder and re-parent share one panel because they are one intent, and splitting them would double the controls on a row that already has four.
- **Destinations come from the VISIBLE notebook set**, not the whole tree: moving a page into a notebook this project cannot see would make it vanish.
- **The lesson, recorded because it generalises:** a passing unit test is evidence about a function, never about a product. The fix therefore includes a live check that performs a real move through the UI (`ui-audit/verify-notes.mjs` §15) — reorder AND re-parent — so the unit tests stop being decorative.
