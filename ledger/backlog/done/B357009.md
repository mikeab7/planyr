- **HIS REPORT.** *"If I do a single click, it goes still goes all the way to the left, which is probably part of the error."* He is right and it is not cosmetic: on a mostly-empty page the nearest text position to a press low on the sheet is the end of a paragraph far above, or the end of the document.
- **⛔ THE CHOICE, AND WHY. (b) — the caret goes where you pressed.** (a) doing nothing is defensible and it loses the thing he has asked for five times: click where you want to write, and write there. (b) also makes the double-click requirement moot, which is the real prize: press two lands inside the block press one made, so it is a press on content. **One gesture, one rule, no invisible document state deciding between them** — which is precisely what killed round 2. The separate double-click handler is DELETED.
- **AND IT COSTS NOTHING WHEN IT WAS NOT WHAT YOU MEANT** — which is the only reason it can be this aggressive. The block a stray click makes is provisional (B357008); click elsewhere and it is gone, with no undo frame and nothing written.
- **⛔ "BESIDE A LINE" IS MEASURED, NOT GUESSED.** The nearest position is asked for and then CHECKED: accepted only if the press landed within one line of where that position actually IS, with the tolerance read from the browser as the line's own height. So a press in the white space to the right of a short line still puts the caret at its end — the ordinary case B1368 was for — and a press two inches below the writing is open page.
- **A NODE THAT OWNS ITS OWN GESTURES KEEPS THEM** — the sketch canvas's double-click, a picture, an attachment. Caught by the sketch rows of `verify-notes` rather than assumed.
