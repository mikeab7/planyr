- **AUDIT-FIRST — the state model, and why one defect produced both symptoms.** B750 implemented "a plain click must not open Properties" with a marker, `propsFor`, that had to keep MATCHING the current selection (`propsMatches`) for the panel to render, plus an effect that cleared it whenever the selection moved. So the panel's open/closed state was never owner-owned — it was a function of the selection, and it flapped in both directions: any path that set the marker (B875's pond reveal, B656's parcel effect) opened the panel on a plain click, and any deselect — a click-drag on empty canvas is a deselect — evaporated a panel the owner had opened himself. The owner's read was exactly right; both halves are one bug.
- **THE FIX — separate the two pieces of state, structurally.** `propsFor` / `propsMatches` are **deleted**. The inspector is open exactly when the DOCK holds it (`leftPanel === "properties"`) on desktop, or when the phone companion overlay is up (`narrowProps`) — both already owner-toggled. `companionOpen` is now `narrow ? narrowProps : leftPanel === "properties"`, with **no reference to `sel` at all**. One explicit `openInspector()` (double-click on any type, the pond reveal, the right-click "Properties…" row, the ✎ pill) and one explicit `closeInspector()` (the ✕, Esc, the rail tab) are the ONLY two things that can change it. The dock takeover moved out of a selection-keyed `useLayoutEffect` and into `openInspector` itself. Selection now chooses the panel's BODY only: with nothing selected it renders the pre-existing "Nothing selected" state — widened from the Properties TAB to ANY open inspector, and given its own ✕ — so a deselect swaps contents instead of vanishing.
- **Removed, by name, so they cannot come back:** the parcel auto-open effect (`if (sel?.kind === "parcel") setLeftPanel("parcel")`), the phone effect `if (!companionSel) setNarrowProps(false)`, the marker-clearing effect, and B875's `revealPondSel` plain-click branch (both the locked and unlocked paths).
- **Per-type conformance (the owner asked us to check them all).** POND — plain click selects only; the double-click still routes through `revealPondInspector`, so B875's scroll+flash onto the pond card is preserved, as are the map label (now double-click, single click selects), the right-click menu rows and Enter.
