### B2494336 — A bare map point near Houston resolves to South Central even when it is in a Central-zone county `[shared/coordinates]` (bug) #site-planner #coordinates  *(owner brief 2026-10-29, NEW-2 of the B2478961 block. DEDUPE-FIRST: closes B2478963 by a different, better fix — see that item. Stopping rule: closes when the red-proof points below resolve by county on a real fetch path.)*

`[x]` **Cause (measured on main, not guessed):** `zoneForPoint(lat, lon)` ran the two legacy envelopes first; the Houston box (27.0–31.2 N, −99.8 – −93.5 W) covers Conroe, Liberty and Beaumont-area Texas Central counties, and the Front Range box covers interleaved Colorado counties. Eleven probe points on main: Conroe and Liberty → `tx_sc` (wrong, Central), Yonkers → `ny_long_island` (wrong, NY East), Anaheim → `ca_5` (wrong, CA 6), Memphis → `ar_north` (wrong — Tennessee's single zone). Map/Comps overlay placements and the Site Analysis distances (`placementZone`, `proximityScreen`) all take this path.
`[x]` **Fix:** a bare point resolves by the county it is in FIRST. `statePlaneCore.js` gained a locator seam (`setPointZoneLocator`); `site-planner/lib/pointZoneLocator.js` joins the bundled county polygons to the county table and is installed by `countyPolygons.js` (dynamic import, at the moment the polygons are resident — never static, so the 22 KB county table stays off the Site boot path). Answers carry `via:"county-point"`, not coarse, `nearEdge:true` within ~150 m of a county line. The legacy envelopes remain only as the flagged-coarse LAST fallback (polygons not loaded yet, point in no county, or the state in the call disagrees with the county found). Alaska/Hawaii stay positional. Houston-area results are unchanged (Harris, Fort Bend stay `tx_sc`; the `Object.is` golden is green).
`[x]` **Verified:** red-proof points — Conroe (30.3119, −95.4561) and Liberty (30.0580, −94.7955) → `tx_central`; downtown Houston (29.7604, −95.3698) and Katy (29.7858, −95.8245) → `tx_sc` unchanged. Adjacent counties either side of a zone line, by county key AND bare point, in GA (Fulton/Chatham), AL (Jefferson/Montgomery), MS (Hinds/Lee/DeSoto), TN (Shelby, beside MS/AR), NY (Westchester/Bronx/New York), CA (LA/Orange/SF/Sacramento), FL (Hillsborough/Miami-Dade/Leon) — `test/statePlaneStatute.test.js` §2, with a known-good arm that reports VOID rather than a vacuous pass if the polygons never loaded. The REAL fetch path (not the test seam): `ui-audit/verify-point-zone-county.mjs` drives the app's own `loadCountyPolygons()` in Chromium — before the load Conroe is the coarse `tx_sc*`, after it `tx_central/county-point`, Houston/Katy unchanged. Wider suites (61 files, 11.8k tests touching coordinates/overlays/counties) green; bundle gate: this branch +1.2 KB on the Site route, `statePlaneCore` registered in the allowlist as the same bytes in one more file (the existing "GREW BY ONE" pattern).
- Verify: sandbox
- **Said plainly:** (a) the zone for a bare point can now CHANGE once, when the polygons land, for an overlay that renders in the first moments of a load — a rotation shift of a fraction of a degree only where the county answer differs from the old box (Conroe-type ground); Houston-area overlays do not move. A per-overlay stored zone stamp was considered and not built (it would be another stored copy of a derivable fact). (b) A comp pinned to an overlay has its lat/lon re-derived when the overlay is next moved, in the new zone — screening-grade, never on open.
