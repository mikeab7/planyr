- **The module.** `lib/labelFitLadder.js` — `LADDER_RUNGS` (the ordered vocabulary, with deliberately no
  `hidden` rung), `labelForms` / `inlineLines` (the reflow forms), `interiorFitter` / `pointInRing` (the
  polygon interior measurer). Pure geometry, no React, no DOM. `labelLayout.js` consumes it; a source guard
  asserts that import, so a future edit cannot quietly re-derive fit beside it.
- **The invariant, stated once and tested:** *a label may be relocated or abbreviated, but a FIT failure
  alone can NEVER produce no label at all.* Hiding stays available to the COLLISION engine as a deliberate
  declutter decision — that is B121/B951 and it is correct — but it is not, and may never be, the end of the
  fit ladder. `test/labelFitLadder.test.js` runs 240 placements (6 hostile shapes × 4 label sets × 5 zooms ×
  2 rotations) with NO obstacles, so nothing can be blamed on a collision, and asserts every one is placed
  with its name line intact and a legal rung; rect elements are covered separately.
- **Which label types consume the ladder, and which deliberately do not:**
  - **Every centred element label goes through it** — it is inside `layoutLabels`, the one collision engine
    the whole canvas and the export sheet share. Buildings, paving, roads, parking, sidewalks, trailer
    strips, ponds and the added-detention label all reflow through the same rungs.
  - **Polygon elements additionally get INTERIOR measurement** (`ring`/`ringOrigin`/`ringPpf`) — ponds,
    drawn polygon buildings/paving, added-ground labels. This is where the bounding box lied.
  - **Rect elements deliberately do NOT** pass a ring: for an axis-aligned or rotated rectangle the bounding
    box IS the interior, so measuring a raster would cost work and buy nothing. They keep their existing
    behaviour exactly.
  - **Ponds alone are `mustLabel`** — a named piece of infrastructure carrying acreage and storage duty may
    not go silent. Buildings and paving deliberately stay declutterable: a dense plan needs the collision
    engine to be allowed to thin the field, and every one of them is identifiable by shape and position in
    a way a pond is not.
  - **Not in scope, deliberately:** the parcel-area badges (a fixed layer that is an obstacle, not a
    reflowable label — B951), the red per-edge dimension numbers (lowest tier, pinned to their footprint by
    B592 and hidden rather than moved), contour labels (their own placement rule, B1088), and the
    Doc Review markup layer (a different canvas with its own model).
