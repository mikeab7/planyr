`[x]` **Shipped.**
- Verify: sandbox
- Origin: filed 2026-07-31 from chat (round three, NEW-2)
- **What was wrong:** the document only claimed the box its own text filled. Below the last line and out to either side of it, a press hit the mat and was swallowed — so on a two-line note, most of the page was not clickable at all.
- **What shipped:** the mat is the whole pane and forwards the press. Anything that is not already the document, a field or a control lands the caret at the NEAREST real position — the press is clamped into the text column horizontally and keeps its height, so a click out to the right of a short line lands at the end of THAT line rather than jumping to the end of the note; a press genuinely below the document falls back to the end. `preventDefault` on the mousedown is what stops the press blurring the editor before the focus lands.
- Guard: `verify-notes.mjs` §21 clicks the blank space below the text, asserts the caret is genuinely inside the document, types, and reads the typed text back out of storage — then clicks beside a line and asserts focus again.
