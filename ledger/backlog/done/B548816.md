`[x]` **⛔ THE RETRACTION IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF THIS ITEM. "The measurement is behind the markup" was measured and is FALSE.** It is on top, it was always on top, and any fix that reordered anything would have been wrong. What is true:

        the measurement's default ink   PAL.accent   = #C2410C = rgb(194, 65, 12)
        the markup family's default FILL tools.matrix = #c2410c = rgb(194, 65, 12)

  Identical. A measurement is a 1.5px hairline plus a 10% tint, so painted over a solid slab of its own colour there is nothing left to see. **Camouflaged, not buried.**
- **⛔ WHY EVERY PROBE MISSED IT, THIS SESSION'S OWN INCLUDED.** `diagnose-band-ordering.mjs` seeds markups in deliberately loud colours (#c02020 red, #2020c0 blue) so it can tell them apart — and against those the measurement is perfectly legible. **Only the DEFAULT collides, which is to say only what a real user actually draws.** Same species as B820's four correct-but-wrong-case fixes: the fixture hid the defect. The new harness seeds a markup with NO colour for exactly this reason.
- **THE RULE, in the owner's words, now enforced rather than described:** an element whose job is to display a number must never be able to disappear into another element, and two different element classes must not share a default colour. `src/shared/theme/familyInk.js` is the one table; `test/familyInk.test.js` checks it **pairwise in CIEDE2000, not by string inequality** — #c2410c and #c3420d are also "different colours" and camouflage just as completely. Bar: **ΔE00 ≥ 10**, an order of magnitude over the classical JND, chosen before the candidates were measured.
- **THE NEW INK IS MAGENTA `#A21CAF`, and the obvious alternative was measured and REJECTED.** Doc Review already gives its measure kinds a teal ink (#0e7490) and adopting it here was the tidy answer — but teal sits **9.1 ΔE00 from the detention-pond stroke**, under the bar, and a measurement drawn around a pond is one of the commonest things on these plans. Trading one camouflage for another is not a fix. Magenta clears every element colour and every other annotation ink by ≥25 ΔE00, and is the traditional dimension colour on a civil sheet.
- The `accent` OPTION is **removed** from `measureStyle`, not merely un-passed: a caller handing it the app accent is the defect, and a silently-ignored option would let it walk back in. A measurement carrying its own colour keeps it — this is the fallback only.
