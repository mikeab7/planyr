### B632 — "Not aligned" overlay balloons over the sheet at low zoom `[Doc Review / Stitch]` (bug — rendering)  *(Cowork brief 2026-07-03 "NEW-3"; minted **B632**)*
`[x]` **Fixed + shipped this session.** The overlay lived inside the zoomed world group at a constant ON-SCREEN size (`ls = n/zoom`), so at the ~1–15% zoom needed to see a whole set the warning text + dashed border ballooned over each small sheet and read as "broken." New pure `alignBadgeMetrics(screenMin)` (`lib/stitchGeom.js`) sizes the badge to the sheet's on-screen footprint: **font ≤ ~11% of the sheet's smaller on-screen side** (and never > the old 22px), border likewise; below ~54px the badge **collapses to an outline-only dashed border** (no text), and the label shortens to "⚠ Not aligned" below 150px. The render uses an `lc(px)` helper (`px / (view.zoom * sheetScale)`) to convert the desired on-screen px into the sheet's local units. Visually unchanged at normal zoom; a small legible fraction at low zoom. Unit-tested at every tier + non-finite/negative inputs.
