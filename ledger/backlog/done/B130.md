### B130 — Parking tool: "Split into rows" tiles single-loaded rows — generate double-loaded modules + correct curb logic + rename `[Site Planner]` (bug)  *(arrived as "NEW-1"; minted B130 — next after B129)*
`[x]` **Repro:** draw a car-parking area, click **"Split into rows."** **Current (wrong):** the field is tiled as `[stall row + drive aisle]` repeated down its whole depth — single-loaded throughout, a full aisle against every row (the most pavement-hungry layout; ~40% more depth per row than needed, so fewer rows fit and stall yield comes in low). **Expected:** tile a **double-loaded module** — two stall rows sharing one aisle.
- **Generation logic.** Repeating module = double-loaded: stall row + aisle + stall row. At 90°: 18′ + 24′ + 18′ = **60′ module**; stall width 9′ default. **Single-load only where geometry forces it:** the top row against a building face/sidewalk, a row against a property line, or a final strip too shallow for a full 60′ bay. **Remainder:** if the last band can't fit a full 60′ module, drop a single-loaded row or hand the strip back as leftover/landscape — **never insert another full aisle to fill it.**
- **Orientation:** rows parallel to the building face by default; fall back to the polygon's **longest edge**.
- **Parameters to expose:** stall width (8.5–9.5′, default 9′), stall depth (default 18′), aisle width (default 24′ at 90°), parking angle (90/60/45°), single- vs. double-loaded.
- **Curb logic (6″ curb).** Apply one 6″ curb around the **perimeter** of the paving, wherever pavement meets non-paving (dirt, landscape, building face, property line). **Never at internal seams** — aisle-to-stall-row inside a module, or stall-row-backing-onto-stall-row between stacked modules (that's continuous paving). **No curb across drive-aisle openings** where vehicles pass (entry throat, aisle-to-aisle, aisle-to-street) — there pavement meets pavement, so the meets-non-paving rule already skips it. A **dead-end aisle terminating against landscape DOES get a curb** (terminus, not blockage). Test: vehicles cross the edge → gap, no curb; vehicles stop at the edge → curb.
- **Rename:** change the button **"Split into rows" → "Split rows/aisles"** (it splits the field into stall rows + drive aisles). *(Owner 2026-06-17: explicitly NOT "Generate parking" / "Lay out stalls" — those don't fit; "Split rows/aisles" is the label.)*
- **Out of scope (tracked separately, large lift):** clipping the field to the parcel boundary.
