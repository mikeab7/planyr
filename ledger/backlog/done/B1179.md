- **THE THREE, with what is actually asserted:**
  - **`module-keepalive` → "global keys act on the visible module only (hidden headers ignore 'f')".** After `f` the spec presses **Escape** and expects the chrome back. Measured directly: **headless Chromium does not exit the Fullscreen API on Escape** — that is browser-chrome behaviour and headless has none (probe: `after f {fs:true}` → `after Escape {fs:true}`). The app is right to leave Esc to the browser in native mode (that is B1156's explicit design, and fighting it would double-toggle); the SPEC is the stale half — it was written when `f` was a chrome-hide, where Esc did the work. Likely fix: exit via `document.exitFullscreen()` (what the ui-audit harness already does) and keep a separate Esc case for the refused/fallback path.
  - **`inspector-close` → "✕ closes the inspector even when the Properties rail TAB holds the dock".** Fails EARLIER than its own subject: the double-tap that is supposed to OPEN the inspector never opens it, so the ✕ is never reached. Reproduced standalone — double-tapping a building opens Properties normally, but after clicking the Properties rail tab first it does not, at the coordinates the spec computed before the panel docked. **Not yet established whether the drawing simply moved under the stale coordinate or whether the docked rail genuinely swallows the gesture** — that distinction is the whole item, because the second reading is an owner-visible product bug in the same family as B1174 and would deserve its own fix and its own `V###`.
  - **`click-behavior` → "double-click a centerline road always opens Properties, never the inline editor".** Same failing shape (the panel never becomes visible after the double-tap on the road). Same open question.
- **WHY IT IS FILED RATHER THAN FIXED HERE, stated plainly rather than quietly.** Two of the three need a real investigation of the double-tap-versus-docked-panel interaction to tell a stale spec from a product bug, and answering that properly is comparable in size to the four items this dispatch shipped.
