- **Fix:** in boundary mode the slope is thrown INWARD from the limit: every boundary sample `b` of the sweep's own `ctx.bs` (the SAME samples `maskAt` paints its blocking discs from) bounds the surface at `p` to `[z(b) − d/ratio, z(b) + d/ratio]`; the pad elevation clamped into every band is the slope (`bandZ`), a point no sample constrains is the plateau — the sweep's disc rule by construction, so plateau = mask still holds (the existing "within one cell" test). A cell outside `limit.regions` is never graded (`continue` before the open-ground and slope branches, and in `probe`); `noteOut` is a no-op in boundary mode, so the setback / property-line flag is impossible there (the pond and a stream buffer are holes in the limit, not flags); the traced plateau is `clipRegions`-clipped to the limit and `plateauAt` reads the sweep cell on the other side of a traced edge when a lattice point lands exactly on one (one cell of the harness fixture fell through to the inverse-distance infill otherwise); the daylight line IS the limit's rings (outer + holes), so the band starts exactly at the setback line and the Cut & Fill ring is smooth.
