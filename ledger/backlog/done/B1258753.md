- Verify: sandbox — `test/roadCrossSection.test.js` (`imperviousCorrectionWidths`, the BAND_TYPES `impervious` table) and `test/siteMetrics.test.js` (the median-subtracted / sidewalk-added cases against a real `siteMetrics()` call) both green; full suite green; build green.
- **THE FIX.** `BAND_TYPES` (`lib/roadCrossSection.js`) gains a third, independent boolean `impervious` — NOT a synonym for `paved` (curb & gutter and sidewalk are impervious concrete, not asphalt) and NOT a synonym for `withinCurb` (a median sits inside the curb line but isn't impervious): `travel`/`turnLane`/`shoulder`/`parking`/`bike`/`curbGutter`/`sidewalk` → `true`; `median`/`parkway`/`ditch` → `false`. `imperviousCorrectionWidths(xsection)` sums two corrections: `perviousWithinCurb` (a within-curb band that ISN'T impervious — the median) and `imperviousOutsideCurb` (an outside-curb band that IS — a sidewalk). `lib/siteGeometry.js`'s new `roadImperviousArea(el, settings, sharpAt, trim, extraSf)` takes `roadStripArea`'s own figure and applies `− perviousWithinCurb × centerlineLength + imperviousOutsideCurb × centerlineLength`, deriving the correction from the SAME ring/centerline `roadStripArea` already builds — so every curve and junction fillet the ring captures carries straight into the correction. `roadStripRing`, `el.travelW`, every junction dissolve, and the dialog's own "Pavement area" figure (still `pavedWidth`, an asphalt-COST number, deliberately untouched — this item is about impervious/detention, never paving cost) are all byte-identical. `lib/siteMetrics.js`'s road branch now calls `roadImperviousArea` instead of `roadStripArea` for the `paving` (→ `impervious`) rollup only.
- **⛔ THE MOVEMENT ON A REAL DIVIDED-ROAD PLAN, stated plainly per the dispatch's own instruction.** On the boulevard-with-sidewalks repro, the corrected impervious width is 72' (curb-to-curb) − 20' (median) + 10' (both sidewalks) = 62' effective, against the previous 72' — a **10-foot-of-road-width DECREASE** in impervious area for every foot the road runs (a road with a grass median but no sidewalks would see a larger decrease; one with sidewalks but no median would see an increase). Any EXISTING plan with a designed cross-section carrying a median and/or an outside-curb sidewalk will see its impervious/detention numbers shift by exactly this correction the next time it's opened — a more ACCURATE number, not a regression, but real movement worth knowing about on a live plan.
