  **NOT BUILT, with the reason (each is a decision, not a gap in effort):**
  · **Stream / wetland buffers — no buffer layer, deliberately.** No statewide Mississippi stream or wetland buffer width was found in the sources checked (web research of MDEQ and state rules came back empty; this is a finding, not proof none exists — confirm with MDEQ and the county); local ordinances set buffers where they exist. A buffer width is never drawn without a rule behind it (house rule), so none is. Streams (USGS NHD) and wetlands (NWI) are already the national rows; MDEQ's 303(d) impaired streams are built.
  · **Karst / sinkholes** — no state or federal karst layer found for Mississippi.
  · **Airport noise / airport zoning (Memphis International, Olive Branch Airport)** — the FAA-accepted Part 150 noise maps are PDFs; no GIS service from the Airport Authority, DeSoto County or the cities was found. Ask the Memphis–Shelby County Airport Authority for the contour files.
  · **Oil & Gas Board's own "Data Explorer"; MDAH's Historic Resources Inventory** — neither is an open map service; the Board's data is used through MARIS (the state clearinghouse), historic places through the National Register.
  · **Sixteenth-section school lands** — MARIS's `MS_16thSectLands_2021` is a mislabeled PAD-US shell holding one feature; no open source from the Secretary of State found.
  · **MARIS services that are empty shells** (no layers; 404 on layer 0): rail networks / crossings, FAA runways, tribal lands, cemeteries, wildlife management areas, USACE levees, dam safety, source water, landslide regions, hurricane routes — the federal / MDEQ originals were used instead where they exist (rail, airports, transmission lines, EPA Superfund & RCRA, truck routes and pipelines are already national rows that serve Mississippi).
  · **Dam "rule of thumb" inundation (5,269 polygons)** — one 0.1° box is ~22 MB and larger cells time out through the relay (20 s) — too heavy to draw; the 322 modeled areas are built.
