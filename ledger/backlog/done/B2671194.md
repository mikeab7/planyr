### B2671194 — Drive-in ramps stay behind, off the end of the building, when it is shortened `[Site Planner / drive-in ramps]` (bug) #site-planner #geometry #sync  *(owner chat block 2026-11-06, NEW-1; screenshot of Academy › Concept A, Building 4.)*
**Report.** Two drive-in ramps sat in the gap past the end of Building 4, not on any wall; the owner asked where they came from. **Measured read-only on his real rows (plan not touched):** Building 4 = h 784 / w 400, rot 89, cross dock along y; ramps attached to it at `ramp.at` 68.16 (right+left), 850.84 (right+left, written in the same 09:50 CT op as the shortening edit) and 718 (right+left, re-added 10:05). 850.84 is ~66 ft past the 784 wall; 718 = 784 − 66 is the right far-end spot. Every other building on the plan had all ramps inside their wall.
**Cause (code).** `ramp.at` is an absolute distance from the wall's start. `refitRamps` re-derived only the ramp's BOX from `at`, so a host resized at either end carried its start-end ramps correctly by accident and left its far-end ramps at the old absolute `at`.
**Fix.** `lib/driveInRamp.js` `reseatRamps(base, prev, next, ctx)` at the planner's one write seam (`SitePlanner.jsx` `setEls`, before `refitRamps`): a ramp in the nearer half of the wall it was placed against keeps its distance from that end (start ramps stay, end ramps move with the end), clamped so the footprint is on the wall; a re-seated ramp that lands on another ramp of its wall is dropped (door modules come back — the belt is derived from the ramps that exist; the seam tombstones the id); a position the write itself chose (Properties, the "+") is never followed; a drag measures from `followBase` so shrinking then growing back restores the ramp; undo/redo/loads (`setElsRaw`) are never re-interpreted. The same function is the **on-open heal** (an effect, never mid-gesture): a ramp whose door centre is past a wall end goes to the door-module spot at that end through `pickRampSpot` — the one answer the "+" button also reads (`planRampAt`, over `elementRender.rampDoorRunOf`), so on his plan the 850.84 pair lands on the existing 718 pair and is dropped.
**Guards.** repo-root `test/` **rampFollowsWall** (Building 4's real numbers; shorten near/far end, lengthen, 0°/89°/315°, single + cross dock, mid-wall clamp, overlap drop, gesture restore, undo/redo/reload, seam wiring; red on the pre-fix seam — `refitRamps` alone leaves `at` 832 on a 784 wall); e2e **drive-in-ramp** (real planner: shorten/lengthen by typing Length, undo ×2, reload; red on the pre-fix build — `at` 228.25 on a 150 wall); live ui-audit **verify-ramp-follows-wall-live** (signed in, throwaway plan seeded with Building 4's shape + one drawn building, deleted and verified gone).
**Not claimed:** the exact spot the heal picks for a real plan depends on that building's own door grid; the owner's own "+" press at 10:05 gave 718 through the same function, so it lands there by construction — not re-measured on his plan (not touched).
