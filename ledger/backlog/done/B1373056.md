`[x]` **FIXED THIS SESSION — both halves, and the second one turned out to be the "wouldn't let me save" complaint in NEW-2.**

- **Half 1 — a paste no longer appends past an unfilled row.** `commitText` did `commitRows([...rows, ...newRows])` unconditionally and then parked the cursor on the first NEW row, so the row a map pick had just created (an anchor and nothing else) survived ABOVE the pasted deal as a phantom. The first parsed comp now ABSORBS the first unfilled row, keeping that row's own identity and its anchor — which is both halves of what the owner asked for at once: the paste lands where he was looking, and the skipped row does not survive for **B1373057**'s parcel pick to answer instead. Pure and tested: `absorbPasteIntoSheet` / `isUnfilledRow` in `lib/compSheetColumns.js`. **"Unfilled" is defined against `emptyDraft(anchor)` rather than by listing fields** — a row is unfilled iff its draft is EXACTLY what the app itself would have produced for that anchor, every default included (the parcel-derived `landSizeValue` among them). That makes it impossible to absorb over a typed value, and self-maintaining: a new draft field is covered the day it is added to `emptyDraft`. Undo is honest about it too — it removes the rows the paste APPENDED and RESTORES the row it absorbed into, rather than deleting the location the owner picked by hand.
- **Half 2 — the sheet reports ONE number.** The paste summary was a formatted STRING baked at paste time (`sheetTotal = rows.length + newRows.length`) and then left on screen while the sheet moved on; the footer read `rows.length` live. Any delete, undo or absorb made them disagree — exactly his screenshot. The summary is DATA now (`{kind, count, splitReason}` — what happened, never how many rows exist) and the total is rendered from `rows.length` by the same expression the footer uses, so the two cannot disagree by construction. **Not a label change: the second number was deleted, not reworded.**
- **⛔ A THIRD DEFECT, found in the same frame and not in the report — the footer's count and its label described different things.** `missingLocationCount` counted rows failing ANY `validateComp` check and reported every one of them as "missing a Location". `validateComp` returns two kinds of error (no Type, no anchor), so **a row that HAD a location and was only missing its Type was announced as missing a Location.** Reproduced live before the fix: a pin-dropped row reading "Waller County, TX" in its own Location cell, counted in the footer as "1 missing a Location". That is what the owner's NEW-2 sentence ("it even wouldnt let me save bc of the location even though I had already selected a parcel") was reading on screen. The two are counted and named separately now.
