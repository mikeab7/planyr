**The report.** *"The text is bright green, and I thought we talked about the text just being black, and the outline of the thing. So I don't know what happened here — because when I clicked reset setback line then the text went black."* His parcel's setback line is bright green; the chip renders on a white plate, so bright-green numerals on white are barely legible, and **Reset setback line** put the colour back to the near-black default, which is exactly why resetting "fixed" it.

**Cause, confirmed in the code rather than inferred.** The chip was `<rect … stroke={sbCol}>` + `<text … fill={sbCol}>` with `sbCol = pc.sbStroke || PAL.chipInk`. One value drove both the border and the text, so the label's contrast was a function of a decoration the user is free to set to anything.

**The fix.** The chip draws from `setbackChipStyle(PAL.chipInk)` — plate white, border and text `canvasChipInk` (`#15171C`, the same token in both themes). Nothing on the chip reads `pc.sbStroke` any more; the setback LINE still carries the user's colour (B1100), its chip never follows. Because the plate is white in both themes and the ink is one token, the contrast is a fixed **17.9:1** everywhere, which the tests assert rather than assume. **"Darken the user's colour" was considered and rejected** — it still fails on a pale yellow or a near-white, and it leaves legibility depending on a decoration.

**Deviation from the brief, stated rather than buried.** The brief said the colour "belongs on the setback LINE and on the chip's border only". While this was in flight, `setbackChipStyle` landed on `main` and took the border off the line colour **as well** — same reason, one step further: a saturated border on a chip this small reproduces exactly the green-on-white unreadability the ink default was minted to end. That is **kept** rather than reverted; NEW-3's quieting then rides on top as an opacity, which is what makes the border read as the neutral hairline the brief's own item 3 asked for. The bug the owner reported — unreadable numerals that went black when he reset the line — is fixed either way.

> **Tests / verification.** `test/setbackChipQuiet.test.js` — the WCAG contrast of the ink on the plate in light AND dark, that the two themes share the token, and that four real colours a user can pick from the setback colour field (bright green, pale yellow, near-white, cyan) each fail on that plate — the reason the ink token, not a derived colour, is the fix; plus source guards that neither `fill={sbCol}` nor its `const sbCol = selParcel.sbStroke` source can return. `test/setbackChipInk.test.js` (main's guard, retargeted where NEW-3 moved the factory's end) still pins the whole render site to `setbackChipStyle`. Headless: `ui-audit/verify-setback-chip-quiet.mjs` seeds the owner's parcel WITH his bright green and asserts, off the live DOM in both themes, that neither the text nor the border follows it while the setback RING still does.
