### B1557012 — Creating a contact from the Owner field took two confirmations `[Scheduler]` (bug) #scheduler #ui  *(owner chat block, 2026-09-11, NEW-5, verbatim: "it almost took a double click to confirm when I'm typing in someone's name. Like, it says add contact, and then I have to click add contact or something similar again." Established flow BEFORE any fix, per the brief's own instruction to report it rather than assume: on this build, a MOUSE click on the dropdown's "+ Add … as new contact" row already created in ONE action; typing a full new name and pressing Enter — the far more common flow — took TWO: Enter raised a separate "No match — add … as a new contact?" popup requiring a second Enter or button click. Ships as part of B1557008's PR. Minted **B1557012** from the same reserved block. DEDUPE-FIRST — searched for "pendingNew", "Add contact?", "second confirmation": the two-step design itself was **B443536-era NEW-1** (Done, still correctly cited in this item's own writeup as the protection being kept) — this item changes ITS SHAPE, it is not a duplicate of shipping it.)*

`[x]` **FIXED THIS SESSION** — `ui-audit/verify-contact-confirm.mjs` check 1 asserts the old confirm popup (`[data-contact-confirm]`) never appears at all any more; `test/contactCreateConfirm.test.js` asserts the two-step design's own markers (`pendingNew`, `data-contact-confirm`) are entirely gone from the source, not merely bypassed on one path.

**WHAT WAS KEPT, verbatim from the brief:** "creating a contact should stay DELIBERATE — a stray Enter on a typo must not mint a contact. One click on an explicit 'Add X as new contact' row is deliberate enough. A second confirmation on top of that is not caution, it is friction." Both routes into creation (Enter on genuinely-new typed text, and clicking the dropdown's own "+ Add" row) now call the exact same guarded function (`attemptCreate`) and both create in one action when neither NEW-2 guard fires. The original protection is kept in full on the passive routes: clicking away or Tab-ing off an unconfirmed new name still discards it silently rather than creating anything (verified live: "clicking away with an unconfirmed new name commits NOTHING").
