- **The bar is the real work, never a timer.** `runChunked` gained an optional `onProgress`; the surface lattice loops (`proposedSurface.js`, round-2 and the grading-limit engine) and the contour lattice now `yield` their own column fraction instead of a bare `yield` (every other consumer ignores a yielded number; `drainSteps` drains byte-identically — asserted). Stages are weighted over the ones the rebuild EXPECTS (surface 3 · contours 2 · band 1 · floor search 2), a finished stage counts 1, the bar never goes backwards inside a rebuild, and a step that cannot report (the terrain-worker traces, the floor search) is **indeterminate** — a short bar slides across the track (a CSS keyframe on `transform`: compositor-only, no JS).
- **Finding stated plainly (the brief assumed otherwise): the SURFACE build is NOT in the terrain worker** — it is a generator driven by `runChunked` on the main thread in ≤ 10 ms slices (`useGradeSurface.js`); only the contour / band traces and the buildable sweep run in the worker, and those can post no chunk counts, so they are the indeterminate steps. The determinate part of the bar is therefore the surface lattice + the contour lattice, which is where the time goes.
- **Cost.** No rAF loop and no interval of its own (static guard + a live A/B: 61 rAF calls with the banner mounted vs 64 without, the same rebuild); progress reaches the one tiny leaf component at most every 100 ms (≈ 10 / s, one trailing flush so the last value always lands).
