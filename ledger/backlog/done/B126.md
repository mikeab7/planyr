### B126 — Data-loss ROOT FIX: sync kept the whole last-saved copy (a thinner one erased a fuller one) + automatic local backups `[Persistence]` (bug) — DATA-LOSS, critical
`[x]` Follow-up to **B124/B125**. The shipped B124 fix stopped whole *sites* from vanishing on pull, but buildings could still disappear *inside* a surviving site: both `saveSite`/the cloud and `mergePulledSites` reconciled two copies by **whole-record newer-`updatedAt`-wins** with **no content merge**, so any copy saved last with fewer buildings (a stale tab, a second device, a partial/mid-load save) silently overwrote the fuller one — and `pullCloud`'s re-push then propagated the thin copy to the cloud. Owner hit this: ~12 projects emptied while the last-edited one (SCHIEL, intact at ~10 buildings / 24 building-elements once bump-outs are counted) survived. Free-tier Supabase → no PITR, so the emptied sites weren't server-recoverable; owner opted to **fix forward** (quick to recreate) and harden against recurrence.
