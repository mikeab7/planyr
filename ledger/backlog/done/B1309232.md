- Verify: sandbox — a pure, data-independent hit-test rule, no auth/GIS/real-data wall; measured live in a real headless browser both before (pristine `main`, reproduced the exact reported symptom) and after the fix, per ATTEMPT-BEFORE-YOU-PARK.
- **⛔ READ BEFORE TOUCHING `markupPick.js`'s fill rule AGAIN.** B920 (2026-07-21) made an UNFILLED closed markup grab on its stroke only, never its interior — fixing a real, reported, LOCKED ~5,000-ft invisible boundary that blanketed every click on the roads underneath it on the owner's live Sylvestri plan. That fix was correct for its own case and is UNCHANGED for that case. But it also silently broke the ordinary, ubiquitous case: draw ANY unfilled polygon/rect/ellipse/cloud (the default — the ticket is explicit that adding a default visible fill is not an acceptable fix) and its interior stopped being clickable at all, which is standard behaviour in every drawing tool and is what the owner hit.
- **THE RULE, amended, not reverted.** A closed markup now grabs by its whole interior when it is FILLED, **or simply UNLOCKED**. Only a LOCKED + unfilled shape keeps the original stroke-only grab — locking is the explicit signal a shape is a passive backdrop reference, which is exactly what the real B920 case was (and still is: `!m.locked` is false for it, so it is untouched). A freshly drawn, unlocked shape is not that case.
- **Fix, both halves kept in sync by construction (the SAME rule, read from the SAME field):**
  - `SitePlanner.jsx`'s `renderMarkupNode` — `closedHitPE = (closedFill || !m.locked) ? "all" : "stroke"` (was `closedFill ? "all" : "stroke"`), gating the fat transparent hit companion every closed markup kind (polygon/rect/ellipse/cloud) shares.
  - `lib/markupPick.js` — `hasVisibleFill` renamed `grabsWholeBody`, now `(m.fillOpacity||0)>0 || (hatch visible) || !m.locked`, feeding `markupHitModel`'s `filled` flag for the JS picker (the B921 repeat/Alt-click cycle), so the declarative hit area and the imperative picker can never diverge.
- **MEASURED, real headless browser, both builds, same repro (draw a Polygon with Shift+P, Escape, click dead-centre in the interior):** BEFORE (pristine `main`) — selection after the interior click is `null` (the reported bug, reproduced). AFTER (this fix) — selection is the drawn polygon; same result for Rectangle, Ellipse, and Cloud, each drawn with its own tool (R / E / the 4-click Cloud gesture) and clicked dead-centre after a deselect.
