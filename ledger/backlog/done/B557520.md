All eight were painted, correctly positioned, correctly labelled, correctly cursored — and completely dead. **Every unit test passed and every DOM reading said the control was present and correct.** What said otherwise was eight harness rows reading `400×200 → 400×200`.
- **⛔ AND THE SECOND ONE, WHICH IS THE SAME SPECIES: `overflow: hidden` ON THE BOX CLIPS ITS OWN HANDLES.** They straddle the border box by design — that is what makes them grabbable — so clipping the anchor leaves them LAID OUT (a perfectly sensible 10px square, in a perfectly sensible place, which `getBoundingClientRect` will confirm) but not hit-testable: `elementFromPoint` at a handle's own centre answers the EDITOR. Clip the picture, never the frame. **Both are CHROME-NEVER-EATS-A-PRESS inverted** — not chrome swallowing a press, but the box swallowing its own chrome — and neither is visible to anything short of a real mouse.

ProseMirror runs `posAtCoords` FIRST and returns before `handleDrop` when it is null, so a drop past the sheet is not a case the app can have. Written into the harness so nobody re-derives it.
