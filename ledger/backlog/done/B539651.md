### B539651 — A real right-click menu, and no delete button on selection `[Notes]` (bug/feature) #notes #ui  *(owner chat block 2026-08-14, NEW-3. Minted **B539651**. DEDUPE-FIRST: **B434416/B434418** built the two-stage selection and moved the controls OFF hover onto selection — this changes what selection reveals and what right-click offers; **B1367** is the RAIL's row menu, a different surface. Net-new.)*
`[ ]` **⛔ SHIPPED — driven with real right-clicks, judged on the STORED document (26/26).**
- Verify: sandbox — `ui-audit/verify-notes-context-menu.mjs`.
- **WHAT SHIPPED.** The delete × is **gone from the box entirely** — selecting a box shows the ring and the resize handle and nothing destructive. Delete/Backspace still removes it. **Right-clicking a box** offers `Delete this box`, last and separated because it is the destructive one. **Right-clicking the document** gives Word's menu: cut · copy · the three paste modes · bold · italic · underline · strikethrough · bullets · numbering · increase/decrease indent · link. A box's menu is **the document's plus its own action**, because right-clicking a box is still right-clicking inside text.
- **THE ITEMS ARE A TABLE, NOT MARKUP**, which is what lets the two contexts be one component rather than two menus that drift apart. Every row cancels `mousedown`: a menu that steals the selection cannot act on it, and the failure is SILENT — the command runs against an empty range and looks like a dead button.
- **⛔ CUT AND COPY GO THROUGH THE BROWSER'S OWN EDITING COMMAND, deliberately.** The async Clipboard API needs a permission this app cannot ask for from a menu click, and a refused permission is a silent no-op. `execCommand` acts on the live selection and REPORTS whether it worked, so a refusal is said out loud and names the shortcut that always works (LOUD-FAILURE).
- **MEASURED, not asserted:** Underline from the menu reaches the stored document · Bullets from the menu reaches the stored document · the document menu offers no `delete-box` while the box menu does · `Delete this box` removes it from storage · **and Ctrl+Z brings it back**, because focus comes home after the delete (the B421489 trap).
- **HIS WORDS:** *"the delete option shouldn't just be shown, like, anytime I click on the box… I should only be able to use the keystroke to delete or a right click and then delete option. And then the right click should have the normal formatting option, like it's a Word document or an email… Just copy Word."*
- **THE SCOPE:** selecting a box shows the ring and the resize handles and **no delete ×** · Delete/Backspace still deletes · right-click on a box offers Delete plus its text formatting · right-click in the document gives a Word/Outlook-style menu — cut, copy, paste (the three paste modes already exist), bold, italic, underline, font, size, colour, highlight, bullets and numbering, indent and outdent, link.
- **THE STOPPING RULE IT CLOSED ON:** the delete × gone from selection · both menus driven by a real right-click in a harness · every item asserted against the STORED document. All three met.
