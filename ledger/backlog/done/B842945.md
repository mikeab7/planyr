In his screenshot the "Older version → Newer version" bar plus the legend sits ON TOP of the note content mid-scroll — his own lines "jerry@broadacrellc.com" and "M: (832) 309-0891" were visibly occluded behind it. Minted **B842945** from the same reserved block. DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open/⏳Verify/Done for "sticky header overlap", "conflict review scroll", "legend overlap": no prior item — the sticky-header layout was part of the original B849107 ship and never previously reported as overlapping.)*
`[x]` **SHIPPED.** ROOT CAUSE: the version bar + legend was `position: sticky` INSIDE the scrolling redline pane. Sticky reserves its layout space only ONCE, at its original flow position; once that gap scrolls past, the pinned copy paints (at `zIndex: 1`, above the redline card's `zIndex: auto`) over whatever note content has since scrolled up underneath it — exactly his reported symptom. FIX, structural rather than a sticky-offset tweak: the version bar + legend is now DOCKED — its own `flex: 0 0 auto` sibling ABOVE the scrolling pane (`flex: 1 1 auto; overflow-y: auto`), never inside it. `overflow-y: auto` clips all inner content to the pane's own box, so content can structurally never render above the pane's top edge and reach the header's space — not merely made less likely, made impossible by the CSS containment itself. It is still visible throughout the scroll (better than sticky: no scroll-timing edge case to get wrong).
