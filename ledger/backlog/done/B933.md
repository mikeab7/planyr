- **AUDIT-FIRST correction of B821's diagnosis:** B821 blamed a "docked-panel resize → un-ghosted `setView` tile-wipe." Instrumented in a headless build, a panel toggle actually fires **zero** `viewprereset` — Leaflet converts a same-zoom, in-viewport `setView({animate:false})` into an internal `panBy`, so it never wipes on a toggle. The real flash is the **ghost B837 added**: B837 made the `sizeChanged` branch ALWAYS `spawnGhost()` (a full `wrap.cloneNode(true)` of the tile layer) before re-syncing; that frozen clone sits on top at the PRE-shift position and drops up to 5 s later, so every click shows the aerial off-position then snap back.
- **Fix (`SitePlanner.jsx` basemap view-sync effect):** `viewprereset` (the wipe) only needs to fire on a ZOOM change. A panel/rail toggle and a pure-pan settle are same-zoom re-centers, so `commit()` now moves with `map.panBy` (translates the pane, lazy-loads only the newly exposed edge tiles, never wipes) and spawns NO ghost. The ghost is reserved for a genuine zoom change (setView really wipes → B65's snapshot masks it). `sizeChanged` now calls `invalidateSize({pan:false})` (resize with no pan/wipe) then re-centers through the no-wipe commit. Supersedes B821/B837's wipe-then-mask for the toggle path; B65's zoom-gesture ghost is left intact.
