- Verify: live
- **MEASURED FIRST (the brief said the cause was not measured):** reproduced on unmodified main in a real browser, four one-line boxes 40 px apart. TWO independent causes:
  1. **A box inside a box (the reported "indented" and "over the neighbour").** A real Ctrl+C of text in a box puts `<p data-pm-slice="1 1 ["noteAnchor",{aid,x:40,y:100,w:560,…}]">…</p>` on the clipboard — the slice remembers it came from inside a `noteAnchor`. Pasting it at the start of ANOTHER box's text made ProseMirror re-create the wrapper: stored shape `noteAnchor b › noteAnchor{x:40,y:100} › paragraph "…anWe did get…"`. A box is absolutely placed, so the inner one drew at ITS OWN x/y from the OUTER box's corner — 57 px right of the receiving box and 100 px below it, on top of the boxes underneath. The schema allowed it (`noteAnchor` is a `block`, a box's content is `block+`) and nothing noticed.
  2. **Growth.** A box's height is its words (`h: null`), boxes are absolutely placed, so ANY edit that adds a line grew the box straight down over what sat there (plain-text paste: 37 → 54 px, bottom edge 16 px past the next box's top). Nothing ever asked whether the growth had just covered somebody.
  3. **The chip.** The paste-options tab was placed "just below the caret" with no knowledge of what was there.
- **Fix:** `lib/notesAnchorNest.js` — a box is only ever a direct child of the document; anywhere else it is unwrapped to its own children (an `appendTransaction`, so it rides the paste's undo step; and `unnestDoc` on read for a page that already stored one). `lib/notesBoxClear.js` (pure) + `lib/notesBoxClearPlugin.js` — after a user edit that changes a box's rectangle, boxes it NEWLY covers are pushed just clear (cascading), as one appended transaction (one Ctrl+Z puts words and neighbours back; stored on the node so it survives reload); an overlap the person already had is never touched; never after undo/redo, sync, version restore, on open, or mid-gesture. `lib/notesChipPlace.js` — the tab takes its old spot when clear, else the nearest spot that covers no text. The paste offer is not retired by the push.
