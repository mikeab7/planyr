- **Two different hatches, two different causes, both measured.** (1) **The app's own hatches** (trailer stripes, sidewalk dots, berm, landscape, easement/encumbrance/markup hatches) are SVG `<pattern patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse">`, whose lattice starts at the canvas's own top-left corner. The drag moves that corner and the pan compensation moves the drawing back — so every edge held while the stripes slid by the drag (mod one tile). At 100 % the hatched strips and sidewalk dots were the ONLY drawing pixels that changed on a step. (2) **FEMA's red floodway stripes** are drawn by FEMA's server into each exported picture. Exporting two extents 8 px apart and diffing showed FEMA anchors its stripes to the GROUND (zero difference outside labels) — the change was ours: every drag step resized the map and re-requested the picture, re-flowing its "Zone AE" labels and, on a scaled display, re-sampling every edge (up to Δ115/255).
- **Fix.** (1) `lib/hatchAnchor.js`: every planner pattern's `patternTransform` is `translate(<render view's feet origin>) rotate(…) scale(labelK)`, the origin rounded to whole device px — the stripes are pinned to a fixed piece of ground, so a panel drag/open/close, window resize or pan never slides them (a zoom re-lays them around the same ground point; the tile is constant-screen-size by design). Export unchanged: the anchor composes before B794960's `labelK` scale, which still sets the printed tile size. (2) The map's box no longer follows the panel (B2154768 ×2), so FEMA is not re-requested during a docked-panel drag at all.
- **Result (`ui-audit/verify-panel-resize-steady.mjs` pixel phase):** a line across an app-hatched strip outside the panel: **byte-identical on every step at 100 %**; ≤ 2/255 at 125/150/215 % (a pattern re-samples within 2/255 even on whole device px — below a just-noticeable difference; the stated allowance, scaled displays only); a line across FEMA's floodway stripes and the whole FEMA picture: **0 changed pixels** at every scale. Pre-fix build: hatch lines Δ9–19, FEMA picture up to 2,706 px changed. Unit: `test/hatchAnchor.test.js` (transform, anchor maths, wiring of every `<pattern>`), `test/pixelSteady.test.js`. `e2e/hatch-pattern-export-scale.spec.js` (B794960, printed tile size) is unaffected by construction — it reads the `scale()` term only.
- **Adjacent cases from the brief:** panel open/close and every left-rail panel ride the same box (`geoDockX`) and anchor; window resize/fullscreen keep the app hatches pinned (anchor) but DO re-request FEMA's picture for the new extent — its stripes stay on the ground (server-anchored), its text labels may re-flow once (FEMA's label placement, not a hatch). The right Tools rail is fixed-width. PDF/PNG export: the anchor is a translation before the export scale, so the printed tile size is unchanged; the phase on paper is now ground-anchored too.
