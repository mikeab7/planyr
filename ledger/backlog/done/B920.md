`[x]` **FIXED + sandbox-verified this session (build green · 15-test unit lib · headless e2e pass logged-out).** Root-caused on the owner's live plan (site `smrp1wrgg6u5`): a single closed markup — `kind:"polygon"`, `fillOpacity:0` (invisible), locked, bounding box ≈5,117 ft × 4,430 ft — blanketed the whole site, so clicking anywhere in its interior (all the empty corridor between the road strips) selected the invisible shape instead of the road you meant to grab; because it was also locked you just got the four-arrow move cursor and nothing happened, and "Send to Back" was powerless (it's already at the back, **and** it's a hit-AREA problem, not paint order). - Origin: filed 2026-07-21 from chat ("NEW-1").
- **Fix (`SitePlanner.jsx` `renderMarkupNode`):** a closed markup (polygon/rect/ellipse) now grabs by its whole INTERIOR only when it is FILLED (`closedFill = fillOpacity>0`). Each closed shape renders as a **fat transparent hit companion** (`stroke rgba(0,0,0,0.001)`, `strokeWidth={MK_HIT_PX}`) UNDER a pointer-inert visible shape — the exact line/polyline technique — with the companion's `pointerEvents` = `"all"` when filled (whole-body grab, B155/B156 small annotations preserved) vs `"stroke"` when unfilled (grab only along the stroke + tolerance, interior falls through to whatever's under it). `fillProps` retired.
- **New pure lib `lib/markupPick.js`** (Node-tested, zoom-agnostic — tolerance is feet = on-screen px ÷ `view.ppf`): encodes the SAME `fillOpacity>0` rule the render uses, so the declarative hit area and the JS picker (B921 below) can never diverge. `markupHitModel`/`markupUnderPoint`/`markupsUnderPoint`/`nextMarkupSelection` + geometry helpers (`ringArea`/`pointInRing`/`distToPolyline`/`distToRing`/`boxCorners`/`ellipseRing`).
- **Dedup note (DEDUPE-FIRST):** tightens **B155/B156** (unfilled-closed-shape interior grab — right for a small annotation, pathological for a huge invisible one) without regressing them (filled shapes still grab by interior; open paths unchanged). Reconciled with the shared `src/shared/markup/hitTest.js` — its own header note says the Site Planner deliberately keeps SVG-native picking, so this stays declarative rather than re-routing through the imperative picker (which is fill-agnostic and used by Doc Review, where unfilled-interior-grab is intended). Not forked.
- **Verification harness:** `e2e/markup-reach.spec.js` "B920" drives the real SVG canvas logged-out — draw a building, lay a big UNFILLED markup rect over it, deselect, click the building's centre → the markup is NOT grabbed (`data-testid="markup-selected"` absent) AND a double-click there opens the ELEMENT properties (the click reached the building through the invisible markup); the markup is still reachable by clicking near its stroke.
