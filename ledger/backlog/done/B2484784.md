- **Reported:** a click meant only to select a row by its left (ID) cell "automatically jumps to move it"; follow-up: it does so with essentially zero pointer movement.
- **Root cause (AUDIT-FIRST — inference in the dispatch brief CONFIRMED and then found to be worse; reproduced in real Chromium on `origin/main` 5fad7c5 before any change):** `GridView.startRowDrag` ARMED the move on `mousedown`, not on travel: rows dimmed with a dashed outline, the page cursor pinned to "grabbing", a live drop target painted, and the per-frame loop started — with a motionless mouse. Three consequences, all measured:
  1. **THE ZERO-MOVEMENT MOVE.** The same loop auto-scrolls the grid whenever the pointer is within ~36 px of its top/bottom edge, held still or not. A grip press has no click path (`onClick` was only supplied for the ID cell), so its release COMMITTED whatever drop the autoscroll had slid under the stationary pointer. Grip press-and-release with zero movement, held 400 ms on a row near the bottom edge of the grid: **a row moved** (`moved=true`, a write to storage), single row and inside a multi-row selection alike; the grid had scrolled 130+ px under a still mouse. Held 150 ms it scrolled 60+ px. This is "barely moved or maybe not even moved … and it still moves it".
  2. **Jitter nests.** Any 4 px of travel set a one-way `moved` flag, then the release committed wherever the pointer sat. 5 px (mouse) or 9 px (pen) of wobble at the bottom edge of a selection released over the neighbouring row and wrote a change.
  3. **Armed on every press-and-hold**, on the grip, on an ID cell in a selection, and on ctrl+press (modifiers were ignored) — the visible "jump into moving".
  The grip is the left 8 px of the 30 px ID cell, so "click the left cell" lands on it often.
- **Fix (`public/sequence/index.html`, `startRowDrag` only; the #2544 geometry — `rowFloatAtPointer`, `rowDropIntent`, the zoom-measured drop math — untouched):** a press only RECORDS where it began. Nothing is dimmed, pinned, painted, scrolled or committed until the held pointer has travelled past `rowDragThreshold` — **6 px mouse / 10 px pen / 16 px touch** (screen px, picked per press from the preceding `pointerdown`'s `pointerType`; pure `rowDragThreshold` / `rowDragArmed`). A release before that is a CLICK (ID cell: selects that row as before; **the grip now also selects its row**, it did nothing before). A drag that arms but is let go back within the threshold of its press point writes nothing (no reorder, no parent/date change, no undo entry, no sync). The drop is re-read at the release position (the last frame's paint could be a frame stale). A context menu (touch long-press), Escape, window blur, a second press, a button released where the page could not see it, or unmount CANCEL the gesture instead of leaving a half-armed drag for the next click to drop. Ctrl/Cmd/Alt+press on an ID cell no longer picks the selection up. One `moveRows` → one setData → one Ctrl+Z, unchanged.
- **Judgement calls, stated:** (a) a plain click on an ID cell inside a multi-row selection still collapses the selection to that row on release (Microsoft Project / Excel behaviour, and what it did before #2544); the dispatch asked it not to collapse "unless a deliberate drag" — a click that is only a click is the one way to narrow a selection, so I kept it; it never collapses on press or on a wobble. (b) Ctrl-click does NOT toggle row selection — it never did; not built (out of scope, and ctrl was starting a pick-up, now fixed). (c) Touch: a tap reaches the page only as a compatibility mouse press/release at lift-off, so it was never at risk of arming; a touch DRAG scrolls the page (no mouse events) and the long-press context menu now cancels. Touch/pen cases are regression guards, not red on the old code.
