**THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION AS ASKED, verbatim first, before any explanation.** `minGap` — `ROW2_CENTER_HYSTERESIS_PX` is 12, `CENTER_SLOT_GAP` (the bound formula's own gap term) is also 12. **12 is below his computed 33px headroom.** By his own either/or framing that means "the condition is passing and the chip is not centring anyway" — but neither of his two hypotheses was quite right once measured, because a third possibility existed that his framing didn't name: **the code was not centring the 157px control he was asking about at all — it was centring a DIFFERENT, wider thing and calling it "the chip."**

**THE MEASUREMENT, live, at 1191px, before touching any code.** `row2CenterContentRef` (B1511712's own centering wrapper) wrapped BOTH the Grid/Split/Gantt `ViewToggle` (157.2px, matching his hand measurement almost exactly) AND the review-inbox button beside it (61.1px) as ONE combined unit — 222.3px total including the internal gap. Using the owner's own real numbers (leftW 484, rightW 316, rowW 1191, minGap 12): `centerSlotMaxWidth` = `1191 - 2*(484+12)` = **199px**. 199 < 222.3, so the code's own condition correctly and consistently evaluated to FALSE for the 222px bundle it was measuring — flow mode, exactly as observed. Flow mode's resulting position for that bundle's first child (the ViewToggle) computes to centre 647.0 — matching his reported 647 to one decimal place. **The math was never wrong. `minGap` was never the defect.** The defect was that "the chip" in code and "the chip" in the owner's report were two different DOM subtrees of two different widths, and only one of them was the Grid/Split/Gantt control he was actually measuring.

**Re-running the SAME bound against the 157px control ALONE — the one he asked about — proves it would have centred all along:** 199 ≥ 157.2, clearing by 32.6–41.8px depending on scope (grid/split/gantt), consistent with his own hand-computed 33px headroom on the left side. So the honest answer to his forced choice is: this is not "the fallback working as designed" (the real control fits fine) and it is not "a bug in `minGap`" (12 was always the right number) — it is a **scoping bug**: the wrong element was being measured, bounded, and positioned.

**THE FIX.** `src/workspaces/scheduler/components/ScheduleToolbar.jsx` — `ScheduleCenter` now renders ONLY `<ViewToggle/>` (Grid/Split/Gantt); the review-inbox button (with its unread badge) moved into `ScheduleActions`, placed first, using the identical reserved-visibility idiom (`visibility`, never conditional mount) that Format/B1218496 already established there — a control that mounts a beat late still cannot shift an already-pressable sibling sideways, the exact bug class B1218496 fixed once already. `AppHeader.jsx`'s centering math (the bound formula, the hysteresis, the snap-not-animate rule) is **completely unchanged** — B1511712's implementation was correct; only what it was asked to centre was wrong. This also fixed a latent measurement bug in `ui-audit/verify-schedule-header-widths.mjs`'s own probe: `centerParts` had unioned in the review button as part of "the center group's content box" (correct before this fix, since both lived in the same zone) — left as-is it would now silently double-count the button into both the center AND toolbar boxes.
