- **THE ANSWERS TO HIS TWO QUESTIONS.** It snaps back at **zero non-whitespace characters** — not earlier — and it restores **the DEFAULT**, "Untitled page", never the previous value.
- **THE CAUSE.** `renameNode` coerced a blank name to the default, and the title field is a CONTROLLED input that writes on every keystroke. So deleting the last character wrote "Untitled page" straight back and the field re-rendered with all thirteen characters restored. There was no way to clear it.
- **THE FIX IS WHERE THE DEFAULT LIVES.** `renameNode` stores what it is given, including nothing; `commitTitle` applies the default when the field is LEFT (blur, Enter, or Tab into the page); the rail shows a dimmed placeholder for a page that is momentarily unnamed and **never writes it back**. A blank title is a legal momentary state and costs nothing — since B342992 a title is never load-bearing for identity or reachability.
- **THE GUARD WAS AMENDED RATHER THAN DELETED.** "No exported op can mint a titleless node" now reads "a page may be blank WHILE YOU TYPE and may never SETTLE blank", and asserts all three: the rename may leave it empty, the commit fills it, and the rail never shows it empty.
