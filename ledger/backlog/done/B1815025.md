`[x]` **What it found on `origin/main`, measured with ONE instrument: 14/48 rows green.** Six families, none covered by the three prior rounds:
  1. **Grab-jump** — touching either grip on a page pinned narrower than the natural card snapped the page ~140px wider before the pointer had moved (140px of page against 2.5px of finger). The drag's floor included the natural page width instead of what content actually needs.
  2. **The opposite edge moves** — narrowing from the LEFT grip moved the RIGHT boundary by the full travel. You grab one edge and a different one moves.
  3. **Presets shift the content** — switching width presets moved the body text by up to 318px.
  4. **A right-grip narrow shifts the content** — 50px.
  5. **Everything breaks at a zoom other than 100%** — content moved 20–46px.
  6. **A release outside the window commits a width nobody asked for.**
`[x]` **THE FIX IS THE DELETION OF THE MECHANISM, NOT A FOURTH PATCH.** All three prior rounds held the words still by COMPENSATING — writing `scrollLeft` by the measured layout shift — and a scroll is a BOUNDED resource, which is why round 2 double-applied it and round 3 watched it clamp. On the transform workspace (B1815024) a left-edge widen moves the sheet's own workspace origin OUT by exactly the amount its inner padding grows IN, so the body's workspace position is **arithmetically invariant** and the view is neither read nor written. **Nothing to compensate, so nothing to double-apply and nothing to clamp.** Deleted outright: `matSidePads`, `leftWidthGripPad`, `matPadX`, `matPaneWidth`, `matReachWidth`, `MAT_EXTRA_RIGHT`, `MAT_EXTRA_BOTTOM*`, `boxGestureActive`, the grip auto-scroll, and the growth-compensation layout effect.
`[x]` **The page has TWO stored numbers now**, and separating them is what makes a left edge expressible at all: `pageWidth` (the writing COLUMN — what that attribute has always meant, so nothing migrates) and **`pageMarginLeft`** (the blank paper to its left; new, defaults to 0, renders byte-identically on every existing page). Both commit in ONE transaction (`setNotePageGeometry`) so a drag is one undo step and an undo cannot restore half a geometry.
`[x]` **The margin SURVIVES A RELOAD**, which the React ref it replaces did not — a margin a drag opened silently vanished on the next open and the page came back narrower than it was left. That old limitation was an ASSERTION in `verify-notes-page-width.mjs`; it is now inverted.
`[x]` **Stated behaviour change, deliberate:** narrowing from the left past the blank paper now squeezes the COLUMN and the words move right with the boundary (what dragging a margin marker into your own text does in every word processor), instead of moving the right edge. Returned explicitly as `contentShift` rather than left for a caller to infer.
`[x]` **Stated exception, deliberate:** picking **"Full width"** moves the view to bring the page's full width on screen. It is the one preset that names the PANE rather than a number, and leaving the view alone was measured at a 907px frame overhanging a 923px pane by 156px — a preset called Full width you could not see all of. Every other preset still moves the content by zero.
