### B51 — Stitcher async handlers have no in-flight guard → re-entrant interleave corrupts placed sheets `[Document Review]` (bug) — data-loss, high
`[x]` 🔧 fixed 2026-06-15 (branch `doc-review/async-safety`): `loadStitch` sets a `loadingRef` while it rebuilds `pdfs[]`/`placed[]`, and `openFiles`/`addSheet` bail when it's set — so a click during a load can't have its sheet clobbered by the load's blind `setPlaced(out)`. (`bindSource` stays unguarded; it's internal to `openFiles`.) `openFiles`/`addSheet`/`bindSource`/`loadStitch` each `setBusy(true)…await…finally setBusy(false)` but none *reads* busy as a re-entrancy guard, and they mix functional updates (`addSheet`: `setPlaced(arr=>[...arr,…])`) with snapshot overwrites (`loadStitch`: `setPlaced(out)`). Clicking "add sheet" while a load is mid-await lets the load's blind `setPlaced(out)` clobber the just-added sheet. Fix: `busyRef` guard at the top of each, or disable the tray/open while busy; prefer functional updates / reconcile rather than overwrite.
