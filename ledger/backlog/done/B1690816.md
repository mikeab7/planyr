`[x]` **FIXED AND VERIFIED THIS SESSION.**

**ROOT CAUSE, measured, not assumed.** `collapseRingSpikes`/`ringSpikes` (`roadNetwork.js`, B1611840) flag a vertex only when its turn exceeds `RING_SPIKE_TURN_DEG` (100°) AND at least one adjoining segment is under `RING_SPIKE_LEN_FT` (2.0 ft). The header comment's own reasoning for 100° was to keep a real corner in this geometry (a road's flat end cap ~90°, a curb-return mouth chord near 180°) from ever being caught — but that protection was ALREADY provided by the LENGTH half alone, since a real corner always rides a segment at least the road's own half-width, comfortably over 2 ft. So the 100° pad was slack the length guard never needed, and it let exactly the reported class through: a fine-grained (0.1°) sweep of the same oblique road-into-pad junction reproduced genuine spurious vertices 0.03–0.05 ft apart turning 76–99°, all under the old 100° bound and therefore invisible to `ringSpikes`. The only real ceiling a tessellated arc vertex can ever reach is `DEFAULT_TESS_DEG` (6°, `roadGeometry.js`) — the arc builder always spends `ceil(sweepDeg / DEFAULT_TESS_DEG)` steps, so no single step can turn more than that, by construction, not by observation.

**THE FIX.** `RING_SPIKE_TURN_DEG` moved from the literal `100` to `DEFAULT_TESS_DEG * 5` (30°, imported from `roadGeometry.js` so the two constants can't drift apart) — over 5× the one real ceiling a legitimate tessellated vertex can reach, with every legitimate sharp corner still excluded by `RING_SPIKE_LEN_FT` exactly as before (unchanged). Swept at 0.1° resolution across the oblique 0–89° range on two width/radius classes and the road-to-road tee's 5–175° range (2,172 rings total, scripted diagnostic, not the shipped test): the new bound leaves no ring uncleaned except an already-triangular sliver, which single-vertex removal cannot shrink further by design (`collapseRingSpikes` correctly refuses a ring below 4 vertices).

**A SECOND, RELATED DEFECT FOUND BY THE SAME FINE SWEEP.** Extending the sweep to the road-to-road tee case at side angles nothing had swept before (e.g. 11°) found the union occasionally emits a whole SEPARATE micro-polygon — a phantom disconnected fleck of pavement (a 0.03 sf triangular "region") or a phantom enclosed fleck of unpaved ground (a 1.33 sf triangular "hole"). Neither is a vertex spike riding a still-viable ring — each IS the ring, already at the 3-vertex floor `collapseRingSpikes` correctly refuses to touch — so it needed a second, AREA-based rule rather than a lower turn bound. `dissolveRings` now drops any emitted outer region or hole whose area (after `collapseRingSpikes`) is under a new `RING_MICRO_AREA_FLOOR_SQFT` (10 sq ft) — ~7.5× the largest measured noise fragment (1.33 sf), ~13× below the smallest legitimate feature this module ever emits (a roundabout's own central island, ≥~130 sf at the smallest vehicle class, `roundabout.js`). Dropping a sub-floor hole only ever ADDS pavement (removes an unpaved gap), which is the correct direction for the "no enclosed unpaved area" bar this whole family of fixes exists to hold — never a regression of it.
