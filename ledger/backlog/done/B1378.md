`[x]` **Shipped 2026-08-03.** A bonded pad with no wall role is now a reported defect in its own right.
- Verify: sandbox
- Origin: filed 2026-08-03 from chat (NEW-4)
- **Why it stayed quiet for five days across six sites.** `assemblyIntegrity`'s detector IS the healer's own geometry diff — which is the right design and is kept. But three untagged pads bonded to a building, sitting exactly where side parking belongs, are **geometrically indistinguishable from correct**: nothing was in the wrong place, so nothing could be reported. What was wrong was that they had no identity, one of the lookups keyed on that identity gated a destructive rung, and the owner found out by noticing missing sidewalks.
- **The invariant added:** a non-stack pad bonded to a building host must resolve to a wall AND carry the role tag for that wall. `siteModel.orphanWallPads(els)` is the pure detector, measured on the INPUT before anything is healed; `assemblyIntegrity(...).orphans` surfaces it alongside `.tears`, `orphanPayload` bounds the telemetry, and `assemblyGuard` in `SitePlanner.jsx` reports `assembly-orphan-pad` at the same seam as every other assembly invariant. Because the re-tag is a **zero-geometry** repair it would otherwise be discarded there (that seam ignores sub-tolerance churn on purpose), so the orphan case adopts the healed list explicitly.
- Tests: `test/orphanWallPads.test.js` — reports all nine Silvestri orphans while `.tears` is empty (the whole point), silent on a coherent plan, bounded payload, and a source guard on the seam wiring.
