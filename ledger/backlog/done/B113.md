### B113 — Planner is not responsive at mobile width `[Site Planyr / UI]` (feature)  *(branch "B99")*
`[x]` **Done 2026-06-16.** At ~390px the fixed 168px right rail + the left rail/panel consume most of the width and the canvas collapses to a sliver; the header wraps. The planner is effectively desktop-only. Needs a mobile treatment (collapsible/off-canvas rails, a bottom toolbar). Larger effort — roadmap, not a quick fix. (Audit L1; see `ui-audit/screens/planner-mobile.png`.)
> Fix (`SitePlanner.jsx`): a `matchMedia("(max-width: 760px)")` `narrow` flag drives a phone treatment — **every mobile style is `narrow ?`-gated, so the desktop layout is byte-identical**. When narrow: (1) the right 168px tool palette no longer eats width — it **slides in from the right as an overlay** (with a tap-to-dismiss backdrop) summoned by a floating **"✎ Tools"** button, and auto-closes when you pick a tool so you can draw; (2) the left menu panel (Yield/Parcel/…) **overlays the canvas** (`position:absolute`, capped to `min(320px, 100vw-74px)`) instead of pushing it, its drag-resize handle hidden; (3) the thin 54px left rail stays inline; (4) the header scrolls horizontally instead of wrapping (condensed gap/padding). Net: the canvas keeps full usable width on a phone. Verified headless at 390×844: `planner-mobile.png` (collapsed + FAB), `planner-mobile-tools.png` (tool drawer), `planner-mobile-panel.png` (yield drawer); desktop `planner-plan.png` unchanged.
