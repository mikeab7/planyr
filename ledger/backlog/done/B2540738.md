Belong to ≥2 teams, click A then B quickly → B's name and admin controls over A's members; if A's answers landed last they stayed, and Remove / Change role then sent `removeMember(B, uidFromA)`. Also: a Remove that finished after the person switched reloaded the OLD team's roster over the new one (`loadRoster(sel)` closed over the old selection).
- **Fix:** `lib/teamRoster.js` `createRosterLoader` — every load takes a ticket; a result is applied only if it is the newest ticket AND its team is still the selected one (read through `selRef`), members + invites are fetched together and applied TOGETHER, a failure is reported ("Couldn't load this team's members…") instead of leaving a stale list. `TeamPanel` clears both lists the moment the selection changes and cancels the in-flight load on cleanup.
- **Tests:** `test/teamRosterRace.test.js` — a resolver that answers A after B; A's members back but invites still out when B is chosen; a late reload for a deselected team; atomic apply + error reporting; source guards. Red-proof: removing the ticket / selection check fails 3 of them.
