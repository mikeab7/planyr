- **THE THREE NUMBERS, from his real production data (read-only Supabase query, 529 leaf tasks across 6 plans):** healthOverride is true on **302 (57%)**; a rule actually decides the colour on **29 (5.5%)**; not overridden and matching no rule (falls through to raw/stale stored colour) on **198 (37%)**. His live `settings.healthRules` holds exactly **one** rule — `finishPastDays` days=1 → red — confirmed from storage, not guessed off his screenshot. **Said loudly, as he asked: automation is effectively not running on his schedules — it decides about 1 task in 18.**
- **THE MECHANISM, traced in code:** `updateTask` sets `healthOverride:true` any time `health` is written without the caller also passing `healthOverride` itself (`if ('health' in updates && !('healthOverride' in updates)) u.healthOverride = true;`). Every ordinary status pick — the grid dot, the status pill — routes through this, so **the badge is set by normal use, not by a deliberate lock gesture**, which is why it marks 57% of his schedule and why he can't read it as a signal.
- **THE BUG ITSELF:** `RuleColorPicker`'s dropdown rendered inline (no portal) inside `AutomationPanel`, whose own `onMouseDown={stopPropagation}` — the same pattern every side panel here uses — ate every mousedown inside the panel before it could reach the picker's own outside-click listener. Measured live: a click on the panel's blank space, or on a DIFFERENT rule row's own `<select>`, left the dropdown open and floating over that control; only a click outside the whole panel closed it.
- **THE FIX, matching how every other menu here behaves (HealthPicker/StatusPicker):** portal the dropdown to `document.body` as `position:fixed` with `{...MENU_STOPS_GRID}` on the menu's own root (not the panel's), **and** listen for the outside click in the capture phase — the one piece no other picker needed, because no other picker sits inside a panel that blanket-stops propagation. Confirmed live: blank-panel click, another row's control, and outside-the-panel click all now close it correctly; picking a colour still applies and closes; the panel itself never closes by accident.
