So "recents at the bottom of the picker" is only reachable by replacing the always-visible native control with our own popover and keeping the native wheel as a "Custom…" escape hatch inside it. That is exactly what shipped.

Clicking it opens a compact popover anchored to the chip: **(a)** the standard palette on a real grid · **(b)** a hairline divider · **(c)** **RECENTLY USED**, same swatch size, same grid, and the section is **absent entirely when empty** · **(d)** a quiet **Custom…** row that opens the native wheel for any colour off the grid.
