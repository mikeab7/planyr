### B1756546 — A drive running nearly PARALLEL to a pad edge builds a curb return hundreds of feet from the junction `[Site Planner / road]` (bug) #site-planner #road #geometry
`[ ]` **Found while diagnosing B1717616 (×4); PRE-EXISTING ON EVERY BUILD, not fixed there, same scope reasoning as B1756545.**
`[x]` **MEASURED.** The fillet's corner is the intersection of the drive's own edge LINE with the target edge. As the approach flattens, that intersection runs away without bound: a 48 ft drive meeting a pad edge 86° off perpendicular puts the corner **351 ft** along that edge — **50 ft past the drive's own far end** — and a full R = 40 return is built out there. The two wedges then cover **4,834 sq ft** (pre-#1755) or **9,127 sq ft** (PR #1755) against a 14,700 sq ft road strip, reaching 391 ft from the junction. It shows up at 86–89° off perpendicular across widths 16–48 ft and radii 24–40 ft (25 of 7,200 swept cases).
`[ ]` **A partial fix was written and deliberately NOT shipped.** Clamping the fillet's side-road room to what is left PAST the corner (`sideAvail − dot(corner − T, d)`, the same shape of error B1717617 fixed in `nodeJunction`'s overshoot) removes the return on the side whose corner is past the drive's end — but the OPPOSITE corner still reaches 352 ft, because its corner sits *behind* the cap and the run that carries it is along the pad edge. A half-fix inside the function that has failed six times is not worth shipping; the whole question needs answering at once.
`[x]` **DECIDED AND BUILT (B2498960, 2026-10-29) — the product call:** a drive within 10° of the face, or whose corner lies past its own far end (hw / sin φ > the drive's run), is a SKIM: it builds NO curb return, the pavement lies against the field for the stretch it really touches, and the field's outline is open exactly there (`roadJunctions.skimGeometry`, `SKIM_MAX_PHI_DEG`). **Measured:** `test/roadSkimSweep.test.js` sweeps 380 cases (width 16–48 × radius 24/40 × approach 0–89° × drive length 60/140): no wedge vertex lies beyond the drive's run plus its return radius (the 2026-07 worst case was 391 ft from the junction); the sweep is checked non-vacuous (126 skims, 100+ ordinary two-return junctions). The half-fix that was deliberately not shipped earlier is not what shipped: the whole question is answered at once, in the caller, with `teeGeometry` untouched apart from one clamp (a sharp corner is never built past the end of its edge).
