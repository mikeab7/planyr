### B342995 — Delete confirmation counted the note itself as one of its own pages `[Notes]` (bug) #notes #ui  *(owner chat block, 2026-08-10)*
`[x]` "Delete 2?" for one page with one child.
- Verify: sandbox
- Origin: filed 2026-08-10 from chat (NEW-4).
- **THE CAUSE, in one line:** `subtreePageIds` includes the node itself — correct for a DELETE, which needs every body to clear — and the confirmation rendered that same number. So a page with a single child announced "Delete 2?" and then "and its 2 pages".
- Now `descendantPageIds` + `subpagesPhrase`: **"Delete + 1 subpage?"** and **"…and its 1 subpage."** The delete's cascade is unchanged; only the number shown to a person moved.
- **⛔ AND THERE IS DELIBERATELY NO "SECTION" IN THE WORDING.** The owner's suggested phrasing was "1 page in 1 section", but B1420 removed that species on purpose — an empty parent is a PAGE with children, not a different kind of thing. Re-introducing it in copy would re-introduce it in people's heads. **Subpages** is the honest word at every depth; flagged here rather than silently diverged.
- An inflated count is not cosmetic: it is how somebody is led to believe they lost something they did not, which is what sent the previous session chasing a phantom.
