- **Proven on the live DB, not inferred.** On Goose Creek "Plan II — 220K, 440K, 700K" (site `sms69x8rb2qk`) and both copies (`sms93hmiy4nl`, `sms93j3sfc04`), two host buildings had 1 sidewalk instead of 2: `e1454629danlgq` lost `e1454689dshobp` (tombstoned 2026-07-29 21:36:28) and `e1454729ykduhm` lost `e1454744tcmstb` (2026-07-30 14:49:29). **Both were SOLO single-row commits with the host alive and untouched** — not a cascade, not a merge. The four intact hosts on the plan still have both sidewalks, and the two damaged hosts are exactly the two whose side-parking field had been EXPLODED.
- **The mechanism.** `empSidePark(b, side)` found side parking strictly by `x.sideParkSide === side`, so after the Explode it returned `undefined`. `growEmployeeSide(b, side, -1)` then fell past `else if (park)` into its last branch, `else if (sw) removeFeature(sw.id)` — **and deleted the sidewalk with a full parking field still sitting on that wall.** The ladder is meant to walk rows → remove parking → remove sidewalk; the missing tag made the middle rung invisible, so one click jumped to the end. `removeFeature` then called `relayoutWallKids` to pull the parking back flush now the strip was gone, and skipped the same untagged pieces — the physical receipt: `e1454736ykduhm` still sat with its inner face **5.00 ft** off the wall of `e1454729ykduhm`, the empty sidewalk slot, unchanged.
- **The fix, in two halves.** (a) *"What is on this wall?"* is answered by **GEOMETRY** — `sideParkPadsOn` returns every bonded, non-stack, non-strip pad whose side resolves to this wall, with the tag only as a fast path, which is the same forgiving test `empSideSidewalk` has always used (and precisely why the sidewalk stayed visible while the parking vanished). `sideParkingOn`, `empSidePark`, `empSideAddTitle`, `employeeSideHasAny` and `ownedSidePark` all read it. (b) The ladder is **structurally incapable** of reaching the sidewalk: that rung is now the `else` of `pads.length`, so a future tag-loss bug degrades to a rung that does nothing instead of one that destroys geometry. On an exploded set "−" peels the outermost piece (the dock-stack LIFO idiom next door) and "+" appends a stall row beyond it, matching its neighbour's hand-trimmed run.
