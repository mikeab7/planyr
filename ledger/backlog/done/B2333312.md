The owner gate (`gradingEnabled()` over `is_admin()`) is untouched and still decides whether the dock item and the switch exist.
- **Off:** no controller (no DEM fetch, sweep, auto-FFE solve, rock fetch, alternative pricing or fix search; the model is null); `gsInputs` equal a non-owner account's; no bands, daylight line, flag pills/dots, corner pills or level-pad line; the two Layers rows are hidden; the panel shows the switch and one line, "Turn this on to suggest finish elevations and show cut and fill for this plan." Turning it off clears everything in the same render; typed FFEs are never touched.
- **Proof (sandbox):** `test/gradingPerPlanSwitch.test.js` (cfg default, grOn wiring, controller mount, non-owner inputs, panel markup off/on) + `ui-audit/verify-grading-harness.mjs` §switch (switch off → no layers, no pills, no cards, model null, one line; on → the round-2 picture returns).
