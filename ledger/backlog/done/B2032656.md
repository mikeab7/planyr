`[x]` **Now:** every kind is shown in the Review pane as `Formatted: …` (author, time, label: "Bold", "Not bold, Italic", "paragraph style", "alignment, indent / spacing", "bullets / numbering", "Table", "Table column widths", "Table row", "Table cell", "Paragraph mark", "Section"), marked in the text (dotted underline), and can be accepted (drop the record, keep the new look) or rejected (the OLD properties come back) singly or with Accept/Reject all.
