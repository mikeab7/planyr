  1. **Double-click ALWAYS opens Properties** for an easement, a centerline road, or a line/polyline markup — it never opens the old on-canvas inline-label text editor. Previously a *second* double-click on an already-selected text-bearing feature popped a floating in-place editor; the owner wants label text edited **only** in the Properties panel's "Inline label" field.   2. **New "Label position" option (road & easement only):** a checkbox under the Inline-label controls — **On the line** (default, unchanged) vs **Inside the road/easement (off the line)**. When "Inside", the repeating label is pushed perpendicular into the body of the feature so it sits in clear space instead of riding on top of the drawn centerline / boundary line: a road or strip easement tucks a quarter of its width off the centerline; a boundary easement aims its labels toward the ring's centroid (a fraction of the ring's smaller extent), so they land inside the boundary. Plain lines/polylines have no interior, so the control only shows for road + easement.
