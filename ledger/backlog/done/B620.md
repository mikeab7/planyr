### B620 — Inline line labels (text riding along a line) as an alternative to callouts `[Site Planner]` (feature)  *(owner-dropped 2026-07-02 as "NEW-6"; deferred that session as not-urgent, then picked up + expanded by the owner 2026-07-02; minted **B620** = B619 + 1; branch `claude/site-planner-backlog-impl-rm7ma4`)*
`[x]` **Shipped 2026-07-02.** A line/polyline/road/easement can carry an optional `inlineLabel` that renders **along the feature itself** (e.g. `18" SANITARY SEWER` on the line) — no separate callout needed. Owner-expanded scope + behavior:
- **Applies to drawn lines/polylines, roads, AND easements**, with **per-feature label density** (owner: "a sewer is small and needs more references, a road far fewer, an easement in between"): the label **repeats along the path** at a per-type spacing — line/polyline **150 ft**, easement **350 ft**, road **700 ft** (always ≥1, centered, when shorter than the spacing). Pure module helpers `inlineLabelPlaces`/`inlineLabelEls`/`inlineLabelAnchorFeet` + `INLINE_LABEL_SPACING`.
- Each instance is **rotated to its local segment**, **auto-flipped** into `[-90,90]` (never upside-down — `((a%180)+180)%180; if(a>90)a-=180`), **offset above the centerline**, in the feature's **own color + a white halo**, **LOD-gated** (hides when the on-screen segment can't fit the text; returns on zoom-in), and — unlike selection chrome — **appears in PNG/PDF exports** (no `data-export="skip"`).
- **Edited two ways:** a panel **"Inline label"** field in each feature's Properties, **and double-click the feature** to type in place (`editInline` state + `beginEditInline`/`commitEditInline`, mirroring the callout editor). Double-clicking a centerline **road** opens the label editor; its type/actions menu stays on **right-click**.
- **Non-sticky by construction** (the key trap): every writer patches the ONE feature (`setMarkups`/`setSelEl`/`commitEditInline`), never `mkStyle`/`liveMarkup`, so a typed label can't bleed into the next drawn shape. Panel fields push **one undo frame per edit** (onFocus), not per keystroke (a review follow-up). **No schema/migration** — `inlineLabel` rides through verbatim (`markups: arr(...)`; roads spread through `migrateRoad`).
