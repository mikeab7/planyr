**Root cause (`lib/siteRegion.js`):** `siteState` let a hand-tuned outline overrule the real state polygon within `NE_BAND_DEG` (0.012°). `TUNED_OVERRULES` limited only Tennessee, on the false belief that every other tuned outline was drawn to all of its lines. Measured by walking every edge of the real polygons and by 0.003° grids (the ticket's numbers were a lower bound — the full scan found more pairs): AL→FL 4,509 (Florida's north edge at 31.02° vs the true 31.000°; Perdido-river west edge ~0.1° into Alabama), AL→GA 5,083 and SC→GA 4,216 (+451, the Savannah) (Georgia's west corner at -85.65° vs the true -85.605°; the whole west edge a straight chord), GA↔FL 3,378 / 1,523 (two overlapping outlines fighting over the St. Marys line), AL→TN 2,804 and MS→TN 1,416 (Tennessee's southern edge at 34.985–34.995° vs the true 35.0°), NC→GA, and Texas: OK→TX 1,895, LA→TX 1,343, AR→TX 506, NM→TX 243 (the outline cuts the Red River in straight chords). An Oklahoma site there lost all 32 Oklahoma rows (#2661) and was offered Texas-only rows.
**Fix:** the real boundary is authoritative everywhere. `TUNED_REACHES` is now the ONLY way a tuned outline may overrule it — an explicit, documented list of river stretches, each with a bounding box, its own band, a reason, and a landmark on each bank (asserted): the Chattahoochee (GA over AL, Florida corner to West Point — Columbus GA, West Point GA) and Perdido Key (FL over AL — NE gives the island's western 5 km to Alabama). Tennessee, Texas, Colorado and every other stretch have no reach (Chattanooga, the Red River, Sabine, El Paso all pass on the real polygon alone). Florida's north edge corrected to the 31.000° line (and Georgia's matching vertex), Georgia's west corner corrected to the AL/GA/TN corner Tennessee's outline already used. `TEXAS_OUTLINE` keeps its vertices (it is still the RRC trust gate and the seaward fallback; the gate also requires `siteState` to say Texas, so an Oklahoma strip the outline reaches is never trusted). Interior answers unchanged (Texas golden master, coloradoGuard, georgia*, florida*, oklahoma*, siteAnalysisStateGate, layerLocationScope, stateAnswerEveryState, stateLineLandmarks, tennesseeJurisdiction, njny — all green).
**Instrument:** `test/stateLineReaches.test.js` walks every edge of every state (2.0 M probes, both sides to 0.016°) and the 0.003° grids over the AL|FL line, the AL|GA|SC lines and the Red River, requires `siteState === realStateAt` except inside a named reach or the Hudson settlement, carries a known-good arm (Columbus GA / Perdido Key must still flip) and a vacuity guard (a reach that never fires, or a scan that probed nothing, fails). Red-proved: with the old default re-enabled the file fails 7 tests (incl. Terral OK reading TX).
**Documented limit (not a regression):** within ~1.3 km of the Chattahoochee the answer can be the other bank — Phenix City AL and Valley AL read GA while Columbus GA and West Point GA read GA correctly; the two banks sit closer together than NE's error.
