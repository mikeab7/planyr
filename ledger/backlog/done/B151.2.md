`[x]` Repro (owner): Pearland, account **`0440520000010`** ("TRS 3 & 5 (AG-USE)", 15.5 AC, situs "0 DEL PAPA"). Clicking the **meat** of the parcel registers the account (selection count ticks up) but the clicked piece **does not highlight** — instead the **adjacent piece to the right** lights up. Owner clarification: *"I didn't click on a county line — it just won't let me click the parcel in the meat of the parcel."*
> **Root cause (confirmed by live endpoint probes AND a live headless-browser repro, not assumption).** `0440520000010` is a **HARRIS** parcel (HCAD + the statewide TxGIO layer agree; identical geometry), but it is **MULTIPART** — two physically separate tracts under one account: a **west tract ~6.66 AC** and an **east tract ~8.12 AC**, with a *different* parcel (`0440520000002`) between them. The geometry helpers in `arcgis.js` (`largestRingLngLat`/`featureToParcel`) deliberately returned only the **single largest ring** (the B36c "biggest |area| ring can't be a hole" pick). So a click anywhere in the parcel resolves the right account, but the highlight (`MapFinder` `handleClick`) and the planner import only ever drew/added the **east (largest) tract** → clicking the **west** tract appeared to select the neighbour, and planning the site silently **dropped ~6.66 AC** (card showed 8.12 AC, not ~15). Disproves the pasted brief's guesses: the parcel is Harris (not Brazoria/"missing county"); click coords round-trip exactly in EPSG:4326 (no SR bug — verified the geometry the app sends matches the click latlng); IDs are present and the highlight is drawn from geometry, not the ID field; the query is a true `esriSpatialRelIntersects` point-in-polygon (no nearest/buffer). The seam *does* exist (the parcel's south edge is the diagonal Harris/Brazoria line; a click just over it selects a Brazoria parcel that lives only in the statewide TxGIO layer) but that was **not** the owner's symptom — multipart was.
> **Fix (minimal + shared; branch `claude/nifty-bohr-mtz70c`).** New `outerRingsLngLat(feature)` in `lib/arcgis.js` returns **every outer-boundary ring** of a (possibly multipart) feature, classifying outers vs. holes by winding **sign** (so a separate small tract is kept even when a hole's |area| exceeds it — the B36c size trap), holes dropped, each ring opened. Wired into **all three** parcel-import paths so it's fixed everywhere, not per-symptom: (1) **map-click select** (`MapFinder.handleClick`) now highlights all parts via Leaflet **multipolygon** nesting (`[[part],[part]]`, not a hole-punch) and stores `rings`/`latlngsList`; `computeAssembly` emits **one planner parcel per part** in the shared frame (full acreage) and the cursor hit-test checks every part; (2) **in-planner identify → Add to plan** (`SitePlanner.addIdentifiedParcel`) adds every part (`ring` = largest is still kept for the jurisdiction/road straddle tests); (3) **in-planner address/account lookup → import** (`importFeature`) imports every part on one **shared** anchor so the tracts keep their true relative position. 5 new unit tests (`test/arcgis.test.js`: single ring; both tracts of a 2-part; donut drops the hole; small separate part kept despite a bigger hole; empty → []). lint **0 errors** · **230 tests** · build green; `SitePlannerApp` lazy chunk intact.
