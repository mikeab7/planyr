`[x]` **Root cause: both `AnchoredMenu` call sites passed `placement="below-left"`**, which left-aligns the panel to the ANCHOR's own left edge. The anchor is the CARET button (the narrow trailing segment of the split control) — its `left` sits deep inside the control, not at the control's own left edge, so a panel hung off `caret.left` necessarily spills out past `caret.right` (== the control's own right edge, since the caret is the last flex segment) by however much the panel is wider than the caret itself (200 − 22 = 178px, exactly the measured overhang). **Fix: `placement="below-right"`** — already an existing, tested branch of the shared pure placement math (`anchoredMenuPlacement.js`'s `placeMenu`, `left = anchorRect.right − menuW`) — which right-aligns the panel to the anchor's right edge, i.e. the control's own right edge, so the panel reads as belonging to the button that opened it. One-line change at each of the two `MapFinder.jsx` call sites (`startBlankMenuBtnRef`/`placeCompMenuBtnRef`); no change needed to the placement math itself, which already had the right primitive.
