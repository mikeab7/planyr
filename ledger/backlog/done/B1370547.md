`[x]` **FIXED THIS SESSION — and the reported cause was wrong, which is the finding.**
- **MEASURED ON A LOCAL BUILD BEFORE ANYTHING WAS CHANGED, on a fresh page, at 20 · 60 · 100 · 140 · 180 · 260 · 400px out into the right mat: EVERY ONE created a box and grew the page.** There is no horizontal reach limit and there never was.
- **WHAT ACTUALLY DECIDES IT IS THE PRESS'S HEIGHT.** At a y level with ANY line of body text the press is "beside a line" (`pressIsBesideLine`) and is forwarded to the caret instead of placing — at 40px out exactly as at 140px out. Measured: level with line 1 → 0 anchors at both 40 and 140px out; below the text at 140px out → 1 anchor. His page's text ran down to where he was clicking, so the failure tracked how far right he went only by coincidence, which is exactly why it read as a wall with no marking on it.
- **THE NEW RULE IS ONE SENTENCE SOMEBODY CAN HOLD: a press ON the white page goes to the text; a press on the grey mat AROUND the page places a note there.** Nothing invisible decides it and there is no distance in it. The forwarding is kept for presses inside the sheet, including its own left and right margins, so B1368's "click beside a short line to put the caret at its end" is untouched.
- Verify: sandbox — `ui-audit/verify-notes-free-placement.mjs` §9 drives four mat presses (left and right × level-with-a-line and below-the-text); all four create a note. **It carries a KNOWN-GOOD ARM** (DRIVER-SCROLL-IS-NOT-APP-SCROLL §6): a press ON the page beside a line must still take the caret and place nothing — without it, all four rows would pass just as happily on a build that had turned every press everywhere into a placement.
