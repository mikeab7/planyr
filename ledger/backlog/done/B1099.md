- **Repro (owner, measured).** Change an element's `cx/cy` directly in `site_elements` with rev+1 while a client has that site open → the client's live state does not change and it queues commit batches pushing its own copy back. Once torn, whichever client holds the stale copy keeps winning, forever.
- **Root cause (AUDIT-FIRST) — two independent holes, either sufficient.**
  - **(a) `atOrBelowOwnHighWater` classified by REV ARITHMETIC ALONE.** B812 (Angle 4) rightly claims any row at/below our own high-water rev as our echo — but only a row we could have WRITTEN. A genuine foreign row whose rev lands under our high-water (an out-of-band repair; a writer whose rev fell below ours after a stale refetch rolled our shadow backward) was swallowed as a self-echo. Now a row stamped with a DIFFERENT `updated_by` is never treated as our echo. **Fails open** — no `selfUid`, or no `updated_by` on the row, behaves exactly as before, so every B757/B812 guarantee is untouched.
  - **(b) "pending local wins" applied to DERIVED churn.** The B673 matrix keeps local data on canvas and re-commits it over a conflicting row. That is right for an edit the user just made by hand — but most pending ops on a bonded plan are app-DERIVED relayout output (the paving/sidewalks/parking a building edit re-fits, tagged `direct: false` since B673's NEW-1 pass). Re-pushing derived geometry over another writer's deliberate row IS the non-convergence.
- **Fix — derived churn never beats a foreign row, in BOTH halves of the loop.** In `applyRemoteRow`: when the only pending op is derived and the row is genuinely foreign (bytes we never sent, not semantically equal, a rev we never produced), the row WINS — our derived op is dropped, its bytes and rev are adopted, and it goes to the canvas as an `upsert`. In `processResults`: a DERIVED op that loses a rev race is **not re-committed** — the remote rev is adopted with our bytes as the diff baseline, so there is no re-push and no ping-pong. **A pending DIRECT user edit still wins and still toasts** — the B673 matrix is unchanged where it was right.

The 65 pre-existing `test/elementSync.test.js` cases (the whole B673/B757/B812 matrix) stay green.
