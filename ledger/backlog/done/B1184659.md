`[x]` **IMPLEMENTED AND UNIT-TESTED THIS SESSION.** Decided per the report's own "pick one, justify it": **bin the tracked site at comp PURGE, never at an ordinary comp delete.** A comp delete is soft and restorable for 30 days (`comps_soft_delete.sql`) — binning the site the moment the comp is merely deleted would leave a later Restore with nothing to reattach to, the same reasoning B1184656/NEW-1 uses to keep a binned SITE's `project_id` on its comps rather than detaching immediately. Only once the comp is genuinely gone for good is it safe to let its site go too.
