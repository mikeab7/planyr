- **Repro (owner):** click parcel A (it selects), hold Shift and click parcel B → A deselected and only B was selected, so the 2-parcel set the Merge needs never formed and Merge stayed disabled.
- **Root cause (AUDIT-FIRST — confirmed in code):** the Site Planner keeps TWO parcel-selection stores — `sel` (the ONE primary parcel, drives the props panel / move) and `combineSel` (the SET picked for the Combine/Merge tool). A plain click selects into `sel`; a Shift-click accumulated into `combineSel` **and** reset `sel` to the clicked parcel. So A — plain-clicked, thus only ever in `sel`, never in `combineSel` — was silently dropped, and Shift-clicking B just moved the single highlight to B while `combineSel` held only `[B]`. (The bug only showed on the natural *plain-click-then-Shift-click* flow; Shift-clicking BOTH parcels already worked, since both went to `combineSel`.)
- **Fix:**
  - New pure `lib/parcelSelect.js` → `extendMergeSelection(current, clickedId, { primaryId, isActive })`: **SEEDS** the set from the current single selection (`primaryId`) the first time you Shift-extend, then toggles the clicked parcel. Reuses the shared `nextSelection` reducer (`shared/markup/selection.js`, B569) for the toggle math — one source of truth, no drift between workspaces — and layers on the two Site-Planner-specific rules: the seed, and the B170 inactive-parcel guard (never ADD an inactive parcel, but an already-picked one can always be removed). 12 unit tests.
  - `SitePlanner.jsx`: a new `shiftPickParcel(id)` wrapper drives `extendMergeSelection` from `sel`; the three Shift-click entry points (canvas body `onBgDown`, boundary `startMoveParcel`, left-rail parcel row) now call it instead of the bare `toggleMerge`. `shiftPickParcel` also OWNS `sel` (the call sites no longer `setSel` after it): a click that ADDS a parcel makes it primary, but a click that REMOVES one falls `sel` back to the last remaining picked parcel (or clears) — never leaving `sel` on the just-removed parcel, which would otherwise **re-seed and resurrect** it on the next Shift-click (a defect an adversarial review pass caught + a headless resurrection-guard check now pins). A **plain** click on a parcel (boundary tap in `onUp`, unlocked press, rail row) or a genuine **tap on empty canvas** (tagged `tapEmpty`, tiny-travel-only so a pan never wipes the set) now clears `combineSel` — matching "plain click replaces the selection." `startMergePick` (the B720 button) also seeds from `sel` so pressing Merge keeps your already-selected parcel.
