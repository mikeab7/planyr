Not a LIVE-VERIFY class on its own terms — a live run against a real GIS endpoint IS the verification, the same shape `probe-statewide-parcels.mjs` already uses and is deliberately kept out of the required CI gate for the identical reason (many hosts unreachable from CI's own sandbox).

**RANKING (item 2 of the brief).** Among every candidate a single winning route returns that clears acceptance, `rankScore` prefers (a) the most recent vintage — a year in the title, or (falling back, since a title need not carry one) the service's own `editingInfo` last-edit date; (b) an organizational publisher over a personal-looking account; (c) a polygon parcel layer over a name suggesting a derived one (points/summary/roll). The chosen candidate's vintage is always recorded in the output, so a stale wire is visible rather than silent. `REJECT_TITLE_RE` is a plain substring match, deliberately NOT `\btest\b` — a service/dataset title glues words together with no separator (`ATestParcel`, the dispatch's own named Lehigh PA example) or digits directly against letters (`Tax_Parcels2018`), so a word-boundary regex misses both; a false positive here just falls through to the next candidate or route, a false negative ships a test service as authoritative. Same fix applied to `probe-statewide-parcels.mjs`'s own `FIELD_PATTERNS.parcelId`, which had the identical word-boundary gap for `APN_CHR`-style field names (only matched `_apn` at the END).

**⛔ FOUND DURING VALIDATION, NOT DESIGNED IN — the vintage check only read a year embedded in a candidate's TITLE, and Wayne County MI's best-scoring live candidate ("Parcels - MI - Wayne County") carries no year anywhere in its name but its own `editingInfo.dataLastEditDate` is 2018 — 8 years stale.** It would have shipped as Wayne's wire, replacing one bad source (the City-of-Detroit-not-the-county one already excluded) with another. `rejectCandidate` now falls back to the service's own last-edit date whenever the title carries no year (a title year still wins when both are present) — caught live, before it ever reached `counties.js`, by running the real harness against the real Tier 1 roster rather than trusting it after a clean unit-test pass alone.
