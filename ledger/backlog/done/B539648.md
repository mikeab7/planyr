`[ ]` **⛔ SHIPPED, and measured with his own test.**
- Verify: sandbox — `ui-audit/measure-notes-right-edge.mjs` runs the sweep he specified; 11 unit cases in `test/notesAnchorZoom.test.js`.
- **HIS REPORT:** *"it's not letting me expand this box out to the right… it seems like there's a wall where when I go past it, it squeezes my text box down to where it's literally one character wide. And it's on the right side of the canvas."*
- **⛔ THE CAUSE, AND HE DIAGNOSED IT HIMSELF.** When the block used to JUMP LEFT away from the click he asked for *"if it will not fit, NARROW the block to the space available — do not slide it sideways."* **Right about not sliding, wrong about narrowing with no floor.** `ANCHOR_MIN_WIDTH` was **32 px** — about two characters at the note's own text size — so a press near the margin left a few pixels of room and the box was narrowed to them. The old comment on that constant actively defended the small number: *"a narrow column at the far edge is a choice he is allowed to make."* A column nobody can read or type in is not a choice; it is the failure.
- **THE FIX, in three parts, and it mirrors what the page already did VERTICALLY.** (a) The floor is **160 px** — about twenty characters, close to the 180 default, and written down with its reasoning. (b) **`anchorExtentX`**, the horizontal twin of `anchorExtent`: past the floor the block does not narrow, **the page grows to the right and scrolls**. Its absence WAS the bug — vertically the sheet had always stretched to hold a block past the bottom, horizontally there was no equivalent, so the only way to keep a block on the sheet was to squeeze it. (c) The resize drag is **no longer capped at the page edge** — pulling right grows the page instead of stopping dead.
- **HIS TEST, VERBATIM, AND ITS RESULT.** *"place a box at increasing x across the full width in small steps and assert the stored width never drops below the floor, and that beyond the floor the scrollable canvas width increases instead."* Measured across 17 steps: rendered width **180 → 172 → 160 and never below**; the canvas held at 1232 while the box fit and then grew **1257 → 1333 → 1421 → 1509 → 1597**; the **left edge was kept at every single step**. His screenshot's case now wraps into **~2 lines instead of ~27**. Dragging the handle rightward: stored width **180 → 660**, canvas **1232 → 1257**.
- **⛔ HIS ORIGINAL ACCEPTANCE TEST IS UNTOUCHED AND STILL PASSES**, which is what makes this safe: *"assert stored left equals click x minus editor left, for EVERY step. No clamping band anywhere."* Raising the floor spends the PAGE's width, never the block's position.
- **⛔ AND A FEEDBACK LOOP I CREATED AND THEN CLOSED, recorded because it SETTLED rather than oscillated.** The first version measured the available room from `dom.clientWidth` — the very element the extent then widens. The fit narrowed a box to the room, the extent widened the editor to hold it, the next measure read the wider editor as "the room"… It converged on a stable wrong number, which is worse than failing, because it looks correct. The denominator is now the SCROLLER's width, which is the one thing in the chain a box cannot change.
- **⛔ AND THREE INSTRUMENT ERRORS BEFORE THE HARNESS COULD BE BELIEVED**, all of them TRAPS.md trap 2 (the wrong baseline): it found the scroller by an inline-style substring and matched an outer wrapper that cannot scroll; it then compared the box against the SHEET's right edge when growth is only owed past the VISIBLE CANVAS; and it started the drag case too far from the edge for a drag to need any room. All three reported correct behaviour as broken.
