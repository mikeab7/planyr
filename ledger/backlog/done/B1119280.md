DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open/⏳Verify/Done for "grid pane", "dead space", "white space" + comps, `GRID_MIN_HEIGHT`: no prior item — B850434/B1066370 are unrelated double-click reports in the same module, not this.)*
`[x]` **SHIPPED THIS SESSION.** Root cause was two compounding things: (1) the panel's own height was a FIXED pixel value (`dockHeight`, viewport-derived), so the grid's `flex: 1` always claimed every byte of leftover space in that fixed box regardless of how little content it held; (2) `GRID_MIN_HEIGHT` additionally forced a ≈5.5-row floor (218.5px) even when the actual content was 1 row. **Fix — the PANEL now owns the height budget, not the grid:** the panel's style changed from `height: dockHeight` to `height: "auto", maxHeight: dockHeight` — `dockHeight` (the resize-handle-controlled value) is now a CEILING the panel shrinks to fit under, not a size it's stretched to fill. The grid pane's own `flex: 1` became `flex: "0 1 auto"` (sizes to its real content — header + N rows — rather than claiming leftover space), and `GRID_MIN_HEIGHT` was cut from `header + 5.5 rows` (218.5px) to `header + 1 row` (79px) — the largest floor that still lets a genuine 1-row sheet render with zero blank space, per the measured case. Every OTHER child of the panel (resize handle, header, paste box, armed-row banner, the empty-state placeholder, ProblemsList, the footer) was pinned `flex: "none"` so the grid stays the ONE element that absorbs a genuine squeeze once total content exceeds the ceiling (same intent as HARDENING-28's original floor, now paid for by the panel shrinking to content instead of a floor forced onto the grid at every row count). At 12+ rows the grid's own `overflow: auto` still scrolls internally (sticky header included) rather than growing the modal past the viewport — verified live at 1191×521 with 12 rows: `panelBottom=509` inside `viewportH=521`. **Narrows, does not undo, the already-filed "grid shrinks as ProblemsList grows" concern (HARDENING-28's own comment thread)** — that class is a separate, pre-existing issue about many-rows-plus-many-notices squeeze and is unaffected in its own right; it is now bounded by the smaller 1-row floor rather than papered over by a floor that broke the common 1-3 row case this item reports.
