`[x]` **FIXED THIS SESSION, and the honest report is that the fix has two halves and one of them was mine.**
- **THE STANDING HALF:** tiptap's `focus()` calls `tr.scrollIntoView()` by default, so every press-driven path — a press beside a line, a press on a box that did not drag, and the placement itself — dragged the scroller to wherever the caret landed. On a page with a box placed far outside the column that is a long way, which is the title going off the top. Every one of those paths now declines the scroll; the caret still goes where it was put.
- **THE HALF I INTRODUCED AND THEN REMOVED:** the first draft of NEW-8 focused the editor when arming the caret. Measured on a long scrolled note: a bare press moved the view from scrollTop 500 to 138. Removed entirely.
- **AND A THIRD, FOUND WHILE PROVING THE FIRST TWO: the page scrolled sideways on EVERY LOAD.** The growth compensation treated the first application of the page's own gutter as a shift to hide, so it scrolled by exactly the gutter — leaving the sheet flush against the pane again, which is B1273296 (×3)'s defect restored one frame later and invisible except on a reload. Measured: scrollLeft 0 → 276, sheet left 544 → 268. A gutter change now re-bases rather than scrolls.
- Verify: sandbox — `verify-notes-pending-caret.mjs` §7 drives a note long enough for the pane to scroll, scrolled half way, and asserts three press kinds move it by zero. **That section carries a vacuity guard** (it fails if the pane cannot scroll at all), because a scroll defect is invisible on a page that cannot scroll — §5 and §6 both stayed green on the broken build and only this scene caught it.
