- **⛔ THE DEFAULT PASTE IS UNCHANGED, and that is stated because it was asked for.** He wanted an OPTION — *"gives you the option"* — not a new default. Ctrl+V does exactly what it did.
- **THREE MODES, Word's, by name and behaviour.** `Keep source formatting` (the default) · `Merge formatting` (**new**: drops fonts, point sizes, colours, highlights and alignment; KEEPS bold, italic, underline, links, lists, headings, so the text adopts the note's body style while staying structured) · `Keep text only` (plain, line breaks kept; **Ctrl+Shift+V**).
- **THE CONTROL.** A clipboard **badge** appears inline at the end of a paste that carried formatting; clicking it — or pressing **Ctrl**, as Word does — expands three icon buttons. Picking one **re-transforms the just-pasted RANGE in place as one undo step**; it never re-pastes from the clipboard and never touches anything outside that range. Escape, a click elsewhere or the next edit retires it. The same three, with the same glyphs, are on the document's **right-click menu**. No dialog anywhere.
- **THE ICONS ARE OURS.** Drawn from scratch — no Microsoft asset copied — in Word's silhouette language so they are readable at a glance: clipboard + paintbrush, clipboard + meeting chevrons, clipboard + a plain letter A. Inline SVG, `currentColor`, the toolbar's own 16-box and 1.7 stroke, each with its name and Word's access key (K / M / T).
- **⛔ AND ONE CLARIFICATION THAT KEEPS IT COHERENT: collapsing `&nbsp;`-only spacer paragraphs and unwrapping single-column layout tables is STRUCTURAL SANITISATION, not formatting, so it happens in `transformPasted` and applies to ALL THREE MODES including Keep Source.** That structure is broken input, not a style choice anyone made — and doing it before a mode is chosen is what stops the two getting out of step.
- **THE REAL NOTE THIS CAME FROM (project Silvestri, page "Utility"), read in the browser, not imagined.** Two whole Outlook signatures had landed **four levels deep inside one list item**: two `&nbsp;` spacer paragraphs, a right-aligned 16pt Arial name in a hard `rgb()` colour, six contact lines, then a `<table>` of five single-cell rows carrying a second person's signature. Three separate defects, fixed in three separate places:
  - **A — Backspace moved a whole chunk.** *"if I press backspace when I'm right in front of Simon Sequeira, it moves the entire… it was like a whole chunk that moves to the left, not just Simon's name. It also moved the line above it to the left. It makes no sense."* It was right: that paragraph was right-aligned among unaligned siblings, so Backspace at position 0 ran `joinBackward` and MERGED it into the spacer above — one keypress, a multi-block restructure, and both lines reflow. **`lib/notesBlockKeys.js`** now undoes the formatting difference FIRST (one predictable step, which is Word's own behaviour) and leaves the join to a second press, by which point the join is visible rather than a surprise.
  - **B — the paste nested inside the list item.** A multi-block payload that is not itself a list now goes **after the list**, never inside the item.
  - **C — spacers and layout tables survived.** Both are now removed on every paste (see the clarification above).
