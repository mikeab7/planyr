**THE SEPARATOR IS TRACKED, NOT GUESSED, and that is worth one line because the obvious rules both fail.** Appending with no space is right after a generated suffix (`Parcel 1A` → `Parcel 1A1`) and wrong after a typed name (`The Wooded Half` → `The Wooded Half 1`).
