- **THE OWNER, verbatim:** *"sometimes when I intend to just click on something to select it, it actually also moves it, like, a couple feet or, like, a pixel or two just because my click is too slow even though, like, to me, it feels like an instantaneous click, and it shouldn't move. Maybe I hold it for a couple milliseconds, and it accidentally moves the element. And I'd like it to stop doing that."*
- **HIS DIAGNOSIS WAS RIGHT, ALL FOUR PARTS.** (1) `SitePlanner.jsx`'s `move` branch computed `dx = fp.x - d.fx` and wrote new positions on the FIRST pointermove — no travel test anywhere. (2) The app already had the constants and the pattern: `PARCEL_CLICK_SLOP_PX` / `PARCEL_CLICK_MS`, used four times (B310 · B735 · B383 · the tap-to-clear), **every one of them on the `pan` branch**. (3) `pushHistory()` fired on pointer DOWN — see B295009. (4) The size: with snap on, the branch runs an ambient `edgeSnapCenter` at up to a **20 ft** tolerance, so one pixel of tremor enters the move path and the flush-snap then aligns the element to a neighbour's edge. That is the "couple of feet", and it is why it is intermittent — it only bites when a neighbour edge is inside tolerance.
- **THE FIX IS ONE GATE, APPLIED ABOVE EVERY BRANCH — not a patch on the branch he named.** `lib/dragGate.js` + `startGate(e)` spread into every `drag.current` that moves existing geometry; the test runs once at the top of `onMove`, so a press that has not travelled past the slop returns before any branch and writes **nothing at all** — no geometry, no history, no row write. **28 drag starts** are gated: element and parcel moves, group moves, markup move/resize/rotate, callout move + width, measurement move + chip, the acreage chip, the dimension slide, all five vertex layers, element resize / edge-resize / rotate, road ends and road vertices, the underlay, sheet-overlay move / scale / rotate, and the print frame. `pan` / `draw` / `mkDraw` / `marquee` are deliberately UNGATED and named in the guard, so the exemption is a decision on the record: a slop gate would change what a click-to-place draw or a tap-to-clear pan MEANS.
- **⛔ TRAVEL ONLY, AND THE MODULE HAS NO CLOCK — his second clause, and he was right that copying the pan test verbatim ships the mirror-image bug.** A slow, deliberate press that never moves is still a click; a fast flick that travels far is a drag however brief. `test/dragGate.test.js` fails if `dragGate.js` ever reaches for `Date.now` / `performance.now` / `timeStamp` / `setTimeout`, and asserts a gate held for 5,000 moves at zero travel is still a click.
- **⛔ THE NO-JUMP CLAUSE SPLIT IN TWO, AND THAT IS THE ONE THING THE OWNER'S SPEC COULD NOT HAVE KNOWN.** Rebasing the pointer by the swallowed travel is right for a MOVE (it carries a grab offset, so nothing downstream can tell) and **WRONG for a drag that AIMS AT A POINT**: a vertex written to `snapPt(fp)` then trails the cursor for the whole gesture, and a road endpoint released on another road's endpoint lands **outside the snap-and-connect magnet and never welds**. Caught by `e2e/road-connect-radius`, not by reasoning. So the rebase is opt-out and the seven point drags (`vertex` · `elVertex` · `measureVertex` · `mkVertex` · `easeVertex` · `roadVtx` · `roadEnd`) pass `{ rebase: false }` — their only "jump" is the one that design has always had, five pixels of travel later than before. Resize / rotate / scale keep the rebase: they aim at a dimension, so starting from the current size is what reads as no jump there.
- **RELEASE PATHS ARE GATED TOO** (`dragArmed(d)`): a plain click on a resize handle no longer prunes a stranded apron, a click on a road vertex no longer welds a junction or flashes a civil-radius warning about a curve nobody touched, and a click on a reshaped building's vertex no longer re-derives its dock frame.
- **AUDITED BEYOND THE PLANNER, as instructed.** Doc Review's markup move **already had** a travel gate (B293, 3 px, travel-only — the pattern the planner never got, which is itself evidence for the diagnosis); its missing half, the arm rebase, is added here so a Review markup no longer hops when its drag begins.
- **PROOF, both directions.** `test/dragGate.test.js` (30) — the pure rule plus a wiring guard that reads every `drag.current` in the planner; **mutation-proven**: removing the gate from the element move, and restoring `pushHistory()` on the press, each take it red (the first version of the history guard passed on a broken build and was rewritten because of it). `e2e/click-never-moves.spec.js` (4) drives the real app logged out — **all four proven red on the pre-fix build**, where the tremor click moved the building **cy 771.43 → 774.29 ft** and the slow press moved it **cx 1028.57 → 1034.28 / cy 771.43 → 774.29**.
