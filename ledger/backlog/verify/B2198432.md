`[x]` **IMPLEMENTED, SANDBOX-VERIFIED; live signed-in pass parked as V1611264.** New pure `lib/easementLabelPlacement.js` (`placeEasementLabel`) decides where the name and the selected-state area line sit; `SitePlanner.jsx`'s easement render now draws them in one `<g translate rotate scale(labelK)>` instead of a bare horizontal `<text>` at the centroid. Rules, as the owner stated them:
- **On the easement, along its long axis, rotated to it.** Strip easements (centerline / parcel-edge) orient on the **longest straight run of the centerline** (a bent / L-shaped strip is labelled on its longest leg — NOT along the path; the label engine has no along-path text for a name); a drawn boundary easement orients on its ring's longest edge. A one-sided parcel-edge strip is anchored on the middle of the STRIP, not on the parcel line.
- **Always upright:** the on-screen bearing is folded into [-90, 90) by modulo (`uprightDeg`), so a 120° strip reads at −60° and a due-west one at 0°, never 360°. The angle is the SCREEN bearing (taken through `f2p`), so it cannot disagree with the picture whatever the y convention.
- **Fits inside the strip width:** the font shrinks from the existing zoom ramp toward the existing floor (`basePx × DIM_FONT_MIN_SCALE`), then the area line is dropped, and only when even the bare name cannot fit at the floor does it keep the floor size and overhang (a ribbon thinner than a line of type has nowhere to put it). The name is still hidden when it cannot fit the run's LENGTH (B435536's rule, now asked of the run, not the bbox).
- **Short, wide easements are unchanged** — a shape under 1.5:1 keeps the old horizontal-at-centroid label byte for byte (same `nameDy 0` / area `+12`).
- Styling (font weight, halo, colour, the 9-px area line) untouched. Other polygon labels (parcels, buildings, ponds, encumbrances) are NOT touched — the shared label code was not the clean fix, so this stayed easement-only.
- **PDF-PARITY:** the export clones the live SVG, and decisions are made at `labelPpf` / scaled by `labelK` exactly as before, so the sheet prints the same rotated label; the live pass checks it.
- **Deliberate small change:** the selected-state area line now travels WITH the name (it used to render even when the name was hidden by the fit gate). A selected easement too small for its name no longer shows an area readout on the canvas; the Properties panel still carries it.
- Guards: repo-root `test/easementLabelPlacement.test.js` (29 — vertical, horizontal, 30°, 60°, 120° flip, −30°, y-flipped projection, L-shape, short-wide ×2, all eight easement types, boundary + parcel-edge modes, narrow-strip shrink/drop-area/floor, plus a source guard on the render); e2e `easement-label-orient` (real browser, seeded vertical + 30° + 120° strips, every corner of every label `<text>` asserted inside its own strip via `isPointInFill`; 3/3 green, 3/3 RED on the pre-fix `SitePlanner.jsx`). `easement-label-fit` (B435536) still green — and was repaired (it was stuck on the Dashboard, which the app now opens on).
