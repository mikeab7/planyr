- **What was wrong, concretely.** The area label was ONE run-on line — `250,000 sf · 5.74 ac · 2,100′ perim` — three unrelated quantities at identical weight separated by middots, painted as raw haloed text at the polygon centroid. Nothing was dominant; both area units competed when only one matters at any scale; an abbreviation carried the headline's emphasis; and haloed text on photography is the least legible option available.
- **(a) One dominant value, the detail subordinate.** Line 1 is the headline in large tabular figures, line 2 the supporting detail in smaller muted text, with the user's own label riding ABOVE as a name line — never concatenated into the number.
- **(b) The headline UNIT is chosen by magnitude.** Below one acre it leads in square feet with acres demoted; at or above, it leads in acres with square feet demoted. Never both at equal weight. One acre exactly is the flip point — a fuzzed threshold would only make it unpredictable.
- **(c) A real chip, not haloed text.** A rounded plate with a hairline keyline in the measurement's own colour, carrying `data-print-chip="measure"` — the parcel acreage chip's contract. `exportSheet`'s restyle now selects on the ATTRIBUTE rather than one of its values, so both chip kinds go through ONE print treatment and cannot drift: the plate is dropped on paper, the text becomes dark haloed exhibit annotation, and the detail line stays subordinate (`data-chip-sub`).
- **(d) Per-edge segment lengths, ON the edges** of a polygon or polyline, upright at any bearing, gated separately from the summary chip on the shared `detailLabelVisible` detail tier — the same gate every other width/length callout on the drawing already uses, so this is one convention rather than a new hand-tuned number. Skipped for a two-point distance, whose one segment IS the headline.
- **(e) Collision-aware and draggable.** The chip is laid out by `labelLayout.layoutLabels` — placed FIRST (a measurement is deliberate), then its boxes seeded as obstacles so element labels yield around it exactly as they do around a parcel badge. It shortens under collision (the detail line drops) but is **never hidden**: a number the user asked for is shortened, not silently dropped. Dragging it stores a `labelOffset` in feet on the measurement and draws a leader back to the anchor — the parcel chip's mechanic.
- **(f) One number convention everywhere.** Tabular figures, thousands separators, acres to two decimals, feet and square feet to zero, and the prime mark as the single feet notation — the app's convention on the rest of the drawing. `perim` is gone; "perimeter" is spelled out and subordinate. The properties panel reads the same formatters as the canvas, so panel, screen and paper cannot disagree.
- **(g) Length and count get the same treatment** — headline the distance or the count, breakdown underneath (`2 segments` · `items`).
- **A REAL BUG THE LIVE HARNESS CAUGHT, worth recording.** An area's grab layer is a transparent FILLED polygon over the whole shape. The chip was painted BEFORE it, so every press over the chip landed on the grab layer and moved the measurement instead of the label — the drag was completely dead. Found by the headless drag check, not by any unit test; fixed by painting the chip last, and guarded by an ordering assertion in `test/measureWiring.test.js`.
