**Shipped.** A drive-in ramp is ONE building-attached element (`type: "driveInRamp"`, `attachedTo` the building, `noLabel`, a `ramp` record `{ side, at, width, gradePct, riseIn?, layout, dir, door, wings, bollards }`) — modelled the way a dog-ear bump-out is, so it moves / rotates / copies / deletes / undoes with its building through the cascade that already exists (`killSetWithChildren`, `assemblyOf`, the history snapshots). Every rule is pure in `lib/driveInRamp.js`: **length = rise ÷ grade** (4 ft at 8 % = 50 ft; 6 % = 66.7 ft; 10 % = 40 ft); **rise** defaults to the PLAN's dock height (`settings.floodMitigation.dockDropFt` × 12 = 48 in — the building has no dock-height property of its own, so the plan-level dock drop, the one number the grading layer already reads, IS the default; `riseIn` ABSENT = follow it, present = the owner's override); **straight-in** = width × length perpendicular to the wall starting at the face, **along the wall** = length × width hugging the face with the door at the high end and a `dir` (left/right) — turning it along keeps the footprint ON the wall (`fitAlongTheWall` slides the door position in and points it at the nearer end); **door modules**: the door centre snaps to a dock-door centre or the midpoint between two (so a 16-ft ramp centred between two doors takes TWO, centred on one takes ONE); **dock-door displacement** is the one `dockDoorRun` (6th argument, the ramp spans) so the canvas, the Properties door count and the "· N drive-in" line cannot disagree; a ramp on a **non-dock wall** is a door MARKER only (no ramp, says so). **Flags** (the amber style): grade over 10 %, straight-in longer than the truck-court depth in front of it, footprint crossing another element or the setback, runs past the end of the wall, non-dock wall. The box (`cx/cy/w/h/rot`) is a DERIVED cache of `rampBox(host, ramp)`, re-fitted by `refitRamps` at the planner's ONE element-write seam (`setEls`, beside `followDrives`) and when the plan's dock height changes, so a moved / rotated / resized host carries its ramps; undo/redo restore exact geometry through `setElsRaw` and are never re-interpreted. The ramp hides with the Buildings View group (no row of its own); a bonded-wall scan that would have treated it as a wall kid (the "maxOut" stack placement, the Docks & walls strip) now skips it.
