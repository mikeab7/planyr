### B2566548 — NEW-5: the grey ramp buttons sit exactly on top of the grading corner tags — every edit button now lies OUTSIDE the walls, every corner tag INSIDE the footprint `[Site Planner / building chrome + grading paint]` (bug, amends B2566545 and B2571041) #site-planner #ui #grading  *(owner live check 2026-10-30 ~1:50 PM CT, Cowork on Michael's account, throwaway duplicate of Georgia Test Site › Concept A, build 0b16d5d, the building selected at the 250-ft scale: PR #2735 had moved the cut/fill tags off the purple "+" buttons, and PR #2736 then placed the grey ramp buttons on the very spot the tags moved to — "cu⊘.1", "fil⊘4.0", "fill⊘7.9": "the overlap moved, it didn't go away." Also a second grey ramp button near each wall end beyond the one by the corner — one more than the brief asked for. DEDUPE-FIRST: this is the recurrence of B2571041's symptom through B2566545's buttons; filed as its own item because it amends both rules at once.)*
**Cause (measured, not guessed):** the buttons sat INSIDE the footprint (24 px in from the wall's middle, 20 px in from each corner) and the tags were placed by DODGING them — so any new button was a new thing to dodge, and the dodge landed on it. The grey ramp "+" was placed 32 px along the wall from the pair and 20 px from the purple corner "+" — exactly where the slid tag went. The extra buttons were the short non-dock end walls' door-marker "+", which I had added beside the green pair.
**Fix — by construction, not by nudging:** `lib/buildingButtons.js` decides EVERY button position. Each lies OUTSIDE the building's footprint, offset perpendicular from its wall into the court, in ONE row along the wall with a fixed gap: the "+ / −" pair on the wall's middle, the purple corner "+" at each end of a dock wall, ONE grey ramp "+" per DOCK wall beside the pair, ONE beside each purple corner "+" toward the middle; NONE on a short end wall (a ramp is a dock-wall feature; the door-marker case is reached only when a ramp's wall stops being a dock wall). A button that would touch one already placed is dropped, never moved (the pair and the purple "+" outrank the ramp). `lib/padCornerTag.js` places every corner tag ENTIRELY INSIDE the footprint (`boxInsideQuad`, 2 px from every wall line), as near its corner as it fits, with a leader + dot to the exact corner when it sits away from it — selected or not, so there is no state in which a tag meets a button: a box inside every wall's half-plane cannot intersect a disc outside one of them. The pre-#2735 anchors (`bumpButtonAnchor` / `sideButtonAnchor`, 20 / 24 px) are gone and `SitePlanner.jsx` `sideAddNodes` draws what `layoutBuildingButtons(wallsFromQuad(...))` hands it. Because the buttons are now outside the walls, a building that is already hovered (nothing selected) stays hovered out to them (`HOVER_BUTTON_REACH_PX`), or they would vanish as the pointer set out toward one.
**Tests:** `test/padCornerTag.test.js` (rewritten — both PRs now agree on one rule): for every building × rotation × zoom × dock set × corner × text, ZERO intersection between the union of button discs and the union of tag boxes (> 2,000 cases), every disc outside the footprint, every tag inside it, exactly one pair per wall / one ramp per dock wall / one per corner button, none on an end wall, no two discs touching, the owner's 1,566 × 650 ft building at every zoom the buttons render; RED PROOF: the same tags DO collide with buttons laid on the INSIDE of the walls. Real browser: `ui-audit/verify-grading-corner-tags.mjs` (32 checks; known-bad arm `?buttons=inside` collides, VOID otherwise) → PNGs `ui-audit/screenshots/grading/corner-tags-250ft.png`, `corner-tags-50ft.png` (+ south-end and whole-building); `e2e/drive-in-ramp.spec.js` and `ui-audit/verify-drive-in-ramp-live.mjs` now assert exactly six ramp buttons on a cross-dock building and every button outside the footprint.
