- Origin: filed 2026-07-29 from chat; shipped the same session.
- **The finding.** The Cowork browser tab on planyr.io reports `document.visibilityState === "hidden"`, and Chrome SUSPENDS `requestAnimationFrame` in that state. Confirmed directly: sampler installed, six real drag gestures executed, `window.__frames.length` was **0**; a 1500 ms idle sample also returned **0**. A screenshot does not foreground the tab. Sample counts wandering **1525 → 316 → 0** across otherwise-similar gesture runs are the signature of intermittent throttling, not of a performance change.
- **What that invalidates.** The pre-fix baseline (median 20 / p90 80 / p99 140 / max 319.9 ms over 1525 frames) and the later post-fix sample (median 20 / p90 120 over 316 frames) both came from that surface, and `frameMedianMs` / `frameP90Ms` were seeded from them. **Seed WITHDRAWN**, in the file and in the docs, with the old numbers and the reason kept on the record so nobody re-seeds from the same place.
- **Re-seeded from the right instrument.** `perf-harness.mjs` headless, reference scenario, 3 reps: median **16.7 / 16.7 / 16.7 ms**, p90 **16.7 / 16.7 / 16.7**, 145–171 samples at **59.1–59.6 fps**, tab `visible`. Ceilings 24 → **25** (median) and 96 → **40** (p90) — p90 keeps real headroom because it is the metric a single scheduler hiccup moves most, and a ceiling that trips on machine noise teaches people to re-run until green. **SCOPE stated on both metrics:** the reference scene is LIGHTER than Sylvestri, so these guard the reference scene, not production.
- **The guard that stops it happening again (the important part).** The dangerous case is not the zero — it is the MIDDLE of the range, where a partly throttled run yields a plausible-looking median from a starved sample, which is exactly how a bad ceiling gets committed. New pure `ui-audit/lib/frameSampling.mjs`: a frame figure is reported ONLY if the tab is `visible` AND the observed rate clears a 30 fps plausibility floor (deliberately well under 60 — a genuinely slow frame is the thing we are trying to measure). Otherwise the harness prints `NOT REPORTED (measurement invalid)` with the specific reason and reports no median at all. 6 unit cases in `test/perfBudgets.test.js` pin it, including the exact live readings (0 frames from a hidden tab; 316 where ~1500 were due) and a case proving a genuinely bad 20 ms median is still measurable.
- **Recorded where it will be read.** `docs/perf/PERF-BUDGETS.md` gains blocker 4 (was "three measurement blockers", now four, three of them silent) and a "withdrawing a seed" rule; **V480(e) and V481(g) both now state that frame time is NOT verifiable from a Cowork browser session, and why**, so nobody retries it there.
- **Deliberately NOT touched:** every other metric in the file is a DOM / network / bundle / heap measurement, independent of rAF and tab visibility, so the 2026-07-28 provenance stands for all of them — as the reporter said of the V481/V482/V478/V479/V483 results.
