- **Report.** Michael: *"for signups, please let them know when verifying via email who the email will come from, I think it's Supabase."*
- **Cause.** The copy read *"Account created — check your email for a confirmation link, then sign in."* A user watching for something from planyr.io never finds the message that actually arrived, concludes the signup failed, and retries or leaves. The password-reset message (*"Password-reset email sent — check your inbox"*) had exactly the same hole and got the same treatment.
- **The fix.** One constant — `lib/authMail.js` — holds the sender, and BOTH messages are generated from it, so the copy cannot drift from what actually sends. One short line each, naming the display name AND the address (the first is what an inbox shows, the second is what a search box matches) plus the spam prompt.
- **⚠ THE SENDER COULD NOT BE READ FROM THE SANDBOX, and was not guessed.** The egress policy 403s `supabase.com` and `supabase.co` at the proxy, so no dashboard read, no Management API call and no real signup could be triggered from here; the project's auth logs (24 h) hold no mail events. What WAS established from the database through the Supabase MCP: three confirmation emails have ever been sent (2026-07-02 ×2, 2026-07-09), each confirmed 14–77 s later, to `hillwood.com` and `planyr.io` addresses. **The owner confirmed in chat (2026-07-30) that the project is on Supabase's BUILT-IN email service**, and that is what the copy names: `Supabase Auth (noreply@mail.app.supabase.io)`.
- **⚠ AND THAT IS A PRODUCTION RISK, raised loudly rather than silently patched.** Supabase's own documentation says the built-in service *"is not meant for production use"*, is rate-limited to a handful of messages per hour, and — the sharp edge — *"will refuse to deliver messages to addresses that are not part of the project's team"*, failing with **"Email address not authorized."** So today a stranger's sign-up cannot receive a confirmation at all; the three deliveries on record are all team/owner addresses, which is consistent with exactly that. **No mail provider was configured from here — that needs credentials and is the owner's call.** Filed for him in `OWNER-TODO.md` with the concrete options (Resend / Postmark / SES wired as custom SMTP, sending from a planyr.io address, which also fixes deliverability and brand trust).
- **When custom SMTP lands, two lines change** (`AUTH_SENDER_NAME` / `AUTH_SENDER_EMAIL`) and the copy follows automatically — the file says so in a ⛔ note at the top.
