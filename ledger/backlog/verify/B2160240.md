- **Reproduced, not guessed (WRONG-CASE — the fixture is the owner's scene):** the REAL Trailer tool, two rows stacked, the upper ~51′ deep and the lower ~143′, then zoom out in steps. At full zoom both rows show "53′ Trailer Parking / N trailers". Two notches out the upper row reads **"53′ Trailer Parking"** with no count; four notches out the whole label is gone — while the deeper row under it keeps both lines at every step. `e2e/trailer-row-labels.spec.js` NEW-1 asserts the count on the upper row at every step and was **red on unmodified main with exactly that string**.
- **Root cause:** a label's lines are priority-ordered with the LAST dropped first (`fitLines` + the engine's drop loop), and the trailer label authors name then count — so the engine's height cap on a shallow strip dropped the count, the one line the label exists to show. Then the strip-label rule (`hideOverflow`, B195) hid the whole label once not even one line fit across the strip. It was never the collision engine, a layer, or the dedupe key: two rows a few feet apart do not trip any of them.
- **Fix (`lib/labelLayout.js`, `lib/trailerRows.js`, `SitePlanner.jsx` label pass):** an item may name a `keepLine` — the one line that is last to go (`takeLines`; in the memo key). The trailer label sets `keepLine: 1` (the count), so a shallow row shows "N trailers" instead of the name; and that kept line standing ALONE may spill across a strip too thin for even one line (still must fit ALONG the strip and still win a free spot — the controlled overflow B195 already accepts) rather than vanish. Labels with no `keepLine` are byte-identical (asserted).
