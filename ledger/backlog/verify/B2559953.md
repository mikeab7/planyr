**WHAT WAS MEASURED (live planyr.io build 9efc47a, signed in, throwaway Olive Branch plan, `ui-audit/measure-layers-panel-hover.mjs`).** A real mouse walked the left group list (8 distinct targets, 349 pointer steps) and the right rows / chevrons, with and without Water & Sewer, Gas, Zoning and Counties & Cities on, with real scrollbars on and at 1× and 2.15× pixel ratio: **zero changes** in the bounding box of the panel, the group nav, the divider, the rows pane or any scroll ancestor; **no** scrollbar toggled; **zero** DOM mutations inside the panel; the layout-shift observer saw nothing; the composited nav strip was pixel-identical frame to frame. So the suspects in the brief are REFUTED for this machine: no layout, no scrollbar and no React churn on hover.
**What WAS found, in the code and read live.** `src/index.css` gives EVERY `<button>`, on hover, `filter: brightness(.97)` and, on press, `transform: translateY(0.5px)`. The left group list is made of buttons (the right pane's rows are label + checkbox; only their ▾ chevrons are buttons) — which is exactly the owner's "mostly the left side". Read live: a hovered group tab computes `filter: brightness(0.97)`, a pressed one `matrix(1,0,0,1,0,0.5)`. A filter makes the button its own effect surface and moves its text out of the panel's normal text rendering and back as the pointer crosses each tab; a half-pixel nudge re-snaps the glyphs; on a display at a fractional pixel ratio (his is ≈ 2.15) that is a visible tremble. **This sandbox's headless Chromium draws text greyscale in one mode only, so it cannot show that last step — said plainly: the mechanism is proven, the on-screen tremble is not reproduced here.** (`.psw-panel` already carries the same override for the same reason.)
**FIX.** The panel root carries `.layer-panel-steady`; inside it `button:hover` / `:active` are `filter: none; transform: none`, and the group tabs and row chevrons get a plain background-colour tint on hover (colour only — no size, border, weight, padding or transform).
