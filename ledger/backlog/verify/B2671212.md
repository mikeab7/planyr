### B2671212 — Roads of different widths join with a smooth TAPER instead of a notch `[Site Planner / road]` (feature) #site-planner #road #export #ui
`[~]` **⏳ Verify — MERGED as #2906 (2f488fe8, 2026-11-10); live check V2115876.** Owner report (NEW-1, 2026-11-10): where a road ends and a road of a different width begins, the two strips just overlapped — a stepped edge, and at a bend a wedge where the edges did not line up. Real case: "Concept B - Quiddity DIA" (Fort Bend, near Cane Island Pkwy), a 50 ft polyline road meeting the narrower drive past Building 1 and the two ponds (NOT edited — reproduced on fixtures). Owner chose ONLY "taper end to end" (curb returns at T joints, snap-and-merge and per-end widths were offered and not chosen — recorded as root constraint #55).
- **Rule (`lib/roadTaper.js`, pure):** exactly two roads' endpoints coincide (`TEE_COINCIDE_FT`) and their widths differ → the WIDER road's end narrows symmetrically to the narrow road's width over a length. Equal width, a tee, a 3-arm node, a loop close, a roundabout end = no taper. DERIVED from the roads (`roadTapersOf`) — move / resize / disconnect either road and it follows; nothing migrated. The only stored fact is the typed length `road.taper = { s?, e? }` on the wider road.
- **Default length: 10 ft of length per foot each edge steps in (the standard low-speed lane-width transition, L = W·S²/60 at ~25 mph), never under 20 ft, clamped to the wide road's last drawn segment and then to the straight stretch that reaches the end (a taper never runs round a fillet); both ends of one road clamp to its length.** Editable in the road Properties "Taper" section (its own collapse, one line per tapered end, blank = automatic), present only on a road with a tapered joint — nothing crowds the cross-section button.
- **Seam:** the taper rides the per-road `trim` object (`trim.taper`) that `siteGeometry.roundaboutsForSite` already hands every geometry consumer, so `roadStripRing` (tapered ring = body ∪ end polygons, one outline), `roadCurbLines` (face-of-curb lines taper), the paved-area number, the dissolved network and the PDF/print clone read ONE answer. ROW lines taper when BOTH roads designate a ROW (a narrow road with none leaves the wide road's ROW line alone — nothing to meet); a designed section's band edges scale by the same ratio, lane seams are clipped to the tapered strip. Angled joint: `weldJunctionsOf` uses `taperJointCover` (outside corner mitred to the repo's limit 2, bevelled past it; the inside corner is the two strips overlapping and is never filled). No bend-flagging warning anywhere.
- **Known limits, said plainly:** an outside-curb band of a designed section tapers by ratio, not by the narrow road's own section; ROW lines on an angled joint still end at each road's own end (no corner join — unchanged behaviour); `elToRingFeet` (map-overview / KMZ single-element outline) stays junction-unaware by design.
- **Sandbox-verified:** repo-root `test/roadTaper.test.js` (26: orientation matrix, straight + 8 angled joints incl. a 100° turn with a wedge-area check, both-ends, clamp, typed length, equal widths byte-identical, live move / width change / disconnect, save + reload, ROW) — **24 of 26 RED on an untouched main checkout** (the 2 green are the equal-width controls); e2e `road-taper.spec.js` in the real planner (outline narrows monotonically 50→24 with no step, Properties "Taper" field, typed 60 redraws at once and is the only stored field, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y one frame, survives reload).
