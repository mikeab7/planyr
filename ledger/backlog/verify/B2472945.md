### B2472945 — The drawn "ETJ" map layer painted the publishers' overlap: Houston's ETJ polygon was drawn over ground inside Sugar Land's / Humble's limits. Now net of city limits, the same rule as the header `[Site Planner / jurisdiction layers]` (bug) #site-planner #gis #testing  *(found while fixing B2472944, 2026-10-28; owner said ship it 2026-10-29: "the ETJ map layer should not shade another city's ETJ over ground inside a city's limits, same rule you just shipped for the header, reusing your net-of-city-limits clip so the map and header pull from one source of truth.")*
**Cause:** the Layers panel's ETJ row drew the publishers' polygons as published (`jur_etj`, several services merged), so on Concept A / Will Clayton the dotted Houston outline ran through ground the header (B2472944) correctly calls city limits. Measured in-browser on the OLD layer code, the layer's own hit-test found ETJ painted at the overlap band, the limits corner of the straddle, and Humble's centre; the drawn Houston ETJ overlapped Sugar Land's limits by **754 acres** in the test window.
**Fix:** `jur_etj`'s query declares `netAgainst: "jur_city"`; `vectorLayers.fetchCached` cuts the ETJ by the CITY layer's own cached limits (`fetchCached` on the `jur_city` source — one source of truth for what a city's limits are, read through its own cache entry) with `etjNet.netEtjFeatures` → `jurisdictionShare.netPolysToEsriRings` (the SAME clipper subtraction the header uses, `subtractNorm`), before the result is cached/drawn. Cache key `keyRev: 3`. Slivers: the two layers are generalised independently at the zoom tier, so a ribbon of "ETJ" would remain along every shared border; the result is opened by half the tier's generalisation distance (`minWidthM`) — real ETJ strips narrower than that tier's generalisation are not drawn at that zoom (≈330 m at the widest tier, ≈11 m at parcel zoom). An ETJ clear of every city is returned untouched (same object). SB 2038 release areas are left alone. If the city layer cannot answer, the ETJ pull FAILS loudly (never an un-netted ETJ cached as netted). `out.netted` rides the cached payload. PDF-PARITY: the export's vector branch reads the live painted layer's GeoJSON (guarded by test), so the sheet prints the netted ETJ.
**Found on the way, fixed in the same change (it would have regressed click-to-identify on this layer):** `vectorLayers.hitFeature`'s `inPolygon` treated ring 0 of an Esri ring list as the outer and every other ring as a hole, so ANY multi-part feature (the publisher's own 12-part Sugar Land ETJ, and the netted Houston ETJ with 23 rings) answered nothing on a click over ground the map painted. It is now even-odd across all rings — the rule the map draws by — identical for a well-formed outer + holes.
**Adjacent cases (real browser, real services, plans seeded):** Sugar Land / Houston overlap band → no ETJ painted (city layer still answers); Concept A's origin → none; genuine straddle → ETJ painted on the outside part, none on the limits part; plain Houston ETJ ground → painted; Richmond's own ETJ → painted; plain unincorporated Fort Bend → none; Humble limits (Will Clayton's pattern) → none, plain Houston ETJ near it → painted.
