- **What was actually wrong.** Driven headless, three clicks DID store three control points with a rounded corner at the turn — the drawing mechanic was never broken. What was missing was any way to say "done": the draft rendered its centerline, its provisional strip and a running length, and **nothing named the finish action**. Enter and double-click both worked but were undiscoverable, and **Esc — the instinctive "I'm finished" key — deletes the whole draft.** So the owner's experience was three clicks and then a dead end, which reads exactly as "I can't draw a three-point road."
- **Fix:** the last placed point now carries an on-canvas **✓ Done** control (`data-testid="road-draft-finish"`) with "or press Enter" under it and a tooltip naming Esc/Backspace; a single-point draft prompts "Click the next point". The control takes pointer events inside the otherwise inert preview group, and finishing commits through the same `finishRoad` path as Enter — so weld/tee/drive resolution on the final point is unchanged.
- **Sandbox-verified:** `e2e/road-corner-selffix.spec.js` draws with exactly three clicks, asserts the finish control is present (its ABSENCE is the reported bug), clicks it, and asserts three stored points with an `arc` corner. Harness `ui-audit/diagnose-three-click-road.mjs` finishes by clicking the chip, no keyboard.
- **Live-verify (V459):** on his own plan, three clicks and one Done click produce the road he expected.
