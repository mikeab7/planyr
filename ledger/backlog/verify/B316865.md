- **THE FIX IS AT THE RESOLVER, ONCE.** `onChipContext` asks `resolveDoubleClickTarget(hitStackAt(x, y))` and forwards to the real target through a new `featureContextAction` — the right-click twin of `featureDoubleAction`, ONE decision covering all five families (el · markup · callout · measure · parcel), so the next chrome of this shape is closed too.
