### B2431297 — NEW-2: moving an area measurement in Bluebeam asks to set a scale, then its fill turns solid purple and hides the plan `[Site Planner / print-export]` (bug) #site-planner #export #markup  *(owner report 2026-10-21, Bluebeam, screenshot; amends B2384528 / B2143584.)*
`[ ]` **⏳ merged — V1864240 PENDING (Verify: live — Bluebeam behaviour; the session arm is the dictionary + proxy, the Bluebeam arm is Michael's).** After moving or reshaping a measurement in Bluebeam: no scale prompt, never an opaque block over the plan.
- **Scale prompt — fix:** the page now carries ONE `/VP` viewport (`/BBox` = the plan window, `/Name (Plan)`, `/Measure` = the sheet's scale in feet, areas in sq ft — the same dictionary every measurement annotation carries), written only when a measurement is on the page and the scale is known (`pdfAnnotations.viewportEntry`, `imagePdf.jpegToPdf({viewport})`, `exportSheet`). Per-annotation `/Measure` is kept. **Which of the two Bluebeam honours is unknown** (no Bluebeam here, no public spec for it) — both are written so either works.
- **Fill — what was actually wrong, from the owner's dump of what we wrote:** the area measurement was `/C` = `/IC` = the purple, `/CA 0.1`. Bluebeam rebuilds a measurement from its dictionary, paints `/IC` and does NOT read `/CA` as the fill's opacity (its UI has Line opacity and Fill opacity as separate settings; it stores fill opacity somewhere this repo cannot see — I found no documented key and did not invent one). So a stored `/IC` is an opaque block after any rebuild. **Fix:** a translucent measurement fill is written to the appearance only (what shows until the viewer rebuilds); the dictionary carries the outline (`/C`, `/CA 1`) and `/Measure`, so a rebuilt measurement is outline + value, never a block. The cost is real and stated: after an edit in Bluebeam the translucent fill is gone (he can set Bluebeam's own Fill + Fill opacity); the export's warning list says so. Opaque fills (≥ 0.95) and fills with no `/Measure` (nothing rebuilds them) keep `/IC` + `/CA` as before.
- **Not changed, on evidence:** buildings and other elements (opaque `/IC`, `/CA 1`; `/IC` is the footprint's own fill colour, asserted equal to the appearance's `rg`), plain markups (translucent `/IC` + `/CA`) — he moves these today without trouble, and only measurements (`/IT` + `/Measure`) are rebuilt on a move.
- **Measured:** MuPDF move + `update()` proxy on the real export: 50/50 annotations move, paint at the new place, leave nothing behind; the rebuilt area's interior heavy-ink share is 0.000 (an opaque fill reads ~1.0); `pdf_regen_compare` 13/13 within limits.
