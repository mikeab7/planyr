- **THE HONESTY FIX THIS ROUND FORCED — the watershed-truncation guard.** A DEM grid is a finite window; a creek crossing a 64-acre tract drains land for MILES upstream. Accumulating flow over the site envelope alone would have returned a few acres and produced a confidently UNDERSTATED discharge, and therefore an understated flood elevation — worse than no number, and precisely the failure this feature exists to prevent. Two changes: the watershed is delineated over a **WIDE, COARSE** request through the same `fetchSiteGrid`/`demGrid` sampler (~12 × 12 miles at ~200-ft cells — delineation wants REACH, the section wants RESOLUTION), and `channelSection.upstreamEdgeFlags` propagates a "my upstream area reaches the grid border" flag downstream so a basin that still runs past the window returns an **explicit UNKNOWN naming the truncation**, never an elevation derived from a fraction of the real basin.
