- *Why an index:* scanning the roll per request measured 43 s cold and Cloudflare error 1102 on deep accounts (the Worker's CPU budget is ~3 s; DEFLATE cannot be entered mid-stream; a Worker's clock is frozen during computation). - *Complete-or-hidden:* a unit with no published rate, or one that has not adopted the year's rate yet, makes the answer incomplete → hidden (and falls back to the previous roll year).
