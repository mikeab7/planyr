- `BaytownGIS.SDE.BaytownParcels` is layer 13 of the same city service. The owner's point is sound — a city's own parcel layer is usually fresher than a regional harvest, and he works Goose Creek heavily.
- **Why it was not swapped in blind:** parcels are a different subsystem from jurisdiction (the nightly snapshot builder, `parcelTruncation`, the situs ladder, county routing, the `sharedLayerUrlConflicts` health policy). And the specific trap on THIS site: Goose Creek spans Baytown AND unincorporated Harris County, so a city-only parcel source would leave the unincorporated two-thirds — the majority of the site — with no parcels at all. It has to be an ADDITIVE per-area source, not a replacement.
- **Stopping rule:** closes when a Baytown-area site draws parcels from the city layer where it covers and from HCAD elsewhere, with the situs ladder and truncation guard proven on both, or when a measured freshness comparison shows the city layer is not actually ahead.
