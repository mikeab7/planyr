`[x]` **FIX, two parts.** (1) `legShares` (new, `roadGeometry.js`) replaces `cornerShares` at every interior-corner call site (`roadCenterlineTagged`, `roadCornerRadii` — now accepting `sharpAt`, and `fixRoadRadii`): a shared leg is split by what each end actually NEEDS (its unclamped tangent run `T = R·tan(θ/2)`; zero for a sharp or straight-through/tee vertex; the legacy midpoint half for a `smooth` vertex, whose anchor is a midpoint by construction). If both neighbours' needs fit inside the leg, each corner may take everything the other does not need; if they don't fit, the leg splits pro rata; end legs stay wholly available exactly as before. (2) `armStraightRun` (new, `roadJunctions.js`) replaces the raw `roadRunFrom` polyline distance at every junction-arm call site (`driveJunctionsOf`'s side run, `teeJunctionsOf`'s side/back/forward runs): it subtracts the neighbouring corner's own rendered tangent length (via `roadCornerRadii`) from the naive polyline run, so a curb return's straight approach ends at the corner's arc ENTRY rather than running through it. `teeNodeIndicesOf` (new) marks every vertex where another road tees onto a road as a hard corner (`sharpAt`) for both of the above, since a tee node takes no tangent.
`[x]` **Unit coverage.** `test/roadCornerFit.test.js` updated: the old "still halves that leg when a further corner shares it" case (asserting a flat 30 ft half-share) is replaced with the need-based pro-rata split (60·60/70 vs 10·60/70, tangents proven never to overlap) and a new case proving a corner next to a `sharpAt` vertex gets the whole leg. `test/roadPadSubtract.test.js` adds `armStraightRun` coverage (an arc-entry-aware interior leg, a road-END leg left alone, and a `sharpAt` neighbour charged no tangent) and `legShares` coverage (both-fit, pro-rata, and sharp-neighbour cases).
