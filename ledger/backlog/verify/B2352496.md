`[ ]` **⏳ VERIFY — V1783024 PENDING (live, signed in, after the deploy).**
- **Measured first:** a bump-out is its own element (`type "building"`, `dogEar`, `attachedTo` its host — `lib/dogEar.js`), rendered as its own `data-feature="el:<id>"` node in the clone, a SIBLING of the host's node (not a child), with `data-label-for` only on the host. The grading layer's `isPadMember` rule (PR #2388) is the same association; `bumpOutsOf` reads it from the element list at the point of use. `isBuilding` excludes `dogEar`, so the extractor never saw a bump-out as a building and left its ink in the page.
- **What ships:** `lib/buildingOutline.js` (pure) unions a host and its bump-outs into ONE outer ring, in the host's own frame so a rotated building snaps flush (measured: world-frame rounding at 33° left hair gaps and the union fell apart), with the collinear corner dropped — eight vertices for two bump-outs on one wall. `annotationExtract` makes every bump-out node a MEMBER of its host's entry: the entry sits at the first member's paint position, every member node leaves the page together, the Polygon's `hints.verts` is that outline (so `/Vertices` and `/Rect` span the bump-outs), and the appearance paints the whole shape as the flattened export does. Pinning is judged per member at its own paint position (a truck court sitting in the notch between two bump-outs does not pin the building; later content over a bump-out alone does; two buildings whose members straddle each other both stay in the page); lifted objects are written in last-member order. A bump-out that does not touch its host is drawn in the appearance, named in the degraded list and left out of the vertices — never silently dropped. Flatten ON never calls the extractor, so it is untouched.
- **Also found by the parity render, fixed:** a building's stacked label (name / SF / dimensions are `<tspan dy>` lines of one `<text>`) was read through `textContent` and ran together on one line in the editable page ("Building 1450,000 SF900′ × 500′"); `domToTree` now emits one text line per tspan at its own baseline, and the annotation's comment text reads the same lines. This predates the bump-out work (B2326896 shipped it).
