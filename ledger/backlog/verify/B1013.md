`[ ]` **⏳ merged — V458 PENDING (Verify: live — `Blocker: real-data`).** Three defects, one root.
- **Root cause — the leg-share clamp was wrong at a road's end.** `arcCorner` limits a corner's run-up to *half* the shorter adjacent leg. That half is correct only for a leg **shared with a neighbouring corner** — it is what stops two fillets overrunning each other. A leg that runs to the road's **own endpoint** has no neighbour to share with, so halving it was pure loss: on the owner's real plan his fire-lane corner drew at **11 ft against a 28 ft class minimum when its geometry could hold 22**, and B1009's flag then reported the app's own clamp as a defect in his layout. **Fixed:** new pure `cornerShares(i, n)` — full leg at a terminus, half on a shared leg — applied identically in `arcCorner`, `roadCornerRadii` and `fixRoadRadii`'s `feasR`, so the render and both measurement paths cannot disagree.
- **The mark never became a fix (owner rule: "the software should self fix").** `fixRoadRadii`'s only geometric move was a vertex **nudge toward the chord**, which on his fire lane skewed a square entry into a **chamfer no one lays out on a site plan** — and still missed the minimum. **Fixed:** new pure `fitRoadCorners` asks for the class radius outright (never writing back a clamped value as if the user had chosen it — the pathology that left a stored 22.89 ft request on a 50 ft truck route) and, where a corner is starved by a leg running to a road END, **runs that approach out along its own bearing**, bounded by `ROAD_FIX_MAX_EXTEND_FT`. Bearings are preserved, so roads still meet square. `solveRoadCorners` runs **extension first, nudge only as the fallback** — order matters, and the chamfer is why.
- **The bare "!" is gone.** The canvas flag is now a chip that names the remedy — *"needs 5′ more approach"* — with an inline **Fix** that applies it in one click and one undo; the toast says what it did in plain language. Still `data-export="skip"`.
- **Verified on the OWNER'S REAL PLAN, not a mock** (`ui-audit/fixtures/tsakiris-concept-a-live.json`): both flagged corners — the fire lane into employee parking and the truck stub into the 40′ aisle — clear on one click each, each road gaining ~5 ft of approach into pavement that already existed, with every bearing unchanged. `ui-audit/verify-corner-selffix.mjs` drives it end-to-end and exits non-zero on any leftover mark or page error.
- **Sandbox-verified:** new `test/roadCornerFit.test.js` (16 cases — shares, the neighbouring-fillet invariant that justifies the half rule, the shortfall readout, extension bounds, sharp-corner and no-op guards) + `e2e/road-corner-selffix.spec.js` asserting the chip names its remedy, clears on click, and does **not** skew the alignment. Two prior tests that encoded the half-leg clamp as truth were updated with the reason.
