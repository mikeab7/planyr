- **Report.** Owner, with a zoom on one curved corner: *"how many dots show up. I don't need that many to show up that big — it's obviously overwhelming the screen."* About twelve handles along one fillet, hiding the boundary and the aerial under them.
- **The fix — display decimation, never a geometry edit.** Handles are thinned in SCREEN space by the shared `spaceOut` helper: nothing is drawn within ~22 px of an already-drawn handle. Priority is **corner-ness** (`cornerTurns` — how hard the ring turns at that vertex), so the lot's real corners always win and the middle of a digitized arc is what drops; the **selected** vertex is pinned first, because it is the Delete-key target and may never be invisible. Re-decided every frame off the live zoom, so a vertex that clears the threshold on zoom-in comes straight back, fully draggable — the ring is never touched.
- **Smaller mark, same grab.** The square shrinks from 10 px to 8 px with a lighter keyline, but a transparent 18 px hit square sits under it, so the drag target did not shrink with the mark. Decimation guarantees ≥22 px spacing, so two hit squares can never fight over one press.
- **"Only when selected" was already true and is confirmed, not re-implemented** — the handle layer has always been gated on the parcel being selected in the Select tool; the harness now asserts it (an unselected parcel draws a clean outline and zero handles) so it stays true.
- **Applied to polygon ELEMENTS too**, not just parcels: an imported or traced pond outline carries the identical dense-vertex problem, and it is the same helper.
- **Verified here.** Unit tests for the thinning (input order preserved so indices and B912 side selection are untouched · deterministic tie-break · a 4,000-point stress case under 500 ms with no survivor pair closer than the threshold · the pinned-selection case) and the harness's live reads: 23 of 32 handles at fit, 32 when zoomed into the corner, closest pair never under the threshold at either zoom, 8 px mark. lint 0 · full suite green · build green.
