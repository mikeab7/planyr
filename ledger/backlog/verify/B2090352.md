`[x]` **Shipped, parked ⏳ Verify (live).** Root cause, measured before the fix: `mergeRings` (SitePlanner.jsx) only fused two rings when an edge of one had an exact reversed twin in the other (both endpoints within 0.75 ft). On a 200×100 lot against a neighbour: identical twin edge MERGED; neighbour shorter (its corner mid-way along the other's edge), one extra vertex on the common line, same winding, 1 ft gap, 1 ft overlap and a 1 ft slide were all REFUSED — six of seven realistic adjacencies. County data is full of the first two, hence "sometimes". **Fix:** `polyClip.mergeParcelRings` — a real clipper UNION with a morphological close (grow every ring, union, shrink back), winding- and vertex-independent. `mergeRings` is deleted; `mergeParcels` keeps `pushHistory("merge")`, tombstones, `locked:true`, 3+ collapse and pick-mode exit.
- **Tolerances (stated, chosen before measuring):** `MERGE_GAP_FT` = **1.5 ft** — the widest survey gap/overlap that still fuses; far below any street/alley/ROW (≥ 20 ft; a 40 ft street is asserted refused). The close can add at most gap × contact length to the area (0.75% on two 200×100 lots); `mergeParcelRings` enforces `growth ≤ 1.5 ft × total contact × 1.05 + 5 sf` and reports `areaSqft` / `dissolvedSqft` / `growthSqft`. The union is compared to a TRUE clipper union of the inputs, not `dissolvedParcelSqft`, whose overlap-tolerance fast path reads a 1 ft overlap as additive. `MERGE_MIN_CONTACT_FT` = **5 ft** of common boundary to count as touching (a lone corner measures ~3 ft), so corner-only neighbours stay refused.
- **Refusals stay refusals, now specific:** corner-only / clearly apart → the banner, and with 3+ picked lots it NAMES the lot(s) that don't touch the rest. A union that would enclose an unpicked lot (hole) is refused with its own message — the parcel model is single-ring, so a hole is never silently dropped. polygonSplit's own edge-exact snap is untouched; split-then-merge is asserted to round-trip to the original outline (same vertex count, ≤ 0.02 ft per vertex).
