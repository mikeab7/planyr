- Verify: **sandbox** (real keyboard + mouse in Chromium, judged on DOM focus and the stored document) **+ my own live pass on planyr.io after the deploy** (recorded below when done). Stopping rule: closes when the harness is green on the deployed build.

**Measured on unmodified main, not inferred** (`ui-audit/verify-notes-paste-menu.mjs`, 41 of 89 checks red there): the key handler opened the flyout on the Control **KEYDOWN** and never moved focus — focus stayed in the editor, so the arrow keys moved the caret / the selected box and the mouse over the menu fell through to the canvas; and because it was the keydown, **Ctrl+C, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+Shift+X, Ctrl+wheel all popped the menu**.

**Fix (Word's behaviour):** a **lone Ctrl tap** (Control down then up with nothing between — no other key, press or Ctrl+wheel; a lost window disarms too) opens the menu and **moves DOM focus into it**, onto the option applied now. Arrows / Home / End / Tab move the highlight (wrapping), **Enter / Space** pick it, **K / M / T** (the option's own letter) pick directly, hovering highlights, a click picks; **Escape** closes the menu, gives the caret back and does NOT also back the box out of editing; a pick gives the caret back and typing works at once. A press / drag / wheel / double-click / right-click on the menu is stopped natively so nothing under it pans, zooms, places a caret or opens the page menu. Ctrl+V and every other chord never open it; typing dismisses the tab; a second paste replaces the first. Same on a table paste and a paste into a table's cells. On a touch screen focus is not moved (it would drop the soft keyboard). Traps found: the editor's blur timer resized the box and **retired the offer** under the open menu (now guarded); React `stopPropagation` cannot stop the canvas's native listeners (stopped natively on the menu).
