- Verify: **live** · `Blocker: real-data` — a real recorded description with save-and-except tracts. **V96465.**
- **The fix.** "Use as parcel boundary" on both the right-click menu and the deed inspector. The promoted lot goes through the same shape a map-clicked parcel does (fresh id, Standards style stamped at birth), so acreage, setbacks, edge runs, the acreage chip and the area math work identically. It carries three things a clicked parcel does not: `source: "deed"` (provenance), `deedMisclosureFt`, and the save-and-except rings as `exceptions`, deducted from `parcelNetSqft` → `dissolvedParcelSqft`, which is the single figure every yield, coverage, detention and mitigation number is computed off.
- **An OPEN traverse is refused, loudly**, naming the gap and telling the owner to check the description or draw by hand. A boundary is not the place to guess at a missing call.
- **`skipDeedGroup` is load-bearing:** without it a promoted deed fits its own copy at a perfect 0° residual, and "Align to county parcel" would silently do nothing forever after.
- **A gap found by measurement and fixed in the same session:** once promoted, the parcel lies over the deed and the Parcel panel takes the dock, so a right-click where the deed visibly is answers with the PARCEL's menu — measured `{deedMenu:false, parcelMenu:true}`. "We keep the deed so you can still align or compare it" was true of the data and false of the product. The door back in is now on the parcel itself ("Go to the deed this came from"), which sidesteps the hit-test contest instead of trying to win it.
