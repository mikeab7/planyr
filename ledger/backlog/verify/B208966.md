- **THE HALF THAT REPRODUCED, and it reproduced completely.** The rail is one `overflow-y: auto` column and the tool list alone is taller than it at every realistic window size. Measured on the real app: **1,294 px of content in a 536–796 px rail**, at 1440×900, 1280×720 and 1512×640 alike — so the zoom block, which sat after a `spacer`, was pinned to the bottom of the SCROLL CONTENT, **539 px below the visible bottom** in the 1440×900 case, in a narrow column with no scrollbar affordance. The owner's words were exact: there is no visible zoom control anywhere in that mode.
- **THE FIX.** `ToolRail` gained a `footer` slot rendered OUTSIDE the scrolling area; the Review zoom block (readout · In · Out · Fit · Page) moved into it, so it is on screen by construction rather than by luck. "Page" is the fit-to-window reset the owner asked for and its tooltip now says so.
- **⚠ THE "WHEEL DOES NOTHING" HALF IS RETRACTED AT SOURCE, 2026-08-06 — it was never an owner observation and its method could not support it.** It was reported by the ASSISTANT, driven through browser automation, which dispatches a **synthetic scroll** rather than a real wheel event carrying the `deltaMode` and modifier state a zoom handler reads. Driven wheel events zoom correctly against the real build in every position and state tried — sheet centre, over the mat, at the viewport corner, with a drawing tool armed, with Takeoff open, after a module round-trip, and after the exact Stitch → ‹ Single sheet round-trip. **No code path was found that disables wheel zoom, and no cause was invented for one.** Recorded here so nobody re-opens a hunt for a defect that has no evidence behind it. The remaining live step is an ordinary confirmation that the owner's own wheel zooms (V10611 (d)) — not a question put to him about a measurement that was ours and was wrong. **Everything else in this item stands on its own and is real:** the control genuinely was below the fold, which is why he saw no zoom control at all, and the footer slot is the actual fix.
- **Guards.** e2e **review-sheet-rail-and-zoom**: the zoom control's box must be INSIDE the rail's visible box (the old build fails this by 539 px, named in the failure message); pressing it must make the drawing bigger; "Page" must bring it back down; and the wheel must zoom up AND back down over the canvas — a one-way "zoom" is not a zoom.
