`[ ]` The Houston ETJ was missing from the pill with no way to tell "no ETJ here" from "we could not check".
- Verify: live  → **V17707**
- Origin: filed 2026-08-06 from chat
- **`identifyJurisdiction` already recorded per-role state** in `out.sources` (`loaded` / `failed` / `empty` / `unavailable`) — and `formatJurisdictionBadge` never read it. Silence was being used to mean two opposite things.
- **Fixed:** a failed role now says so in its own slot — `ETJ · couldn't check`, `City limits · couldn't check`, `County · couldn't check` — and the badge carries `unresolvedRoles` / `unresolved` for downstream consumers. `unavailable` (no ETJ layer for this area at all) stays quiet, because that is an honest N/A rather than a failure.
- **The trap avoided while fixing it:** keying "couldn't check" on a MISSING centroid rather than on a reported failure would have printed the warning on every legacy/point-shaped caller — the same collapse in the opposite direction. It is keyed on the source state reporting `failed`, and only that.
- Audited the same failed-vs-empty collapse across the other roles: city and county are now covered by the same mechanism; ISD carries no floodplain consequence and is unchanged.
- Why this path matters: the ETJ source is **measurably flaky** — it returned 0 at three of six points in the owner's Houston sweep.
- **⚠ AMENDED 2026-08-08 (B276755) — THAT FLAKINESS WAS MISATTRIBUTED, and the correction matters more than the observation.** The ETJ source is not flaky. Its query URL was being built past the ~2 KB ceiling `services.arcgis.com` enforces, and the resulting **HTML 404 parses as an empty feature set** — so the failure was deterministic in the parcel's vertex count, not random, and it presented as `empty` rather than `failed`, which is precisely the state this item teaches the badge to stay quiet about. The honest-unknown machinery here is right; it was being handed the wrong state. Fixed on B276755; after it, 0 of 27 sites fail to resolve their ETJ.
