### B2639424 — The device store's legacy-ledger inference deleted live plan entries on devices whose mirror write was failing `[Site Planner / Persistence]` (bug) #site-planner #persistence  *(found 2026-11-03 while building B2200384, from production `client_errors`; not owner-reported. Minted **B2639424** from this branch's reserved block. DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open / ⏳ Verify / Done for `legacy-merged`, `reconcileLegacy`, `ledger`, `removed`: B2165120 built the mechanism (inv. 17 §5 "a plan the ledger says K held but K no longer does → the older tab DELETED it"); nothing records this failure.)*
`[~]` **FIXED BY DELETING THE MECHANISM (B2200384, this PR) — live proof pending → V2084096.**
- Verify: live (concurrency / multi-writer + real data: the two affected devices)
- **THE DEFECT.** `planStore.flushMirror` wrote the legacy ledger (`<store>:led`: signature + plan ids) BEFORE writing the legacy entry. On a device at the localStorage cap the legacy write is refused, so the ledger then listed plans the legacy entry never held. The next `reconcileLegacy` compared them, read "the ledger says the legacy entry held X, the legacy entry lacks X now" as "an older-build tab DELETED X", and removed X's per-plan entry (`plan-store-legacy-merged … removed:N`). It repeated on every flush.
- **MEASURED (production `client_errors`, read-only, 2026-11-03):** `plan-store-mirror-failed` ×83 from two users since 2026-10-30; `plan-store-legacy-merged` ×91, and for user c5ddcae0… ~38 events on 2026-11-02 22:43–22:46 carry `removed: 1` or `2` alongside `merged: 3` (builds 343d928, 6734b15). Whether those entries are gone from that device is NOT known from telemetry (a signed-in device re-pulls its cloud rows, which is the net) — **the owner should look at that account's plans**.
- **THE FIX.** Not patched — the ledger, the mirror and every "absence in the legacy entry means delete" rule are deleted (B2200384). The retire/re-adopt fold never deletes an entry; it recovers a plan only the legacy entry holds (unless tombstoned / in `idx.del`). Regression test: repo-root `test/planStore.test.js` "REGRESSION (production, c5ddcae0, 2026-11)" — a ledger listing 30 ids over a legacy entry holding 24 deletes nothing (the old rule removed the 6 newest).
- **Stopping rule:** closes when V2084096 passes AND production shows no `plan-store-legacy-merged` with `removed > 0` after the deploy, and the owner has been told which account to check.
