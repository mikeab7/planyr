WebP decodes fine everywhere this app ships and stays accepted. Fixed at BOTH intake paths (the drag-drop gate AND the plain file-picker, which had no gate at all — `accept="image/*"` doesn't reliably block a HEIC file's own MIME type) with a specific, named rejection message.
