- **Why.** FBC §5.02(h)(1) requires a HYDRAULICALLY EQUIVALENT (one-to-one) offset. Hydraulic equivalence is elevation-matched — water at one level cannot be stored by a hole dug lower — so Bain's 98.2-vs-97.7 total match is not evidence of compliance and would not survive review.
- **Fix.** New pure `lib/mitigationBands.js`: `bandSpans` buckets each priced grid cell's fill COLUMN (existing grade → top of fill, capped at the governing flood surface) into 1-ft bands in proportion to the height in each; `createdBands` reads the pond side straight off `pondStageModel`'s `volumeBetween` integral (so the two ledgers cannot drift) and EXCLUDES volume below the floodplain bottom from credit while still reporting it (`excludedBelowBottomCf`) — that dirt is borrow, not offset; `bandLedger` produces per-band `{lostCf, requiredCf, createdCf, netCf, pass, shortCf}` with `overallPass` as the AND over bands, plus `totalWouldPass` (what the old lump-sum method would have concluded) so the panel can say "the totals tie, the elevations do not".
- **Engine seam.** `floodplainMitigation.js` gained an opt-in `opts.bandSpans` that collects each cell's elevation span in the SAME pass that sums the volume (`gridIntersect` `opts.spanAt`), so a band can never disagree with the number it came from; `combineMitigation` carries the spans on the same fast path as the retained heat-map cells.
- **Surfaced.** A "Replaced at the right levels" row in Mitigation detail — "every level covered" or "N levels short" with the failing bands listed (elevation range, given back vs taken) and the explicit "the acre-foot totals tie — but the levels do not" line. A failing ledger also demotes a numerically-OK Mitigation verdict to SHORT.
- Tests: 6 cases incl. the proportional split of a 3.4-ft column, the totals-tie-but-bands-fail inversion, the below-bottom exclusion, the unanchored-pond and unknown-flood honesty states, and a tie-out of created bands to the pond's own duty-split integral.
