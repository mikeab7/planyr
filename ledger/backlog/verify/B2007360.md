### B2007360 — Mobile comp sheet: tapping most field rows does nothing (Deal name, Size, Clear Ht, Yr Built, Rate, Price, Notes …) `[Shared / Comps]` (bug) #comps #mobile #ui  *(Owner report 2026-10-01, iPhone Safari, planyr.io: pasted a Lease, Houston TX 77032, 322,322 SF; "some of the fields aren't editable". DEDUPE-FIRST: nearest prior is B1091712 (the mobile sheet's build) — a different defect, net-new.)*

`[x]` **Reproduced (AUDIT-FIRST) with a touch-enabled phone-width Chromium run, tapping the CENTRE of every row** (`ui-audit/verify-comp-mobile-every-field.mjs`, 55 checks). **Dead before the fix — 30 rows, i.e. every text/number/date row, filled or empty:** Lease: Deal name, Size, Clear Ht, Yr Built, Rate, OpEx, Escal, Commence, Term, Free, TI, Landlord, Tenant, Notes · Building sale: Deal name, Size, Clear Ht, Yr Built, Price, NOI, Cap, Landlord/Seller, Tenant/Buyer, Notes · Land: Deal name, Size, Price, Landlord/Seller, Tenant/Buyer, Notes. Rows that already worked: the dropdowns (Type, Unit, Per, Basis) and Location. (Notes/Price were first mis-scored by the harness — its label lookup hit the section captions of the same name; scoped by `data-field-key` after the fix. Their pre-fix state is the same code path as every other text row.)
`[x]` **Cause:** `CompEntryMobileSheet.jsx` `EditableRow` made only the right-aligned VALUE TEXT a button. An empty field is a "—" a few pixels wide, and a tap anywhere else on the row (label, blank middle) hit an inert `<div>`. Not the pager's swipe handler (there is none) and not a missing column branch.
`[x]` **Fix:** the whole row is the tap target (`role="button"`, keyboard Enter/Space too, row height unchanged); the "Today" chip stops propagation; focus moves in a layout effect so iOS raises the keyboard inside the tap; numeric rows keep the decimal keypad, `enterKeyHint="done"`. Rows carry `data-field-key` / `data-field-editor`. Edits still go through `onCommitField` → `applyCellEdit` (one source of truth with the desktop grid) — proven to survive paging to another comp and back.
`[x]` **Found along the way and fixed:** Year built displayed "1,999" (thousands separator on a year) on BOTH layouts; `noThousands` flag on the column.
`[x]` **Read-only by design, unchanged:** derived rows ($/SF, $/SF/yr) and the one of Price/NOI/Cap that the cap-rate triangle computes from the other two (desktop behaves the same; clearing one of the others frees it — the harness checks Cap that way).
