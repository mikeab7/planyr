DEDUPE-FIRST — searched Open/⏳Verify/Done/Done-archive for "findDriveConnect", "contained point never relocated", "endpoint welded to face": B1612608 (Done) is the item that shipped the deliberate "never relocate a contained point" behaviour this item narrows; no prior item proposes welding to the crossing when the leg originates outside the pad. `[x]` **FIX.** `findDriveConnect` now takes the road vertex BEHIND the endpoint (`from`). When the dropped point is inside a drive target and `from` is outside it, the endpoint is welded to where the last leg crosses the pad face (`roadEdgeCrossing`) — the same crossing `driveJunctionsOf` already resolves the junction at — instead of staying exactly where it was clicked. A leg that lies wholly inside the pad (or has no resolvable `from`, e.g. the very first point of a fresh draft) keeps the old B1612608 behaviour untouched. Wired through every call site that can produce an endpoint connect: draw-time (`resolveEndpointConnect` in the click handler and the live preview), drag (`from` = the dragged vertex's live neighbour), and both ends of `finishRoad`.
`[x]` **MEASURED.** `ui-audit/verify-road-ends-at-court-face.mjs`'s endpoint check confirms the welded endpoint sits on the pad face rather than at its originally-clicked interior point, in both scenes (perpendicular deep endpoint and oblique arrival), both draw orders — printed as `endpoint on face: true` in the harness's per-scenario line.
