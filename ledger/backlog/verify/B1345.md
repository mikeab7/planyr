`[x]` **Shipped 2026-07-31. Parked in ⏳ Verify — `V664`.** The stall stripes are drawn exactly as before and cost a third of the DOM.
- **AUDITED IN THE LIVE DOM, NOT ESTIMATED**, on the owner's real Goose Creek plan at the whole-site zoom (ppf 0.02, where a 9′ stall is 0.18 px wide): parking 10 elements → **1,135 nodes (1,088 `<line>`)**; trailer 8 elements → **527 nodes (486 `<line>`)**; building 20 elements → **460 `<rect>`**, ~432 of them door leaves. **~2,100 nodes from 38 elements out of a 2,481-node canvas — roughly 85%**, not the estimated 48%, because the real DOM on this plan is ~2,500 nodes rather than the assumed ~4,600.
- **ONE CLAIM IN THE DIAGNOSIS IS REFUTED, in writing: the column grid IS gated.** `FEAT_BTN_MIN_PX` fires here — grid lines first appear at ppf 0.2 and there are none below it — so the grid is not part of this and no gate was added for it.
- **What shipped:** a GEOMETRY LOD tier beside the existing label tiers, same file, same units (`lib/labelLayout.js`). Below a 6 px on-sheet stall pitch the N per-stall `<line>`s become the N **subpaths of one `<path>`** — identical coordinates, identical stroke, same rasteriser, same pass. Nothing is dropped, thinned, merged or approximated, and an angled (leaning) stall field collapses too.
- **Two things were measured and REJECTED rather than shipped:** (1) an SVG `<pattern>` tiled at the run's pitch — Chromium rasterises a tile once and repeats it, so a non-integer pitch accumulates sub-pixel phase error (0.02–1.51% of pixels, worst delta **30/255**, growing with zoom); (2) the **dock-door leaves** (up to **23/255**) — now **B1350**.
- **Proven in pixels.** `ui-audit/verify-stall-lod-parity.mjs` drives two builds differing only in the thresholds and compares them pixel by pixel (`ui-audit/lib/pngDiff.mjs`, a dependency-free decoder). Declared bar, stated not assumed: byte-identical, or **one unit out of 255** on one channel. Result: three rungs byte-identical, two within 1/255 (5 px and 144 px, both in a one-pixel-tall strip on a band's own outline), and a control rung above the gate identical by construction.
- **PDF-PARITY, mandatory here and asserted:** the gate is measured on the LABEL frame (`px / lfK`, the `FEAT_BTN_MIN_PX` precedent), so an export decides at the SHEET's scale. Exporting from a zoomed-out canvas — the case that can go wrong — the sheet carries **1,658 line segments on both builds, drift 0**. Stall COUNT is separately asserted and unchanged (it comes from `carStalls().count`, a different code path).
- **What it bought, at 4× CPU throttle, two reps each:** canvas DOM nodes **2485–2547 → 932–935** at site overview (−63%), peak **2811 → 1261** (−55%); drag frame median **33.3–33.4 → 16.8 ms (halved)**; drag p90 **150–166.6 → 116.6 ms**; wheel-zoom median **200–250 → 116.7–150 ms**; wheel-zoom p90 **216.7–300 → 183.3–200 ms**.
