- Verify: live
- Origin: filed 2026-07-28 from chat
- **Why.** FBC Interim Atlas-14 Criteria §9 requires offsetting any reduction in floodplain storage within the EXISTING (pre-Atlas-14) 500-YR floodplain, not the 100-yr. If Bain's 97.7 ac-ft was derived off the 100-yr line it understates the requirement — and at +0.5% over it, the sign flips.
- **Fix.** `floodplainRules.js` gains `offsetElevBasis` (+ `offsetElevNote`), `mitigationOffsetBasis` (explicit field wins; else inferred from the trigger — a rule reaching into the 0.2% band owes its offset to the 0.2% surface), `offsetSurfaceLabel` and `offsetSurfaceBasis` (label + authority + citation for the panel line). The Fort Bend record is stamped `offsetElevBasis: "02pct"` with the §9 citation. `computeMitigation` now prices 1%-class fill up to the 0.2% surface under such a rule.
- **LOUD-FAILURE, deliberately not a silent substitution.** When the required surface is unresolvable the volume still prices on what is available (better than nothing) but the result carries `offsetBasis.matched:false` + the `offset-basis-unresolved` flag, the Mitigation verdict can no longer read a clean green OK, and the panel says the figure is understated and names the line it actually used.
- **Surfaced.** A "Measured up to — 0.2% (500-yr) flood elevation · NNN.N′" row with a code SourceTag in Mitigation detail, amber with a warn note when the required surface was unavailable.
- Tests: 4 cases (Fort Bend → 02pct with its §9 note; a 1%-only jurisdiction stays on the 100-yr; an explicit rule override beats the inference; the surface changes across five jurisdictions). Headless read-back: "Measured up to ⓘ 0.2% (500-yr) flood elevation".
