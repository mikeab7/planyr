- **THE REPORT.** *"if I'm in the settings just to change my password, and I don't want it to be right there… let's build out a more professional settings menu."* Settings was one tab of three whose entire body WAS the change-password form, so the most consequential control in the account greeted you before anything else.
- **THE SECTIONS.** `Profile` (name, organization, the address you signed in with) · `Team` (lazy, unchanged) · **`Account & security`** (change password, sign out on all devices) · **`Interface`** (display theme, smooth zoom — see B286000 ×2). A left nav on a desktop-width modal, wrapping to a row of chips under a phone width, in CSS with no measurement hook.
- **THE FRONT DOOR IS INTERFACE, NOT SECURITY, and that is the item.** The account dropdown's "Settings" row now lands on the ordinary app preference; the password is one click away in a section that says what it is. `SECTION_ALIAS` is the one place that decides, and the Shell's `initialTab` contract ("profile" / "team" / "settings") is unchanged, so no wiring outside this file moved.
- **⛔ THE RECONCILIATION THE OWNER ASKED TO BE STATED EITHER WAY: PROFILE AND TEAM FOLD IN.** They were already tabs of this same modal — what made them read as a third parallel surface is that "Settings" sat BESIDE them as a peer, as though profile and team were something other than settings. They are now sections OF Settings, and the account dropdown's three rows became DEEP LINKS into those sections rather than routes to different places. Nothing was duplicated and nothing was orphaned.
- **⛔ AND WHAT DELIBERATELY DID NOT SHIP: an "active sessions" list.** Enumerating a user's sessions needs the service-role admin API, which may never reach the browser (`CLAUDE.md` → KEY DECISIONS). A section that could only ever have listed *this* device would be furniture pretending to be a security feature. So Account & security carries the action that IS real — **Sign out on all devices** (`supabase.auth.signOut({ scope: "global" })`, which revokes every refresh token server-side) — and nothing else. An empty section never ships, and neither does a fake one.
- **LOUD-FAILURE on that action specifically.** Unlike the plain `signOut()` beside it, `signOutEverywhere()` RETURNS its outcome and the panel renders the error: a security action that fails silently is worse than one not offered, because the user believes they are safe.
- **PANEL-BREVITY.** Each section is verdict-first with no explanatory paragraphs; the one cloud-sync sentence that used to sit under the password form moved into Interface, where it is about the app rather than about the password, and it is the only prose in the panel. No section gained copy.
- **GUARDS.** `test/fullscreenChrome.test.js` (the NEW-4 half, 6): the four sections are declared; the Settings row aliases to Interface; change password is INSIDE Account & security and **absent from Interface** (sliced, so the assertion is about where it renders, not whether the string exists); the sign-out-everywhere action is present and no "Active sessions" string is; Interface renders the shared component; and the old three-tab row is gone.
- **⛔ WHAT IS NOT PROVEN HERE, stated rather than implied.** Sign-in is impossible in this sandbox, so nothing below the nav has been RENDERED — the nav, the section switch, the password form, the sign-out-everywhere round trip and the responsive collapse are all owed to V111107.
