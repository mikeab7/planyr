### B2179972 — Parcels never move from a stray click or drag; Lock now means "even Edit parcels can't touch it" `[Site Planner]` (feature) #site-planner #selection  *(owner chat block 2026-10-06 NEW-5)*
`[x]` Outside Edit parcels a click only SELECTS a parcel (a locked one too) and a drag PANS the map; corner grips and the add-a-corner gesture exist only inside Edit parcels, where a drag moves the parcel unless it is locked. Lock is off by default (every creation path stopped stamping `locked: true`), a grey padlock icon (no coloured emoji) shows on a row only when locked, and there is no separate "pin". **Meaning change, loudly:** Lock used to make a parcel click-through on the map, and every parcel was born locked — so on every existing plan the flag is the old default, not a decision. A `locked` flag with no `lockSem: 2` stamp therefore reads as UNLOCKED (`siteModel.withLockSemantics`, applied on every load, so stale devices and cloud rows converge); a deliberate lock under the new meaning is always written with the stamp. Anyone who had deliberately locked a parcel under the old meaning will find it unlocked once — a one-time, intentional reset.
