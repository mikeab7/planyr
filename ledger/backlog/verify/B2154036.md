### B2154036 — HARD PAGE FREEZE: a real click on the Site Planner "Properties" rail tab followed immediately by Escape, repeated, freezes the page on a LOCATED plan with a building assembly (V562) `[Site Planner / registration]` (bug, HIGH) #site-planner #view  *(AUTH-SWEEP 2, V562, build 6000483, reproduced 6×)*
Repro: signed in, a plan with an `origin` and a building assembly, aerial on; loop `locator.click` on the left-rail Properties then `keyboard.press("Escape")` with no delay. After 4–8 cycles the next click never completes and `page.evaluate` gets no answer for 45 s+ (main thread frozen). Controls that do not freeze: 15 rail clicks alone; 15 Escapes alone; 15 synthetic click+Escape in one tick; the same real loop on the unlocated `e2e-fixture-testfit`. This is the located-branch case V562 (B1189 follow-up) predicted. **Status:** a root-cause investigation is running (see this item's history). **Stopping rule:** the real click+Escape loop runs 30 cycles on a located assembly plan without a freeze, in a test.

`[x]` **Root cause (found by reproducing it deterministically in a local build, signed out, seeded located plan; the B1189 registration effect is NOT the culprit):** SitePlanner's Layers-coverage effect listed `[overlays, origin, view, size]` (whole objects) and dispatched an unconditional `setCoverage(<fresh object>)` from `prefetchExtents(…).then(recompute)` — an already-resolved promise, so the dispatch is a microtask. With B1189's retained `setSize` updater, every render mints a value-identical `size`; render → new `size` identity → effect → microtask `setCoverage(new {})` → render… The microtask queue never drains (~300 commits/s measured), and React's nested-update breaker never trips. Unlocated plans take the effect's `!origin` early return — which is why the control passed.
`[x]` **Fix:** the effect now depends on view/size NUMBERS and the dispatch is guarded by a pure `sameCoverage` against the last dispatched map (`lib/coverage.js`, `SitePlanner.jsx`). Tests: `test/coverageDispatchGuard.test.js` (RED on untouched code), and `ui-audit/verify-properties-escape-freeze.mjs` (real click+Escape loop on a located plan with an unlocated control and a void guard): froze at cycle 4 unfixed, 30/30 and 40/40 fixed. Other effects keyed on the `size`/`view` objects are still exposed to B1189's identity churn — not audited here.
- Verify: live — V1572240 (run the harness against planyr.io after deploy).
