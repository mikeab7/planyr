`[x]` **What was actually wrong: the hand-off at `zoomend`, three seams, each measured.** (1) **Zooming OUT past the close stage's floor** removed every line at `zoomend` (esri's `_handleZoomChange` + `pruneToLiveCells` detach all ghosts) and the close TILE LAYER, floored at the same zoom, dropped all its canvases (a GridLayer drops everything below its `minZoom`); nothing was drawn until the mid/wide stage had fetched. (2) **Mid ↔ wide** detached the other stage's tiles the moment the stage changed (`syncDrawn`). (3) **Inside the close stage**, `pruneToLiveCells` ran at every `zoomend` and dropped EVERY held lot ("belongs to a cell that is no longer current" — all of them, because the new zoom's cells had not answered), so the map drew no lines until the new cells' queries returned. Plus one frame at the wide→close release, which fired on `load` a frame before the close canvases repainted.

`[x]` **The fix: what is drawn is kept until its replacement is drawn** (`lib/parcelHandoff.js`, pure + unit-tested). (a) The close layer hands the lots it lets go of out of range to the wide layer (`_wide.carry`) instead of detaching them; they go when every tile the new view needs is loaded, attached and ingested (`carryReady`) or after `CARRY_MS` 4 s (a county that never answers cannot pin stale lines). (b) The wide layer keeps the PREVIOUS wide/mid stage's tiles under the new one on a stage change, same release. (c) The close tile layer is floored at the wide floor, not the close floor, so the canvases carrying those lots survive the zoom. (d) The prune waits for the layer's own `load` (`pruneVerdict`: only while in range and requests/ingest are pending; cap `PRUNE_IDLE_CAP_MS` 6 s) — the old lots are on the same ground at the same coordinates, so keeping them until the new cells land is not a stale picture. (e) The wide→close release lets go two frames after `load`. **Keeps #2495's request savings:** no request is added or made earlier — nothing here touches `WideTileEngine`, its debounce, concurrency, abort or back-off; `parcelWideRequest`/`parcelWideEngine`/`gisCacheHandler` tests unchanged and green. Sources with no wide stage (`_wide` null) behave exactly as before. The plain overlay outside Select parcels (the planner's "Pick county lots") shares the close layer, so (c)–(d) apply to it; (a)/(b) are wide-stage only.

`[x]` **ROOT CAUSE, measured (frame dumps + a per-frame sampler of every tile level's transform):** Leaflet animates a zoom by giving each tile LEVEL a CSS transform transition and builds the TARGET zoom's level at `zoomanim`. **Chromium does not start a compositor transition until its element has been rasterised.** Our parcel tiles are canvases painted synchronously in `createTile`, so the parcel layer's new level arrived full of fresh pixels and started its zoom frames late, rode behind the imagery while it faded in (the faint second copy of the outlines, smaller and offset), and snapped into place at the end — "it takes a second to relocate the lines". The imagery's own new level usually holds nothing yet (its images load later) and starts on time — UNLESS the imagery is in the browser cache (zooming back over ground just seen — exactly in-and-out zooming), when the IMAGERY's new level lags the same way. Two more of the same species: a level still FADING IN (opacity ≈ 0) is not drawn, so the next wheel notch waited on it; and a level revealed or created a moment before a zoom starts (the next notch, a stage's freshly added tile layer) waits too. The levels that were ALREADY on screen start together — the sampler showed them identical every frame.
`[x]` **THE FIX — one shared rule, `src/shared/map/steadyZoomLevels.js` `holdNewZoomLevels(layer)`, applied to the parcel tiles (`ParcelTiles`, both the close and the wide/mid layer) and to the Map view's imagery layer:** during a zoom animation only levels already on screen are shown (scaled, as Leaflet always shows the old level); a level built for the target zoom is `visibility: hidden` until the animation's closing `zoom`/`moveend`, when Leaflet has already set its final non-animated transform (its tiles load/paint while hidden, so it is ready on reveal); a level shown less than `HOLD_MS` ago is hidden for a zoom that starts then, when an older drawn level can carry the picture; and no level is pruned while one is hidden or for `HOLD_FRAMES`/`HOLD_MS` after a reveal (with the fade off, Leaflet prunes the moment a hidden tile is ready — that emptied the screen mid-zoom while building this). The Map view's map runs `fadeAnimation: false` (B651872's precedent on the Food and Dashboard maps), with B854832's `throttleTilePruning` on the imagery so per-tile prunes coalesce. **Trackpad pinch** (`src/shared/map/freePinchZoom.js`, shared by the Map view, Food and Dashboard maps): it called `setZoomAround(…)` with animation off on every frame — a full view reset, so every tiled layer (imagery included) threw away and rebuilt its tiles every frame and every `zoomend`/`moveend` listener ran per frame (31–45 frames of a 50-frame pinch showed lines over NO imagery); it now moves the map as Leaflet's own touch pinch does (`_move(…, { pinch: true })` per frame, one settle animation when the fingers stop).
