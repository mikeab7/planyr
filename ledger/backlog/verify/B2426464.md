`[x]` **MEASURED FIRST (house rule: a timing bug gets a live reproduction before any code change).** New instrument `ui-audit/verify-parcel-zoom-lock.mjs`: a rAF loop reads, on EVERY frame of a real animated Leaflet zoom, the distance in screen pixels from a lot's TRUE corner to the nearest painted line pixel. The oracle is Leaflet's own marker (it shares the tiles' zoom clock), so the reading is independent of the layers under test. Known-good arm: at rest the corner reads 0.61 px (else the run is VOID). Real-county twin `ui-audit/verify-parcel-zoom-lock-live.mjs` (Davidson, Harris; blank-frame count + an on-line marker).
`[x]` **The first suspicion — "the lines do not ride the zoom animation" — is REFUTED.** Through the whole animation (mid-animation frames, every gesture) the painted line sits **0.2–1.2 px** from the corner, before and after the fix: both GridLayers animate with the map, the tiles fade in with the old level still under them, and the lines never trail the imagery. Panning reads 0.6 px. Do not re-chase this.
`[x]` **What was actually wrong: the hand-off at `zoomend`, three seams, each measured.** (1) **Zooming OUT past the close stage's floor** removed every line at `zoomend` (esri's `_handleZoomChange` + `pruneToLiveCells` detach all ghosts) and the close TILE LAYER, floored at the same zoom, dropped all its canvases (a GridLayer drops everything below its `minZoom`); nothing was drawn until the mid/wide stage had fetched. (2) **Mid ↔ wide** detached the other stage's tiles the moment the stage changed (`syncDrawn`). (3) **Inside the close stage**, `pruneToLiveCells` ran at every `zoomend` and dropped EVERY held lot ("belongs to a cell that is no longer current" — all of them, because the new zoom's cells had not answered), so the map drew no lines until the new cells' queries returned. Plus one frame at the wide→close release, which fired on `load` a frame before the close canvases repainted.
`[x]` **Before → after, same instrument, same gestures (screen px; "blank" = frames with no line painted at the lot's corner / anywhere in the view).** *Mid-animation offset:* 0.2–1.2 px before, 0.2–1.2 px after (unchanged — it was never the problem). *Right after `zoomend`:* **before — the line is GONE** for the whole gap; **after — 0.4–1.1 px, no gap.** Offline (synthetic Davidson, instant county): close→mid out 19 blank frames (≈300 ms) → 0; mid→wide out 17 → 0; close→18.4 in 3 → 0; close 18.4→17.4 out 3 → 0; wheel out 3 → 0; **with the county answering in 1.5 s over ground the wide layer had never fetched: 109 blank frames (settled after 2.09 s) → 0.** Real county servers (Davidson 20 Culvert St, Harris/Katy, built app, no relay): Davidson close→mid 19 blank frames (525 ms) → 0; mid→wide 14 (466 ms) → 0; close→deeper 4 (208 ms) → 0; Harris close→mid 22 (714 ms) → 0; **Harris mid→wide 115 frames (2,578 ms) → 0**; deeper→close 7 (444 ms) → 0; wheel out 2 (164 ms) → 0. A stray 68.8 px on-line reading on the baseline (Davidson mid→wide) is the line being absent and the oracle falling back to a different line — the blank, not a drift.
`[x]` **The fix: what is drawn is kept until its replacement is drawn** (`lib/parcelHandoff.js`, pure + unit-tested). (a) The close layer hands the lots it lets go of out of range to the wide layer (`_wide.carry`) instead of detaching them; they go when every tile the new view needs is loaded, attached and ingested (`carryReady`) or after `CARRY_MS` 4 s (a county that never answers cannot pin stale lines). (b) The wide layer keeps the PREVIOUS wide/mid stage's tiles under the new one on a stage change, same release. (c) The close tile layer is floored at the wide floor, not the close floor, so the canvases carrying those lots survive the zoom. (d) The prune waits for the layer's own `load` (`pruneVerdict`: only while in range and requests/ingest are pending; cap `PRUNE_IDLE_CAP_MS` 6 s) — the old lots are on the same ground at the same coordinates, so keeping them until the new cells land is not a stale picture. (e) The wide→close release lets go two frames after `load`. **Keeps #2495's request savings:** no request is added or made earlier — nothing here touches `WideTileEngine`, its debounce, concurrency, abort or back-off; `parcelWideRequest`/`parcelWideEngine`/`gisCacheHandler` tests unchanged and green. Sources with no wide stage (`_wide` null) behave exactly as before. The plain overlay outside Select parcels (the planner's "Pick county lots") shares the close layer, so (c)–(d) apply to it; (a)/(b) are wide-stage only.
