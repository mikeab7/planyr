`[x]` **DONE, AND THE ANSWER IS STATED PLAINLY RATHER THAN ASSERTED: a grown page is SCALED to fit the paper's width — nothing is clipped — and height PAGINATES onto the next sheet.**
- **THE ABSENCE OF `@media print` BLOCKS IS NOT THE DEFECT HE SUSPECTED, AND SAYING SO IS PART OF THE ANSWER.** The note is never printed from the app's own stylesheet at all: `printHtmlDocument` writes a SEPARATE, self-contained document (`PRINT_CSS` in `lib/notesPrint.js`) into a hidden iframe and prints that. So there is nothing for a print-scoped anchor rule to override, and adding `@media print` blocks to the app's stylesheet would have been the wrong fix to a real observation.
- **WHAT WAS GENUINELY MISSING: the print sheet mirrored only the RIGHT-hand growth.** A box at a negative `x` or `y` printed left of or above the sheet's own content box, where nothing clips it and it simply falls off the paper. Fixed by mirroring the screen's rule construct-for-construct: `pageAnchorExtentLeftPx`/`pageAnchorExtentTopPx` (new, pure, no schema) add the same growth to the sheet's own padding, and the extra left padding is added to its width too so growing left does not squeeze the text column instead.
- **DRIVEN THROUGH THE REAL TOOLBAR PRINT BUTTON, not the pure functions alone** — that distinction is the one that caught a bug last round, when `NoteEditor.jsx`'s own `printPage` turned out never to pass a `doc` at all while every unit test passed. `ui-audit/verify-notes-print-free-placement.mjs` §1 clicks `nt-print` and reads the sheet the button actually wrote: `max-width: max(190mm, 1152px); padding-left: calc(8mm + 276px); padding-top: calc(10mm + 156px)`, with all four boxes present and their negative coordinates serialised verbatim.
- **AND A REAL PDF, ATTACHED TO THE PR** (`artifacts/notes-free-placement-print.pdf`, plus a PNG of the same sheet). Four boxes, one off each edge; laid out at full size all four are inside the printed sheet (left 46px in, right 46px in, above 139px down, below 919px down).
- **THE SCALED-vs-CLIPPED QUESTION WAS SETTLED BY DECOMPRESSING A PDF'S OWN CONTENT STREAM, not by reasoning.** On a control document, a 1152px sheet with a marker rect at its far right edge came back as `972 100 180 40 re` under a combined transform of `0.24 × 2.2135 = 0.53124` pt/px — putting the marker's right edge at exactly **612.0pt**, the Letter MediaBox's full width (`0 0 612 792`), on one page. The whole layout is scaled to the paper (here 71% of full size), not cut off at it.
