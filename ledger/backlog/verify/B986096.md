  **⛔ THE SAME MISTAKE MADE TWICE, THE RULE THAT CLOSES THE CLASS.** The first cut of the corrected column grouping still merged a lease's annualized rate and a sale's price/size into one shared "$/SF" derived slot; renaming its header from "Annual NNN" to "$/SF/yr" fixed the words and left the unit conflation itself intact — a sale price has no time dimension, so labelling it "/yr" was never honest either way. **The rule, restated because it was broken twice: a derived column's header must be a unit that is TRUE FOR EVERY ROW IT IS NOT GREYED ON — if two row types produce different units, they are different columns, never one slot reused because it's usually empty.** Three derived columns now, in this order under DERIVED: `$/SF or $/AC` (land/building sale, greyed on lease — follows the row's OWN recorded size unit via `landPricePerAreaUnit`, a genuine $/ACRE figure for an acre-quoted land comp, never converted to SF first the way `landPricePerSf` — unchanged, still used elsewhere — does) · `$/SF/yr` (lease only, greyed on land/sale — the annualized rate on its OWN quoted basis) · `Net Effective $/SF/yr` (lease only). **Both lease derived columns print their NNN/GROSS basis inline** ("$7.63/yr NNN") — a net-effective figure on a gross lease and one on an NNN lease are exactly as incomparable as the face rate is, and the fix is the same shape: state the basis, never blend it away.

  **NET EFFECTIVE RENT** (`netEffectiveLeaseRate`, comps.js) — the number brokers actually compare, computed from rate + term + escalation + free rent + TI (all now real, reachable fields): the face rate compounds by the escalation percentage once per full year of the term (a partial final year weighted by its fraction), summed to a total $/SF over the term; free rent (valued at the face rate) and the TI allowance (a one-time $/SF cost) are both subtracted; the net total is spread evenly back across the term. Computed, never stored — the same precedent as every other derived figure here (`landPricePerSf`, `annualLeaseRate`). **Deliberately NOT converted to a true NNN-equivalent for a gross-quoted comp** — that conversion needs an operating-expense figure this app doesn't capture anywhere; inventing one would be a guessed number wearing a precise decimal. True NNN normalization is future work, gated on an opex field this repo does not have.
