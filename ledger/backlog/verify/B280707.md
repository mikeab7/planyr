`[ ]` **🔲 OPEN — deliberately not attempted this session; it is a re-architecture, not a fix.**
- Verify: live (when built)
- **What is missing:** B280706 makes a split site REFUSE to state one FFE. It cannot yet state the RIGHT ones, because every yield number is site-wide by construction — one required detention volume, one governing flood elevation, one pad ledger, one cut/fill balance, one earthwork cost.
- **Why it is not a quick change:** the split has to travel from `identifyJurisdiction`'s per-parcel containment (which now exists, and is the input this needs) through `assessAdministrator`, `buildability`, the pad ledger and the export sheet, each of which currently takes exactly one authority. Doing half of it — a per-parcel FFE that the pads and the sheet do not honour — would put two contradictory floors on one drawing, which is worse than the refusal that ships today.
- **Stopping rule (STANDING RULE #2):** closes when a straddling site shows a per-parcel FFE that the pad check and the exported sheet both honour, verified on Goose Creek. Until then B280706's refusal stands and is the correct behaviour, not a placeholder.
- **Known scope:** at least Goose Creek today; any site whose lots cross a city limit in future.
