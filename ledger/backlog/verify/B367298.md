- **THE DECISION, STATED, AND WHAT WAS REJECTED.**
  - **CHOSEN — a LADDER of complete-facts forms, ending in nothing.** Rung 1 the full line · rung 2 the governing fact + "+N" (B371361's form) · rung 3 the same without its parenthetical qualifier · rung 4 the bare governing identity + "+N" · rung 5 **nothing but the pin**. Every rung is a true, whole statement; the complete label is always in the tooltip and in `data-jurisdiction-full`.
  - **Rung 5 is deliberate.** Below a certain width no true statement about a split jurisdiction fits, and the alternatives are a fragment ("Part in City of Bayto…", which reads as a different answer) or dropping the qualifier that carries the meaning ("City of Baytown", which claims the WHOLE site is in it). Showing nothing is the only one of the three that cannot mislead, and it costs nothing — the hover still has every word. It is "navigation wins" followed to its end.
  - **REJECTED — wrap to a second line** (the row's height is fixed and B1173's two-row header is hard-won; a tag that reflows the chrome while a window edge is dragged is a worse trade than a hover). **REJECTED — a CSS ellipsis on the whole line** (that IS the defect). **REJECTED — per-priority spans with graded `flex-shrink`** (built and measured: a heavily-shrunk band collapses to ZERO width and renders no ellipsis at all — a shorter label with no sign anything was left out, i.e. this item's own defect from the other direction).
- **⛔ THE DEFECT, MEASURED IN A BROWSER, AND WHY THE SHIPPED GUARD COULD NOT SEE IT.** `test/headerNavPriority` reads the SOURCE, and the source was right. The failure is a property of ONE label SHAPE: a split site's governing chain was built as a single pre-joined slot (`"Part in City of Baytown limits (full purpose, 6 of 14 lots) · rest in its ETJ"`), so `abbreviateJurisdiction` had nothing to drop and its "short" form was ~330 px in a ~150 px box. The pill fell back to a CSS ellipsis and cut it mid-word, on the owner's two longest labels — **Goose Creek and Tsakiris, at 761 and 860 px**.
- **TWO FIXES, AND ONLY THE SECOND MAKES A FRAGMENT IMPOSSIBLE.** (1) a split contributes its two governing facts as two SLOTS — the label text is byte-identical, the shortener finally has something to drop, and the "+N" count is right for the first time (it read "+2" for three hidden facts); (2) the rung ladder above.
- **THE MEASUREMENT THAT COST THE MOST, recorded so it is not rebuilt three more times: "how much room do I have" must not be a function of the text.** Three readings failed first — `host.clientWidth` while the zone is `flex: 0 1 auto` (the zone IS its content, so the comparison degenerates to `shown <= shown` and the label **ratchets down and never comes back**: shortest rung at 860 px, still shortest at 1440 px) · row width minus the siblings' content (content-independent, but not what flex grants once the row is over-subscribed — too generous, the pill overflowed) · step DOWN a rung on each clip (terminates, but every step changes the layout, so it overshot and sat blank with room to spare). The shipped reading asks the ZONE: its measured `max-width` when it is out of flow (centred mode), and its granted width with the span briefly filled with the FULL label when it is in flow.
- **PROVEN, in a real browser** (`npm run verify:jurbadge` — the real `AppHeader` + `JurisdictionBadge` over the real identify chain, 6 widths × 2 themes × 8 labels incl. The harness also asserts the floor is honest — a blank pill is allowed ONLY when the granted width is smaller than its shortest true form, so "give up early" is a failure, not a style.
