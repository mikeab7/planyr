### B2671295 — Dish rated on the place card still shows "—" when the rating is dated after an undated row's UTC day [Food] (bug)
- Reported: owner 2026-11-16, Mizuki A.Y.C.E Sushi & Hibachi › Salmon Nigiri, 1 undated visit; Brittany Roll showed 7.5 fine.
- Cause (found by reading, not reproduced on his data): `dishAggregates.dishSortKey` ordered a dish's rows by `rated_on` (LOCAL day) or, for an undated visit, `created_at.slice(0,10)` (UTC day). In the evening in Central time an older unscored row sorts as the newest, so `latestScore` was null → dashed "—".
- Fix: the undated fallback is the local day of `created_at`. Test: test/foodDishFlow.test.js "NEW-1 (2026-11-19)" (red on main in America/Chicago).
- Unchecked: an unscored newest row with an older scored one still shows "—" by design (latest wins).
