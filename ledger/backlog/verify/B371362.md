- Verify: live → **V162864** (`Blocker: auth` + `browser-egress`)
- **⛔ FIRST, THE GUARD THAT HAD TO SURVIVE, because patching around it is how this repo breaks the same thing twice.** `headerCenterFit` exists because the chip was centred inside its SLOT while the slot was `flex: 1 1 0%` leftover space between the breadcrumb and the account controls — so the chip's position was a function of how long the project and plan names were (measured 94 px right of true centre at 1600 px). B371361 pinned the slot OUT OF FLOW at the row's midpoint so the side groups keep their natural widths and NAVIGATION WINS is untouched. Out of flow, nothing stops the pill running over the plan chip's ▾ caret — the defect it had just closed — so `centerSlotMaxWidth` is the replacement bound: the slot is SYMMETRIC about the midpoint, so its width is limited by the WIDER side group plus a gap. Below `CENTER_SLOT_MIN` it returns `tight` and the slot goes back in flow, off-centre but readable.
- **⛔ SO NEITHER SIDE WAS AT FAULT, AND THAT IS THE FINDING.** The bound is right. The pill's fallback to pin-only is right. **The verdict BETWEEN them was decided against a CONSTANT while the quantity it stands for is a VARIABLE:** `CENTER_SLOT_MIN` is 120 px — the width of the word "Unincorporated" — and the least width at which a pill can still say something TRUE depends on the label. Measured on the real header with the owner's own side groups:

  | width | mode | offered | pill needs | shown |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1100 | centered | 236 | 200 | `Part in City of Baytown limits +2` |
  | **1000** | **centered** | **136** | **199** | **(blank — pin only)** |
  | 980 | tight | in flow | 199 | the WHOLE label |

  A slot the content cannot use is not a centred chip; it is an empty space where a chip used to be — and one band lower the fallback the same work already built shows everything.
- **THE FIX: the content declares the threshold.** The pill publishes the width of its shortest true rung on `data-center-min-fit` (a function of its TEXT, never of the width it is granted, so it cannot feed back on the layout that reads it); `AppHeader` passes it to `centerSlotPlan` as `min`. `centerSlotPlan` already took `min` as a parameter — **its shape did not change, and `centerSlotMaxWidth` was not touched.** Anything that declares nothing gets the constant and behaves exactly as before. Result: 1040 and 1000 now go `tight` and show the whole label; 1100 stays centred with a rung that fits.
- **⛔ MEASUREMENT CORRECTED FROM THE B367298 REPORT.** That item recorded "38 px beside a 453 px spacer at 980 px". Re-measured against current `main`, 980 px is now `tight` and shows the full label — the 38 px was the zone's CONTENT width with the pill already blank, not the offer, and the band moved as the centring work landed. The real starvation band is **~1000–1040 px, offering 136 px**. The earlier number is superseded; the symptom the owner reported was real.
- **BOTH RULES ASSERTED IN THE SAME RUN, and each mutation-proven** (`npm run verify:jurbadge`, **129/129** across 8 widths × 2 themes × 8 labels): ignoring the declared minimum → *"header stayed CENTRED offering 136 px while the pill declared it needs 199 px"* · replacing the symmetric bound with the NARROWER side (i.e. buying the pill's room back from the crumb) → *"the pill OVERLAPS the plan switcher by 63.8 px — B371361's defect is back"*. The check that fails a pill going blank **with room to spare** is kept and still green.
