- **THE DEFECT, reproduced the moment FEMA's row has "Draw above plan" ON (the lifted band):** that band is hosted INSIDE the plan SVG (`<foreignObject>` under `<g transform={panT}>`, `SitePlanner.jsx`) and carries the basemap wrapper's mirrored gesture transform ITSELF. During a pan `panT` is the live pan delta (B1440's anchored render), so the lifted fill moved by the delta TWICE. Per-step measurement: imagery +4 px/step, lifted FEMA +8 px/step (red 340→346→350→354…, purple 341→353→361→369…), up to 58 px apart on a 60 px drag, unbounded on a longer one; wheel zoom likewise (k applied twice). At rest `panT` is undefined, so everything agrees — which is exactly why it read "fine" in every static check, and why it snaps into place on release. Worst frames on the unfixed build: 9.5 px (drag), 8.7 px (touch), 5 px (wheel). The Parcels-in-front pick host (`geoPickWrapRef`, county lots on offer) has the identical structure and carried the identical flaw.
- **THE FIX:** `anchorInverseCss(viewT, regShift.dx, regShift.dy)` (`shared/viewport/viewAnchor.js`, pure) — both in-SVG hosts now cancel the plan group's live pan/zoom transform (as well as the registration shift they already cancelled), so they follow the basemap alone. Same 4-decimal rounding `anchorTransformAttr` writes, so it cancels what is on screen. At rest / export it returns `undefined` — nothing changes there.
