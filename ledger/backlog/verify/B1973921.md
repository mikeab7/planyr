### B1973921 — Show city names on the map finder `[site-planner / map]` (feature) #site-planner #gis  *(Owner ask 2026-09-29, NEW-2: "we should show cities on the map.")*

`[x]` **Source decision (evidence-first):** own lazy dataset + canvas layer, NOT Esri `Reference/World_Boundaries_and_Places` tiles. That service is a raster that also draws its own dashed state/country boundary lines and admin-area names (would double up with the state outlines above), and its density/styling cannot be controlled. Data: Natural Earth 1:10m populated places (public domain; carries NE's own per-place label zoom — Houston 3, Baytown 7, Conroe 6.7) as the backbone (`public/geo/place-names.json`, ~200 KB), plus kelvins/US-Cities-Database (MIT) for the small towns NE lacks — **Katy, Pearland, Sugar Land, Brookshire were absent from NE** (`public/geo/place-names-towns.json`, ~870 KB, fetched only at zoom >= 9). Built by `scripts/build-place-names.mjs`. **Honest limit:** the small-town source has no population, so the small-town tier is a heuristic (a name listed under 2+ counties appears from zoom 9, the rest from 10) and the draw-time collision pass keeps earlier tiers first; a hamlet can therefore occasionally win a label slot over a slightly larger town. A population-bearing US places source (Census gazetteer) was unreachable from this environment — swap it in `build-place-names.mjs` if it matters.
`[x]` **Behaviour:** big cities first, more names as you zoom in (verified: Houston at zoom 6 without Katy; Katy/Sugar Land/Baytown/Pearland at 10–12). Greedy collision pass (`layoutLabels`, capped) so a metro never becomes a wall of text. Light text over a dark halo (both finder basemaps are imagery; `setTone("light")` exists for a light background). **Steps back for site work:** band is zoom 3–13, half-opacity at 13, gone at 14 (= `PARCEL_MINZOOM`), no fetch there. Own canvas pane at z-index 260 — above imagery/state lines, below the vector overlay pane (400) and markers, `pointer-events:none` — so a name can never cover a plan, parcel or pin nor take a click.
`[x]` **Layers panel:** one new row "City names" (checkbox + ⓘ, on by default) placed after Road names + its slider; "Road names" ⓘ text and the `LABELS_TILES` comment updated to say it carries no city names and that City names is its own row.
