- **THE FIX, and why this shape (the item asked for a choice with a reason).** The centre slot is taken **OUT OF FLOW** — `position: absolute; left: 50%; translateX(-50%)` inside the now-`position: relative` row — with the side groups flowing normally. **The rejected alternative was an equal flex-basis on the two side groups**, which is precisely the rule B371361 REMOVED: equal bases hand each side the same share regardless of what it holds, which starved navigation and is how the pill came to sit on the plan chip's ▾ caret. Out of flow, NAVIGATION WINS is untouched by construction — the side groups keep their natural widths and the centre cannot take any.
- **The cost of going out of flow, and the bound that pays it.** Nothing else would stop an out-of-flow slot running back over its neighbours, so its width is **MEASURED**: `shared/ui/headerCenterFit.js` returns `rowW − 2 × (max(leftW, rightW) + gap)` — symmetric about the midpoint, so the WIDER group is the binding constraint and both edges clear. Read in a **layout** effect (VIEWPORT-STABLE: folded in before paint, so a resize or a panel toggle never shows a mis-sized frame) off a `ResizeObserver` on the row and both zones. It cannot feed back on itself: an out-of-flow slot changes neither side group's width.
- **THREE OUTCOMES, NEVER TWO (LOUD-FAILURE).** `centered` · **`tight`** — a wide breadcrumb in a narrow window leaves less than a 120 px slot, so the centre goes BACK IN FLOW and takes what remains: off-centre but readable and still able to abbreviate, which beats a sliver and beats a vanished chip · **`unmeasured`** — nothing could be measured, so the pre-existing in-flow layout runs. `tight` and `unmeasured` are deliberately DISTINCT: a header that silently stopped measuring would otherwise report a legitimate-looking `tight` everywhere and pass its own audit. `data-center-mode` publishes which is live, and the browser audit RE-DERIVES every `tight` verdict from the shipped pure rule against the boxes the browser measured.
