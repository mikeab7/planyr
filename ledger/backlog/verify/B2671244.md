- Verify: **live** — V2671269. Stopping rule: closes when the recorder has not fired for him over a normal week of use AND he says clicks place the caret, or when a `notes-snapback` event names the cause (V2671269).

**WHAT WAS TRIED (all green, i.e. the symptom did not appear).** Both inferences in the report were checked and ruled out: (1) the paste-options chip/menu does NOT restore a selection — its only hand-back is `closePasteMenu(true)`, a no-op unless focus is INSIDE the menu, and a press in the document runs `closePasteMenu(false)` (no focus move); (2) the blank pasted items are ordinary paragraphs a caret lands in (clicked each of 6 — all landed). Then the whole flow: sandbox editor AND the signed-in test account on planyr.io (build 36fc007, the pre-fix deploy) with a REAL clipboard and a real Ctrl+V, chip showing, menu open (Ctrl tap), opened-then-Escape, after ArrowUp, and after the save + cloud-sync settle (6 s), on blank paper, clicking Baytown Submittal, Final Plat, "Updates in red" and a typed list — 33 checks, every click placed the caret and kept it (`ui-audit/verify-notes-click-after-paste-live.mjs`).

**A FALSE LEAD, recorded so it is not re-filed:** an early run showed the caret NOT moving on clicks at the top of a tall pasted note. That was the instrument — the click point was above the viewport (`y = -176`) or on the toolbar (`elementFromPoint` named the highlight button), not the app. The live harness now proves each click point lands on the editor before it clicks and names the run VOID otherwise.

**THE INSTRUMENT (`lib/notesCaretSnapback.js` + a read-only recorder in `NoteEditor.jsx`).** A plain left click in the text that ends, 150 ms or 700 ms later, with the caret in a DIFFERENT textblock than the one clicked sends ONE `notes-snapback` event (≤ 1 per 5 min): node types, list depth, direction, whether a paste offer / menu was up, how long since the paste, where focus was — never a character of the note. Skipped for drags, modifiers, double-clicks, presses that began unfocused (select-then-enter blurs on purpose) and a page that changed underneath. Guards: repo-root `test/` **notesCaretSnapback**; `ui-audit/verify-notes-caret-snapback.mjs` (a required `build` step; a forced snap-back must be recorded or the run is VOID; a normal click records nothing).

**What the report could mean, to read first when one arrives:** `pasteOffer:true` + `menuFocus:true` ⇒ the menu is holding focus and the editor hands the caret back; `pasteOffer:false` ⇒ not the paste layer at all (a sync echo or the box's select-then-enter); `msSinceClick` ~150 vs ~700 distinguishes a synchronous restore from a delayed one.

**VERIFIED LIVE 2026-11-13 on planyr.io, build a3f5427 (signed in, test account):** the recorder fires once on a forced snap-back with structure only and no note text (`verify-notes-click-after-paste-live` + `verify-notes-caret-snapback` 6/6 on production), and every real click placed and kept the caret in all five flows above. That proves the INSTRUMENT, not the owner's symptom — which stays open (V2671269).
