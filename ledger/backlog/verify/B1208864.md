- **Fix, mechanically.** `writeFailureLog.js`: entries now carry a `kind` (`"rename" | "status" | "row"`), stamped by the writers going forward (`WHAT_RENAME`/`WHAT_STATUS` exported constants keep the label and the kind in lockstep) and inferred from an EXACT `what` match for legacy entries queued before this fix (never fuzzy — guessing wrong would replay a name and never write a status).
