- **Three findings, as the report asked for, in order:**
  1. **"Null" arrives from the SOURCE, not an app stringify bug.** The record was resolved via the TxGIO statewide fallback (not HCAD's own service — its schema, `SITUS_ADDR`/`GIS_AREA`/etc., is TxGIO's, and its `SOURCE` field literally says "HARRIS APPRAISAL DISTRICT" while carrying TxGIO's column names), and the raw stored `attrs` hold the literal 4-character string `"Null"` on `GEO_ID`/`LEGAL_DESC`/`MAIL_LINE2`/`SITUS_STRE`/`LOC_LAND_USE` — a known TxGIO harvest data-quality convention, not something Planyr computed (`String(null)` would read lowercase `"null"`). Fixed at the resolver: `appraisal.js`'s new `isPlaceholderValue()` treats `"Null"`/`"None"`/`"N/A"`/`"-"`/empty as absent everywhere a curated field is picked (`apprRows`, `apprAll`, `findAttr`, the situs ladder) — so `GEO_ID:"Null"` is skipped and the real 13-digit `PROP_ID` wins the Account/ID row instead.
  2. **The bare "0" was a PRECEDENCE bug, not a placeholder.** `SITUS_NUM` ("0", the house number) and `SITUS_ADDR` ("0 GRAND PKY, HOCKLEY, TX 77447", the real address) both match the situs ladder's rung-1 `/situs/i` pattern, and `SITUS_NUM` sorts first in the record's own key order — the exact "flat alternation, first-key-wins" defect this module's header already documents for the owner-mailing-address case, one level deeper. Fixed by trying every rung-0 key that ALSO names itself a composed `addr` field before falling back to a decomposed one (`SITUS_ADDR` now always beats `SITUS_NUM`/`SITUS_STRE`/etc. when both exist; a plain single-`SITUS`-column CAD is unaffected).
  3. **The 6,113 AC record is real and legitimate lineage, not a mis-match — the LABEL was wrong.** The selected 1.55-acre parcel (`e1455197tjjbyo`) and its 570.27-acre sibling (`e1455196tjjbyo`) are both split children of the SAME pre-split parent tract (`parentId: "psmsdrvzr9gzx_0"`, verified in the DB) — `performSplit` copies the parent's whole county record onto both children with no acreage note, so a 1.55-acre sliver still carries the ~571-acre original tract's stated acreage. Confirmed by measuring the drawn rings' own shoelace-formula area: 570.27 AC (matches the "main" sibling exactly) and 1.5524 AC (matches this parcel exactly, and matches the owner's own report to 3 decimal places) — and a third, separately hand-drawn "Parcel 3" (`e1455198tjjbyo`, no county record at all) measures 1.526 AC, matching the owner's "1.53 AC, no account" note. The Boundary section's Geometry check now says so explicitly — "This county record is inherited from the parcel's pre-split parent tract" — instead of the generic (and here actively wrong) "check calibration/projection" hint.
