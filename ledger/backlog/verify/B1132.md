- **The data — and a SCOPE FINDING the brief's wording would not have led you to expect.** Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain, via `scripts/build-admin-boundaries.mjs` (run by hand; the normal build, test and CI paths never touch the network). **NE's 1:110m admin-1 layer is the 50 US states + DC and NOTHING ELSE — no Canadian provinces, no Mexican states.** So Canada and Mexico read at the **country** level, which is what the requirement ties them to ("so Mexico and Canada read correctly at continental zoom") and is verified by test. Provinces would need the 1:50m layer (2.3 MB source, 13 Canadian features, and it has no Mexican states either — those first appear at 1:10m, ~25 MB). Judged out of scope against this budget and recorded in the script header rather than silently absorbed; revisit deliberately.
- **Simplification, and where the bytes actually went.** Douglas–Peucker at 0.025° (about three pixels at zoom 7, the tightest the layer ever paints) + dropping sub-pixel islets + outer rings only → 11,800 points. Plain GeoJSON of that is 192 KB, because it spends ~17 characters per point re-printing `-95.123,29.456`. Re-encoding each ring as **delta integers in thousandths of a degree** takes it to **104 KB — a ~45% cut with zero further shape loss**, which is a better trade than simplifying three times as hard. The decoder is the exact inverse and lives in the lazy tier.
- **Subordinate by construction, three ways over** — its own pane at z-index **250** (above imagery tiles at 200, **below** the vector-overlay pane at 400 and every marker, so site geometry can never be occluded); `pointer-events: none` and non-interactive paths, so it cannot take a click from parcel-select, the right-click menu or a site pin (the B98 rule); a white hairline over a soft dark casing — map-cartography styling that reads on both the dark aerial and the light USGS topo basemap, deliberately NOT app theme tokens, the same call `vectorOverlay.js` makes for its on-map lettering.
- **Canvas, not SVG.** 346 rings would be 692 individually-transformed `<path>` nodes re-laid-out every pan frame; Leaflet's canvas renderer draws the set into one bitmap. Chosen with B1121 (map memory) open.
- **LOUD-FAILURE.** A failed asset fetch reports `admin-boundaries-unavailable` through the existing `reportClientEvent` channel and clears its cache so a later zoom-out retries — never a silent no-op, and never a throw into the finder over decorative furniture.
