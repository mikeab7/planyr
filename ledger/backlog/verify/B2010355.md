### B2010355 — Typing a comp line leaves unparsed words in Notes `[comps / paste entry]` (bug) #comps #model  *(Owner report 2026-10-01 (NEW-4): `ZZ KML test - safe to delete, lease 50,000 SF at $0.65/SF/mo NNN, executed 9/15/2026` -> every field right, Notes = "ZZ KML test - safe to delete lease at executed". DEDUPE-FIRST: B986096 already fixed the same class for a standalone period word; this is the sibling for type/connector words.)*

`[x]` **Cause:** `extractUnlabeledLine`'s partial-claim path dumped everything no detector blanked into one Notes blob, so the words that introduce a claimed value ("lease", "at", "executed") rode along with the deal name.
`[x]` **Fix:** the leftover is read per SEGMENT of the line (comma/semicolon/bar, never the comma inside a number). A segment a detector touched keeps only its non-glue remainder; an untouched LEAD segment becomes the Title when the line is its own record and Title is empty (introducing words stripped); everything else goes to Notes verbatim. A free fragment after a claimed one, and free lines of a multi-line record, stay in Notes. Capital "A" is a name ("Airtex Building A"), only lowercase articles are glue.
`[x]` **Found, not fixed (pre-existing, separate):** `Sold 12.5 AC at $6.75/SF, closed 6/30/2026` types as a LEASE — a `$/SF` figure is a lease signal in `detectCompType`; not touched here.
`[x]` **One existing assertion changed on purpose** (`compParse.test.js` B1063904 case a): it pinned `notes` matching /land sale/ — the consumed type phrase leaking into Notes — beside the real safety assertion (5 AC stays acres), which is untouched.
