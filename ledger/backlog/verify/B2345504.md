`[x]` **Header reading rewritten** (`compParse.js` `matchHeaderCell` / `HEADER_RULES` / `parsePasteBlock`): rule-based, case- and punctuation-insensitive, priority-ordered (an escalation header can't be read as rent, "Tenant Improvement" can't be read as tenant); the parenthetical is a unit hint ("Lease Term (months)", "Rent per SF/Month" → monthly for every row, so no "Rate has no period" on a sheet that said it once). A sheet title row above the header is skipped (the first of the first six lines with ≥2 known columns and no figures/dates is the header). Every column with no comp field (expiration, total building SF, submarket, transaction type, load type, city, effective rent…) rides into Notes as "Header: value"; nothing is dropped.
`[x]` **Coordinates → pins at exactly that point**: x/y, lon/lat, longitude/latitude, or one "lat, lon" cell (the grid's own Location column). x is longitude; a sheet that swapped them is caught by range. County resolves after the paste with the same lookup a hand-dropped pin uses (`resolveCountyForPoint`, the grid's new county effect); no coordinate but an address → the map search's forward geocoder (`geocodeAddress`).
`[x]` **Title** "<Property Name> - <Tenant>", or "<Address> - <Tenant>" when the property name repeats the address. **Free rent prose** ("6 mo $0.37 NNN", "9 (2 mo gross/ 7 mo net)") → the leading number in Free (mo), the full text in Notes. "-" cells are blank. "Jun-26" → 2026-06-01 (no per-row "day defaulted" flag for a month-year column; a quarter / mid-year still flags). A pure lease header row sets Type = Lease for every row with no "Type wasn't a column — guessed" warning. Excel date serials read.
`[x]` **Rates keep their precision**: `moneyVal`/`findRateToken` rounded to cents, so "$0.665/SF/mo" saved 0.67 on the text path; now only float dust is stripped. **Clear height and year built** are now read from text too ("32' clear", "Clear Height: 36 ft", "built 2019", "Year Built: 2024").
`[x]` **The grid's own header names round-trip** (Type, Location, Title / Address, Size, Unit, Clear Ht (ft), Yr Built, Executed (ISO), Commence (ISO), Term (mo), Rate, Per, Basis, Escal (%), Free (mo), TI ($/SF), Landlord/Seller, Tenant/Buyer, Notes) — guarded generically: every label `SHEET_COLUMNS` renders must be claimed by a real rule.
`[x]` **KML names decode entities** (`kmlImport.js` `decodeEntities`, now also numeric refs): "Dircks Moving &amp; Logistics" no longer reaches a title escaped. (`kmlExport.test.js` had pinned the escaped name; updated.)
