### B2671329 — Map: a comp pin that shares a spot slides against the map on zoom (the B2013744 screen-pixel nudge) `[site-planner / map]` (bug) #site-planner #comps #map-notes #map  *(Owner report 2026-11-24 with screenshot: "the pin moves independently of the map on zoom in or out" ... "the orange one" (an under-construction comp). DEDUPE-FIRST: searched open/verify/done for pin + zoom/drift; the cause is the fix of B2013744 (done), a different symptom, so a new number that cites it.)*

`[x]` **Cause (AUDIT-FIRST):** `lib/pinCluster.js pinClusterOffsets` (B2013744) moved every coincident pin but the first by a FIXED screen-pixel slot (38 px / 50 px) via `iconAnchor`. A screen distance is the same at every zoom, so the pin's spot on the GROUND changed with zoom (zoomed out it sat about a quarter-mile off its building) and slid against the aerial through each zoom animation. The supply import made it common: production has many under-construction rows on one park coordinate (Fairbanks Landing: four on one point; Interchange 249, Lonestar Logistics, Constellation South Belt, ...), and a supply record often shares its coordinate with that building's lease row. Not specific to the orange marker: every comp type and notes did it; orange was simply where the stacks are.
`[x]` **Fix:** stacks, not nudges. Every pin is ALWAYS drawn on its own coordinate. Pins on one spot form a stack (`pinStacks`, `pinPlacement`): only its lead is drawn (on the true point, above a site pin there), wearing a count badge; pressing the badge fans the stack out with tethers back to the point so every member is clickable (B2013744's requirement kept); the fan closes the moment the map starts to zoom or on a press on empty map, so nothing is ever drawn off its point through a zoom. A site pin is never moved or hidden; in a fan the comps/notes all leave the true spot so the site stays reachable.
- Guard: `test/pinCluster.test.js` (stack rules for all five comp types + source guard: no `pinClusterOffsets`/`shiftAnchor` in MapFinder, fan closes on `zoomstart`); `ui-audit/verify-pin-overlap.mjs` rewritten: RED ARM `?mode=legacy` must drift > 5 m on the ground between zooms (measured 2,087 m) or the run is VOID; fixed arm: every drawn pin of all five types within 1 px of its point at zooms 12-19, one badge counting 7, lead + site reachable collapsed, real mouse press on the badge fans 6/6 reachable + site reachable at its centre + 6 tethers, a zoom closes the fan with everything on its point.
