[] : sitePlanOverlays` before it ever reaches `useSitePlanOverlayLayers` — a real, deliberate gate ("a site plan is meaningless zoomed all the way out"), but it is **completely silent**: nothing in the Site plans list, the eye toggle, or the map itself tells you it's the reason nothing painted.
