- **Fix — split by AXIS, per the owner's amendment.** (a) **PERPENDICULAR is always DERIVED and always flush:** re-resolved from the live strip on that side (its thickness only — never its run, so a rotated strip cannot leak a wall-length value) on every host relayout, in `relayoutWallKids` and in the load-time `normalizeWallKids`.
