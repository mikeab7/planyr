`[x]` **⏳ merged — V478 PENDING (Verify: live — `Blocker: real-data`).** Measured live, not assumed: an out-and-back N–S excursion of ~89,000 ft left a **-4.3 ft residual, uniform across all six probes at three screen positions**, and it ACCUMULATES; E–W was exactly lossless (0.0 ft); at the far position the basemap disagreed with the drawing by **+0.18% in both axes** while the SVG element pixel widths were unchanged. **Hypothesis A (a `sec(lat)` correction pinned at origin, worse E–W) is REFUTED** — E–W is perfect. **Hypothesis B (a B183/B837 fixed offset after settle) is REFUTED** — the error scales with N–S travel. **Scope, answered and not re-litigated: ALL elements drift together** (`distinctTransformChains: 1`), so this never went near the `detentionStorage` evidence path.

**Root cause (confirmed against the code, AUDIT-FIRST):** scale was re-derived at the CURRENT view centre (`SitePlanner.jsx` — `ppfToZoom(view.ppf, center[0])`) while position was converted through `feetToLatLng(pt, origin.lat, origin.lon)` (`lib/arcgis.js`), an equirectangular frame pinned at the SITE origin using a constant `FT_PER_DEG_LAT` and `cos(origin.lat)`. Mercator's y is nonlinear in latitude and the feet frame's is linear, so the two round-tripped inconsistently and every excursion left a few feet behind.

**Fix — one model for both halves, in one new home.** `lib/mapLock.js` is now THE projection that welds the two together:
- the feet frame is an exact **uniform scaling of spherical Web Mercator** anchored at the site origin (`x = (lon-lon0)·k`, `y = -(mercDeg(lat)-mercDeg(lat0))·k`, `k = FT_PER_DEG·cos(lat0)`), so feet↔screen is a rigid affine map of Mercator↔screen and the round trip is exact at any pan distance in any direction;
- `ppfToZoom` moved beside it and is anchored at the **site origin latitude**, never the panned-to centre;
- both are derived from the SAME `FT_PER_DEG`. They previously came from different constants (a feet-per-degree constant vs the earth's circumference in metres) that disagreed by ~4 parts per million — small, but it left a residual that grew with distance from the view centre. The lock is now exact by construction rather than approximate.
- `arcgis.js` delegates `lngLatRingToFeet` / `feetToLatLng` to it (one seam, not two), and `aerialGeom` places the export aerial through the same Mercator frame so the print path can't drift from the screen (PDF-PARITY).

**No migration needed, and this is checked, not asserted:** at the site origin the new frame is identical to the old to first order. Saved geometry moves by **under an inch across a 2,000 ft site** and a few tenths of a foot a mile out — and that difference is the CORRECTION, since the basemap under the drawing has always been Mercator. `test/mapLock.test.js` pins both bounds.

**The regression guard the report asked for.** `test/mapLock.test.js` (13 cases) asserts the round-trip invariant directly — `lockOffsetPx` computes where a feet point lands on screen TWO independent ways (the planner's SVG transform, and the basemap's own Mercator projection at the derived zoom) and they must agree — out-and-back in five directions, over ten repeated excursions, plus a "the old mixed model would have failed this" case so the invariant can't go vacuous. Headless: `ui-audit/diagnose-map-lock.mjs` drives the real app through a ~25,000 ft excursion and asserts the basemap sits at EXACTLY the drawing's scale at start, far and back. It **fails on pre-fix code and passes on the fix** — verified both ways this session.
