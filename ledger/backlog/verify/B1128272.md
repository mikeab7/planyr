`[x]` **FIXED THIS SESSION.** Root cause, exactly as reported: pressing the Schedule module's "Dashboard" breadcrumb crumb called `goDashboard()`, which did TWO navigations — `goDashboardWithinModule()` (the correct one: posts the embedded scheduler to its own reports view and clears the routed project) AND `onGoDashboard?.()` (the Shell's "leave this workspace, go to the Site Planner map" action). Both are real, both fire on the same click, and the second usually won the race — the "often" in the report. `onGoDashboard?.()` was added deliberately (B866xxx) so Schedule's crumb would behave like Library/Notes/Review's, which have no dashboard of their own and for which "Dashboard" can only mean "leave" — but Schedule DOES have its own dashboard (the embedded app's reports view), so the same reasoning didn't hold here.
- **Fix:** `goDashboard` in `src/workspaces/scheduler/Scheduler.jsx` now performs the in-module navigation only — the `onGoDashboard?.()` call is removed. `goDashboardWithinModule` (the B1050 route-clear + iframe-post logic) is completely unchanged.
- **The adjacent case (checked, not left for a follow-up ask):** the header had two controls that both said "Dashboard" — the `planyr` wordmark and the breadcrumb crumb — both driven off AppHeader's single `onDashboard` prop, so on Schedule they fired the identical (buggy) handler. Simply dropping the `onGoDashboard?.()` call above would have made BOTH controls stay in-module, silently deleting the only way to leave the Schedule workspace from the header. New pure module `src/shared/ui/dashboardNav.js` (`logoDashboardAction`, `crumbDashboardTitle`) splits the wordmark's action from the crumb's: `AppHeader` gained two new optional props, `onLogoDashboard` (overrides the wordmark's click handler, falling back to `onDashboard` — every caller but Schedule omits it and is byte-for-byte unaffected) and `logoDashboardTitle`; `ProjectBreadcrumb` gained `dashboardTitle` (overrides the crumb's tooltip, same fallback rule). Schedule now wires `onDashboard={goDashboard}` (crumb → stays in Schedule, shows its own dashboard) and `onLogoDashboard={onGoDashboard}` (wordmark → leaves the workspace) — two distinct handlers on two distinct props, so the race is structurally impossible rather than merely fixed by timing. The two tooltips now say what each one does in plain words: wordmark "Leave Schedule — go to the Site Planner map", crumb "Schedule dashboard — reports for every project" (previously "Dashboard: all projects" / "All projects: Dashboard" — near-identical wording for what are now two different actions).
