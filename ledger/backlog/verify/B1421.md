(a) `identifyLayers` pointed at 17/21, which are **mosaic LAYERS**: ArcGIS `identify` expands a mosaic and reports only its children, so `r.layerId === 17` matched nothing, ever. The raster sublayers are **20** ("1 Percent WSE Image") and **24** (".2 Percent WSE Image"). (b) `pixelValueOf` looked for `"Pixel Value"` / `"Stretched.Pixel Value"`; this service reports **`"Service Pixel Value"`**. Net effect: `sampleEbfePoint` had returned a permanent, silent `{null, null}` since B882 — read downstream as "outside InFRM coverage", everywhere, forever.

- **So `availability` is a new registry axis, ORTHOGONAL to `tier`.** `tier` answers "is this the authoritative agency's production endpoint" (a design fact); `availability` answers "does it ANSWER" (an operational fact). Conflating them is exactly how a row sat at `production` with a fresh `lastVerified` while failing in its own home county. A `down`/`degraded` row **requires** a complete `outage` record — `{since, symptom, evidence, impact, replacement}` — and the sampler now **throws a named outage** instead of returning the null that read as "no coverage here". On the owner's core county those are opposite facts, and MAAPnext usually runs HIGHER than the effective FIRM, so the estimate a Harris site DOES get is likely low. That sentence is in `outage.impact`, and the drainage check renders it as a named `unavailable` state.
- **The verifier INVERTS for a declared outage**, which is what keeps this honest without keeping CI permanently red: a `down` row is asserted to be STILL down, and a row that starts answering again is reported as a **PROBLEM** — "recovered, flip it back to live". That is the one message worth having on the day it returns.
- **THE ROOT CAUSE OF THE SILENCE: twelve rows carried NO coverage fixture at all.** A fixture-less row gives the verifier nothing to assert, so it can never go red — which is precisely why both flood rows rotted for weeks behind a green weekly check. `auditRegistry` now FAILS a fixture-less row, and every one of the twelve has a live-measured fixture.
