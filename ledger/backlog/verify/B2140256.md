- **Root cause:** `parcelCoverage.classifySourceKind` inferred kind from the provenance PROSE, and `THIRD_PARTY_RE` won first. Both notes mention the Westwood copy they SUPERSEDE, so "westwood" in a sentence about what was replaced flipped a first-party source to third-party. A prose regex cannot tell "is a Westwood copy" from "replaced a Westwood copy"; the same trap would recur for every future entry that records what it superseded.
- **Fix (ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH):** every record in `countiesProvenance.js` now carries `publisher` (`own` | `statewide` | `third-party` | `unverified`) and a short `publisherName`; `parcelCoverage.js` reads those and nothing else. The three regexes, the aggregator-host test and the `scopeWhere` heuristic are DELETED, no fallback. Registry-structural statewide (derived Texas, a layer URL that is a statewide composite) stays structural. The hover line shows `publisherName` before the host.
- **Backfill method (evidence, not prose):** started from the old classifier's output, then queried ArcGIS Online's item search for every AGOL-hosted service and read its item owner / copyright / org (the account that actually published it), and hand-checked every entry whose prose matched both a third-party word and an own/supersedes word plus all old "unclassified". Rules: own = the jurisdiction, its assessor / appraisal district, or a vendor / regional commission hosting on its behalf (TotaLand for a parish EDC, BIS Consulting for a CAD, a regional commission's shared host); third-party = a firm's, researcher's, individual's or OTHER public agency's republication (Westwood, DesireLine, ES2/Regrid schema, University researcher accounts, a levee district, NLCOG, a city republishing the county's roll).
- **Result:** own 95 · statewide 35 · third-party 31 · unclassified 11 (was own 48 / third-party 21 / statewide 35 / unclassified 68). The 11 are explicit `publisher: "unverified"` records whose `publisherName` says why (owner account has no profile, org name or credit line tying it to the county): Fort Bend, Oklahoma County, Macomb, and Georgia's Dougherty, Bulloch, Camden, Effingham, Butts, Troup, Muscogee, Emanuel. **Stopping rule:** each flips to own/third-party the day someone establishes the publisher (read the item on arcgis.com or ask the CAD/GIS office) — a one-line data edit, no code. Judgment calls to eyeball: Georgia regional commissions (Coastal / Southern / Middle Georgia / River Valley) hosting county layers are `own`, while NLCOG (Caddo/Bossier/Webster) stays `third-party` because its own note calls it a republication of the assessor roll.
- **Guards:** `test/parcelCoverage.test.js` — Calcasieu and Jefferson Parish pinned `own` (with their Westwood-mentioning notes asserted present, so the test keeps proving the old trap), Allen / Lafayette `third-party`, Jefferson Davis `own`, a Florida county `statewide`; "the field alone decides, whatever the prose says"; EVERY wired registry entry must carry a valid `publisher` + `publisherName` (a wiring session cannot skip it); every `unverified` must say why. **Red-proof:** the same tests run against the old classifier on main FAIL (4 red).
