### B1992432 — DFW city limits + ETJ layers (50 mi of Dallas) and the "unavailable, not unincorporated" rule `[site-planner / map + jurisdiction]` (feature) #gis #site-planner  *(Owner ask 2026-09-30, NEW-1: "wire all the city limits and ETJs within a 50 mile radius of Dallas.")*

`[x]` **THE SOURCE FINDING, FIRST — NCTCOG DOES NOT PUBLISH AN ETJ LAYER.** Its hub (`data-nctcoggis.hub.arcgis.com`) lists 195 datasets in its DCAT catalogue; none is an ETJ (and none mentions "extraterritorial"). Its Boundaries service carries Cities Region (2025), counties, ISDs, MPO — the brief's preferred ETJ source does not exist. NCTCOG's own server (`geospatial.nctcog.org`) is also denied by this build environment's egress policy, so its city limits could not be probed either. Fallback used, per the brief: the publishers themselves. **Sources recorded** (all ArcGIS Online, CORS `*` from `Origin: https://planyr.io`, checked 2026-09-30): Collin County GIS `ETJs` (33 city names, edited 2026-09-26) · Rockwall County GIS `ETJs` (6, edited 2026-06-25) · Denton County GIS `_ETJ/1` (33 names, **2022-08-02 — older than SB 2038**) · City of Fort Worth ETJ (already registered; **hosted copy is a 2018-09-13 snapshot** — the city's current layer, `mapit.fortworthtexas.gov/…/OpenData_Boundaries/MapServer/1`, is on a host this environment cannot reach). **City limits: the statewide TxGIO row already covered DFW** — Dallas verified; cross-checked against TxDOT City Boundaries (a 2020 edition): both return 205 cities in the circle and differ on ~5, mostly spelling ("Saint Paul"/"St. Paul"), so TxGIO stays the source.
`[x]` **One registry, one layer, one identify.** `etj_collin` / `etj_rockwall` / `etj_denton` are `GIS_SOURCES` rows (endpoint in ONE place; each states `dataLastEdited`, `roster`, `completeCounties`) → `ETJ_SOURCES` rows in `jurisdiction.js` (region-routed, so a Houston pan never touches a Collin server) → the drawn `jur_etj` layer, which now draws EVERY routed ETJ source through a multi-source `query.sources` (also fixes the old drift where Austin/Baytown/Fort Worth ETJs were identified but never drawn). Still ONE panel row, "City limits & ETJ": city SOLID, ETJ DASHED, same hue (no opacity-as-hierarchy). Layer ⓘ vintage is composed from the registry's dated rows (`etjVintageText`) — Collin edited 2026-09-26, Denton 2022, Fort Worth 2018 — never "current edition". No parallel mechanism was built.
`[x]` **DATA-SAFETY RULE — silence is not a finding.** Inside the 50-mile DFW zone a point with no city and no ETJ hit is "Unincorporated" ONLY when its county is declared complete by a routed source (`completeCounties`: Collin, Rockwall — the publisher IS the county's GIS office). Anywhere else — Tarrant, Dallas, Denton, Ellis, Johnson, Kaufman, Parker, an unknown county, or a FAILED lookup — it reads **"Outside city limits · ETJ data unavailable"** (`etjUnavailable`, on the badge, the Site Analysis rows, and the zoning summary). A real ETJ hit is always a positive finding. `unincorporated` (the back-compat boolean the county tier reads) is untouched. Denton County is deliberately NOT declared complete (its roster omits cities it cannot speak for).
`[x]` **Overlaps reported as ALL, never picked.** Denton County publishes a strip both cities claim as one feature ("Denton/Cross Roads", 8 such); `lib/etjNames.js` splits it (registry data: `nameSplit`, `nameStrip`), the identify unions both, the area-share path counts the polygon toward each claim, the label reads "ETJ crosses City of Denton + City of Cross Roads".
- **Coordinate note (asked for in the brief):** the layers are WGS84 GeoJSON drawn on the Leaflet basemap and queried with `outSR=4326` — zone-independent, so **the North Central (2276) vs South Central (2278) mismatch causes no registration error for these layers** (confirmed on the live render). The spine was not changed. Where the zone DOES matter (unrelated to these layers, not changed here): anything measured through the 2278 spine at Dallas carries a computed scale of 1.00168 (~0.17%, ≈1.7 ft per 1,000 ft) and a ~1.1° grid convergence.
- **Scope decision, stated:** the coverage rule applies inside the DFW zone only. Outside it (Houston, Austin, the rest of Texas) behaviour is byte-identical to before — extending the same honesty statewide would re-label every rural point outside a metro and is filed as B1992434 rather than smuggled in here.
