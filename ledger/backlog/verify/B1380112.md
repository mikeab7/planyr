**DEFECT 1 — THE PERIOD THE CARD SPEAKS IN WAS NEVER NAMED.** Lease rates are recorded monthly OR annually, per comp; Michael's account holds both (the featured Generation Park comp is `annual`, all three siblings are `monthly`). `annualLeaseRate` did normalize correctly, so the arithmetic was right — but nothing in this card SAID which period it spoke in, and nothing would have caught it changing: the period lived implicitly in one helper call and one `"$/SF/yr"` string literal, two modules apart. **Fixed** with `CARD_LEASE_PERIOD` (annual, matching `compSheetColumns.js`'s own derived `$/SF/yr` column, whose header "commits to ONE unit" — so a rate read off the Dashboard and off the sheet are the same number). Every figure the card renders — the featured headline, every peer rate, both scale ticks, the sentence — passes through that one normalization, and `compHeadlineRate` now STAMPS the period on its result rather than leaving it to a caller's string. A lease whose period is not recorded returns null rather than printing its raw number under an annual label.

**DEFECT 2 — THE PEER SET PUT FOUR INCOMPATIBLE RULERS ON ONE SCALE.** Two axes, both reproduced by `probe-comps-peer-set.mjs`: (a) an $11 GROSS rate and a $5 NNN rate are roughly the same deal, and they shared one rule as a 2× spread; (b) a land comp's headline rate is quoted in its OWN recorded unit, so two identically-priced 20-acre tracts — one entered in acres, one in SF, **43,560× apart** — shared a scale and produced the sentence "**$129996.90 above the median**". **Fixed** with `compScaleKey(comp)`: the identity of the ruler a rate is measured against (`lease:annual:nnn` / `lease:annual:gross` / `land:ac` / `land:sf` / `building_sale:sf`). A comp joins the peer set only when its key matches the featured comp's exactly; a comp that WOULD have been a peer (same type, county, band) but sits on a different ruler is **excluded and counted, never rescaled** — the conversion that would make it agree is precisely the one the app cannot honestly perform. This makes the card agree with a rule the repo already held: `shared/comps/lib/comps.js`'s `summarizeLeaseComps` refuses to blend NNN with gross for exactly this reason ("there is no honest conversion between them without the underlying expense figures the app doesn't have"), and a lease with no stated basis counts toward its `unknownCount` — the card now treats that identically (no declared scale → no peer set, never a defaulted one) instead of quietly disagreeing with it. `excludedCount` gained a companion `excludedReason`, because the card's fixed note "missing county or size" stopped being true the moment a second exclusion reason existed.

**DEFECT 3 — A TIE WAS REPORTED AS A RANK.** Four comps all at $6.00 read "**the highest of the four**". `sortedDesc.indexOf(featuredRate) + 1` is a claim that one number beat the others, and it is false whenever any two are equal — the existing test suite even contained a comment dodging the ambiguity ("Use a distinct featured rate to avoid a tie ambiguity in the assertion"). **Fixed** with `rankAmong`, which counts strictly-higher rates and separately counts ties, and a `rankPhrase` that words them: "level with all three of its peers" (everything equal) · "tied for the highest / the lowest of the four" · "tied for second highest of the four". Equality is judged to within half a cent (`RATE_EPSILON`), the SAME threshold the median-delta clause already calls "right at the median" — so one sentence can no longer say "the highest" in its first clause and "right at the median" in its second. Rates are divisions (price ÷ size), so an exact `===` between two genuinely equal rates was never guaranteed either.

**DEFECT 4 — THE FOOTER PRINTED TWO CONTRADICTORY SENTENCES.** On Michael's real account the panel read "**Against your last 1 in Harris County, TX**" and, directly beneath it, "**Not enough comps in Harris County, TX yet to compare — this one stands alone.**" The heading is a claim that a comparison is being drawn, and it rendered unconditionally. **Fixed:** the heading and the scale now render only when a comparison actually is drawn; below `MIN_PEERS_FOR_SCALE` (exported, so the card and `peerComparisonSentence` can no longer hold that boundary separately) the panel shows one honest line and no scale — "No comparable comps in `<county>` yet — this one stands alone." with none, "Only one other comparable comp in `<county>` yet — not enough to place this one on a scale." with one or two. Net copy change in the sparse state: one line replaces two.
