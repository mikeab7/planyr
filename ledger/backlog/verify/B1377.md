- **The repair, `siteModel.normalizeOrphanWallPads`, does exactly two things**, under the same idempotency contract as every other heal, wired into `normalizeBondedChildren` immediately after the cross-host re-bond and **before** every pass keyed on the tag it restores:
  1. **RE-TAG.** A bonded, non-stack, box parking/paving pad on a building host with no role tag whose geometry resolves to a **non-dock** wall is stamped with the `sideParkSide` its own position implies, plus its index in that wall's outward stack. Its current along-wall run and centre are recorded as `sideParkFit` **intent** whenever they differ from the span default — the owner has been trimming these pieces by hand (nine pads tapering 679 → 486 ft on Silvestri), so the geometry on disk IS the record of intent and the B1340 span rule would otherwise correctly read every short run as staleness and stretch them all back to the full wall.
  2. **RESTORE.** On a wall with bonded side parking, NO wall strip, and a clear gap of about one sidewalk width between the wall face and the parking's inner face, the 5 ft sidewalk is minted back into the void. **The gap is the physical evidence:** `addParkingRowSide` offsets a new field by the thickness of the strip that was there, so a strip-width void under intact parking can only mean the strip was deleted out from under it — and a wall the owner deliberately left bare has its parking FLUSH and never matches. Tombstoned rows are **not** resurrected (a merge would strip them straight back out); a fresh id is minted.
