`[x]` **Shipped 2026-07-31. Parked in ⏳ Verify — `V663`.** The benchmark now measures the owner's real plan, with a drag that actually pans, on a machine slow enough to tell two builds apart.
- **THREE independent defects, all live at once, any one of which made the green 16.7 ms frame budget meaningless:**
  1. **The scene had the expensive work taken out.** The hand-authored reference scenario's road was a `{type:'road', cx, cy, w, h}` RECTANGLE — no `pts`, no `vtx` — and it carried **no ponds** and no polygon elements. `roadNet` / `teeJunctionsOf` / `driveJunctionsOf` / `weldJunctionsOf` / `dissolveRings` and all 1,390 lines of `roadGeometry.js`, plus `detentionRules.js` (2,122 lines), `floodplainMitigation.js` (1,233 lines) and the pond ledger, **executed zero times in the benchmark that certifies them.**
  2. **The gesture did nothing.** The scripted drag pressed at the exact canvas CENTRE, which on any real plan lands on an ELEMENT, so the view never panned. Measured head to head on the real plan: **604 DOM mutations** for the centre press versus **641,730** for the identical drag started on bare canvas. The sampler saw a clean 60 fps for both and reported the first as a 16.7 ms median.
  3. **One measurement point, with no dynamic range.** A fast headless CPU at dpr 1 renders the heavier scene at 60 fps too, so an optimisation that halves the work still reads 16.7 → 16.7.
- **Fixed.** The scenario is **derived** from `ui-audit/fixtures/goose-creek-plan1copy.json` (62 elements, 6 centerline roads with 14 arc vertices, 2 ponds, 6 parcels, the plan's own settings) — derived rather than copied, so it cannot drift from the fixture. The press point is chosen to be bare canvas, and `idleGestureFault` REFUSES a sample whose view transform never moved. `--cpu-throttle N` / `--dpr N` emulate a slower machine; under either, every metric is MEASURED BUT NOT JUDGED, and the plausibility floor drops from 30 fps to 2 fps because under deliberate throttling slowness is the measurand. At 1× the floor is exactly the committed 30, unchanged.
- **A DOM NODE COUNT per zoom level** was added — the harness counted leaflet tile requests and nothing else, so the size of the thing React reconciles every frame was invisible to every budget.
- **`npm run perf:ratchet -- --metric runtime.* --from-harness <a real --json run>`** is now the only way a runtime `measured` / `seededFrom` moves. It refuses an emulated run, a faulted sample, a drag that never panned, and any metric without `seededFrom` (`peakHeapMB` / `aerialTileRequests` hold PRODUCTION figures a sandbox floor must never overwrite).
- **THE NUMBERS GOT WORSE, AS EXPECTED, AND ARE REPORTED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED:** canvas DOM nodes **1197 → 2822** at the innermost rung (+136%); peak JS heap **41.7 → 68.8–98.0 MB**; drag median/p90 **16.7 / 16.7 → 16.7 / 16.7** (3 reps) — unchanged at 1×, because this container renders the heavier scene at 60 fps too. At 4× throttle: pan **14.8 fps (49.9 ms median)**, wheel zoom **6.4 fps — 199.9 ms median, 250 ms p90**, the first reproduction in this repo of the owner's *"I scroll out and it takes probably a whole second."*
- **NO CEILING WAS RAISED and no baseline was re-recorded** to accommodate any of it; **B1178 remains open and unexplained.** The two frame metrics were re-seeded through the named ratchet step at their unchanged value, so the provenance moves on the record.
