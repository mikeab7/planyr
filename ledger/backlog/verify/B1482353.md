  **Exactly one surface could ever stack, and it is the one that did.** The rest are single-valued by construction — a boolean or a string, not a list — so there is nothing to dedupe in them and none was touched. Reported honestly rather than claiming six surfaces were fixed.
- **THE FIX, in the SHARED primitive so a future surface inherits it.** `pushToastPure` (`src/shared/ui/Toast.jsx`) now honours a `dedupeKey`: a toast carrying one REPLACES the toast already holding that key, in its existing slot, keeping the same `id` (so React does not remount it and its dismiss timer does not restart under the reader's cursor). `client-stale` carries `dedupeKey: "client-stale"`, stable across streak/pending/reason. Toasts without a key are untouched — two real events are still two notices. The engine ALSO latches its own emit (`announceStale`, B1482352); the two close different holes and both are kept deliberately — the latch stops the ENGINE re-announcing, the key stops ANY surface stacking a repeat.
- **PLACEMENT: already correct, and this is stated rather than re-fixed.** `FloatingNotice` (B1000400) already owns position for every floating app-level notice — one shared fixed host, bottom-centre, above a mobile bottom sheet, one z-index. The banners in the screenshot sit at the bottom edge, not over the canvas centre; what put them over the drawing was that there were THREE of them stacking upward. With at most one, the stack no longer reaches the plan. No placement change was made, because inventing a second position rule is exactly what `FloatingNotice` exists to prevent.
- **THE RELOAD IS NOW REAL, AND NON-DESTRUCTIVE.** The sentence has always ended *"Reload the page to catch up"* while offering no button — the caller hard-coded `action: null`. It now carries a Reload action that **writes the pending-edit journal synchronously before navigating**. This closes a live hazard rather than a cosmetic one: `"stale"` was **the one engine state missing from the journal-write condition** in `SitePlanner.jsx` (`committing`/`retrying`/`failed` were named; `stale` was left to the coincidence of `pending > 0` being true at that instant). A reload re-seeds from the server's rows, and ROWS-CANONICAL-ON-SEED (`docs/DATA.md` §2 inv. 3) makes those rows win over a diverging local canvas unless a journal entry explains the difference — so the banner could have talked the user into losing exactly the edits it was complaining it could not save. `stale` is now named explicitly, and `refetchReplace`'s `foldJournal` restores the work on the way back in.
