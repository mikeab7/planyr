`[x]` **Production change (APPLIED 2026-10-08 by this session, owner-approved; read-only confirmation below):** new read-only `public.food_places_search_by_address(p_query, p_cap, p_center_lat, p_center_lon)` + GIN trigram index `food_places_address_trgm_idx` on `lower(address)`, both in `db/food.sql`.
