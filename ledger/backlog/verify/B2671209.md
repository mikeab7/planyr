### B2671209 — Site Planner left module rail: sideways scrollbar, and the vertical scrollbar covers the labels `[Site Planner / left rail]` (bug) #site-planner #ui
`[x]` **SHIPPED 2026-11-10 (NEW-1, owner screenshot: Academy › Concept A, desktop Chrome on Windows, short window).** Reported: a horizontal scrollbar along the rail's bottom and the vertical thumb sitting over the right edge of "Analysis" / "Drainage" / "Properties" (read "nalysis" / "rainage" / "roperties"; left edges cut too).
- **Root cause (measured on the unmodified build with real classic scrollbars, `--hide-scrollbars` removed):** the rail was a plain `overflowY:auto` column 54 wide with no x-overflow rule and the GLOBAL 10px scrollbar, so the usable width was 43; its content was 46 wide (`scrollWidth 46 > clientWidth 43` — hence the bottom bar) and each button's label room was 36 against labels 44–46 wide ("Drainage" 46.5), so centred labels spilled off both sides, left ones clipped by the rail edge, right ones under the thumb. macOS (overlay scrollbars) never showed it.
- **Fix:** `.left-rail` (global stylesheet) — `overflow-x: hidden`; for fine pointers `scrollbar-gutter: stable` and a fixed 7px `::-webkit-scrollbar` (Firefox: `scrollbar-width: thin`); rail width `LEFT_RAIL_W` 62 = 54 of label room + the 8px strip (`lib/mapChromeStack.js`; a touch device keeps `LEFT_RAIL_W_TOUCH` 54 with its overlay scrollbar, phone layout unchanged); button side padding 0 and `white-space: nowrap`; the narrow panel offsets read `leftRailW`. The Land panel (and every docked panel) sits 8px further right — flush against the rail, nothing between them.
- Verify: live — real classic scrollbars at several heights/zoom levels; checked in the sandbox (Chromium without `--hide-scrollbars`) and on planyr.io after the deploy.
- Guards: repo-root `test/` **leftRailGutter**; e2e **left-rail-scrollbar** (scrollWidth === clientWidth, every label's TEXT box inside the visible rail, centred under its icon, tall = no scrollbar, short = vertical only, panel flush; 8 of 9 red on 9ece547).
