- **THE REPRO, as observed:** navigate to `planyr.io/#/notes` on a machine whose tab predates the Notes deploy. The app renders the Site route; the row-2 nav shows only Site / Schedule / Review / Library, with **no Notes tab at all**; the hash resolves to nothing. `Ctrl+Shift+R` immediately produces the correct build. No banner, no prompt, no indication anything was out of date.
- **WHY IT IS LOAD-BEARING and not cosmetic:** it makes every ship unverifiable from the owner's side. Told a feature is live, he opens the app on another computer, sees no sign of it, and correctly concludes it is not. It did exactly that, and cost most of a session's confusion.
- **TWO INDEPENDENT SIGNALS, one dismissible strip.**
  1. **The definitive one — a route this build cannot resolve.** `route.js` gains `unknownModuleSlug(hash)`: a well-formed slug with no module behind it. The tolerance stays (nothing throws, the fallback still happens, no deep link breaks), but the miss is now REPORTABLE, so the shell can offer a reload instead of pretending the link resolved. This is precisely what `#/notes` looked like from inside a pre-Notes build.
  2. **The general one — the served build stamp.** `vite.config.js` emits `/version.json` = `{ build }` alongside `index.html` (dev serves it from middleware), `public/_headers` gives it `Cache-Control: no-store`, and `src/app/buildSkew.js` re-reads it — twenty seconds after boot, whenever the tab is looked at again, and on a slow interval — and compares against the `__BUILD_ID__` baked into the running bundle. The focus check is the one that matters in practice: a laptop reopened the next morning.
- **⛔ IT IS SILENT WHENEVER IT CANNOT KNOW, and that is the property most of the tests are about.** A missing file, a 404, an offline network, a non-JSON body, a dev build on either side — every one resolves to "no opinion", never to a false "you are out of date". A skew notice that cries wolf gets dismissed by reflex, and then the one that matters is invisible too.
- **It never reloads on its own.** A thin dismissible strip with a Reload button (`reloadFresh`, the same cache-busting reload the stale-chunk guard uses, so it actually lands on the new build) — never a modal and never a forced navigation, because the user may be mid-sentence. A dismissal is scoped to the build it was dismissed FOR, so one shrug does not silence every future deploy.
- Guards: `test/buildSkew.test.js` (15 — the skew decision, the dismissal scoping, every failure shape of the fetch, the focus re-check, unsubscribe) · `test/route.test.js` (+2 — the miss reported in all three hash shapes, silent for every known route including the shorthands) · `ui-audit/verify-notes.mjs` §21 drives both halves in a real browser: a route slug this build has no module for raises the strip, and a fabricated newer `/version.json` plus a tab-focus raises it too, each dismissible.
