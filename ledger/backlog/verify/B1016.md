`[ ]` **⏳ merged — V462 PENDING (Verify: live — zoom-/data-density-dependent rendering).** Pond design numbers are now their own tier below the detail tier.
- **Root cause (AUDIT-FIRST, three separate call sites, none of them sharing a rule):** (a) the berm tag (`SitePlanner.jsx` pond-berm-ring-layer) drew at a **hardcoded `fontSize: 11` bold with a fixed 3px halo** behind a flat `view.ppf > 0.16` gate — i.e. visible at essentially every working zoom regardless of how thin the berm band actually rendered; (b) the contour elevation callouts (`pondContourEls`, `WS …` / `Floor …`) drew at a **fixed `fontSize: 9`** with a fixed 2.6px halo, gated only on `detailLabelVisible(pondMinPlanDimension, ppf)` — keyed to the whole POND, so a big basin passed the gate at overview zoom and painted its floor elevation there; (c) the map label stack's `Holds … · N′ rim to floor` line rode the same whole-pond gate. Net effect at ppf ≈ 0.35 on the owner's pond: an 8.2 ft berm on a 3:1 exterior face is a **~9px band on screen** carrying a full-size 11px bold tag, and a 16.2 ft basin's floor elevation printed over a **~17px** side-slope band — numbers physically larger than the features they annotate, and louder than the building dimension numbers (which correctly shrink and drop out via B911's `dimFontPx` + `dimCalloutVisible`).
- **Fix (`lib/labelLayout.js`, one shared pure tier + three call sites):** new `pondParamLabelVisible(featureFt, ppf)` / `pondParamFontPx(ppf, basePx)` / `POND_PARAM_LABEL_MIN_PX = 40`. Two defences, both self-tuning (no per-zoom breakpoints), mirroring B149 + B911: **(1) visibility** — the number reveals only once the BAND it measures projects to ≥40px, where the band is the berm's exterior face run (`EXT_BERM_SLOPE × bermH`) for the berm tag and the interior side-slope run (`slope × depth-below-top-of-bank`) for an elevation / storage callout, so a taller berm or deeper basin reveals sooner and the number appears exactly when the grading it describes is legible; the floor sits deliberately ABOVE `DETAIL_LABEL_MIN_PX` (30) — a pond design parameter is a rung quieter than a sidewalk width; **(2) size** — both fonts (and their halo strokes) now ride the shared `dimFontScale`, so a big feature that does reveal early can never paint a full-size number over a small-on-screen pond. The pond's **identity** — its name and footprint acreage — stays on the normal overview label stack, exactly like a building's name + sf, and the depth RINGS keep their own sub-pixel gate and still draw at overview zoom: we hid the numbers, not the grading. The `⚠ Too deep — max ≈ N′` callout is untouched (LOUD-FAILURE: an infeasibility warning is not a parameter).
