- **THE OWNER'S CONDITION, ANSWERED IN THREE PARTS, each unit-tested rather than asserted in prose:**
  1. **THE PAVEMENT MATH KNOWS.** `roundaboutArea` is the **ANNULUS**, never the disc — the central island is landscaped, and counting it would overstate impervious cover, which is exactly what a detention volume is priced off. The annulus is added to the owning road's paved figure (ONE road owns it, so two legs can never double-count), and `legTrimFor` shortens each approach so its strip stops at the circle instead of paving the island. **That trim is the HALF-CHORD √(R²−half²), not the radius** — trimming by the radius stops the CENTERLINE on the circle and leaves the strip's square end face poking past the arc at its outer corners, and the lens-shaped void between face and arc closes as a genuine phantom HOLE in the dissolved pavement (measured: two of them, one per side, before the fix).
  2. **THE CURB ENGINE KNOWS.** The circulatory roadway is contributed into the SAME `dissolveRings` union the rest of the road network goes through, as arc **SECTORS whose union IS the annulus** — union-only, which is the one operation the dissolve is proven on, so no boolean difference and no new failure mode. The result is ONE region with ONE continuous curb outline and the central island as a real PolyTree hole, and every leg clusters with its circle. Curb returns are arcs **tangent to both the leg edge and the circle** (fillet centre at R+Rr from the centre and half+Rr from the leg axis — two conditions, one exact placement), omitted rather than faked when a leg is too wide to solve.
  3. **THE DESIGN VEHICLE DECIDES THE SIZE.** `roundaboutDiameterFor` = 2 × the class's design-vehicle turning radius + one circulatory width, clamped into that class's published FHWA / NCHRP-672 band (mini 45–90 for an auto aisle; single-lane 90–180 for a fire lane; 130–180 for a WB-67 truck route). An auto drive aisle, a fire lane and a WB-67 truck route get **three different circles**, and that is a test, not a claim. **`public` deliberately takes a FIXED band value:** its `minRadius` is a horizontal CURVE radius derived from design speed (~185 ft at 25 mph), not a turning radius, and feeding it to the formula would put a 390 ft circle on a site road.
- **BONDED BY CONSTRUCTION.** The roundabout is stored on the ROAD at a terminus (`el.roundabout = {end, d}`) and **records no position at all** — the centre, the leg bearings and the curb returns are re-derived from the road's current `pts` every frame. So moving or re-aligning the road carries it with nothing to keep in sync, which is this repo's own ROWS-CANONICAL / assembly-integrity lesson applied to geometry rather than to rows. A **second road** whose terminus lands on the same node joins as another LEG rather than creating a second circle, and the **larger** declared diameter wins (a truck leg and an aisle leg at one node must get the truck's circle — the smaller cannot turn the bigger vehicle).
- **THE MENU ITEM IS WHERE HE ALREADY RIGHT-CLICKS** — the road-terminus branch of the same vertex menu that hosts "Add / Delete control point" (`onCanvasVtxContextCapture`). The diameter is then editable in place through the **shared inline numeric editor** every other dimension on this canvas uses — never a dialog (owner rule 2026-06-17) — and an out-of-range value is clamped with a named warning rather than silently accepted.
- **PDF-PARITY holds by construction, and is stated rather than assumed:** the island is drawn INSIDE the road-network region group with `data-export="road-network"`, and `buildExportSvg` clones the live `<svg>` stripping only `data-export="skip"` — so the sheet carries the same node the screen does. There is no second render path to drift. (The live PDF/PNG check is still owed — see `V690`.)
- **A TRAP WORTH THE COMMENT IT CARRIES:** a curb return's closing polygon must run through the CORNER, never straight between the two tangent points. A direct chord passes inside the fillet circle, so the ring self-intersects and the union **silently drops it** — the return then floats as its own island instead of joining the pavement. Found by measurement (5 regions where there should be 1), not by reading.
