### B2671297 — Map flashes on zoom: MapLibre 6 worker never loads, road names fall back to the raster layer [site-planner] (bug)
- Reported by owner 2026-11-23 on Goose Creek › Phase II - Base Case: "the map is flashing a ton when I zoom in or out".
- Cause: #2993 (maplibre-gl 5.24 → 6.19) — v6 looks for its worker next to its bundled chunk; the build emits none, request returns the SPA index.html, vector labels silently fell back to the raster road-names layer (reloads a fresh tile set after every zoom step).
- Fix (PR #2995, merged, live as build 22cbce4): `vectorLabelLayer.js` imports `maplibre-gl/dist/maplibre-gl-worker.mjs?url` and calls `setWorkerUrl` first. Guard: test/maplibreWorkerUrl.test.js.
- Live check 2026-11-23 (owner's signed-in Chrome, /version.json read = 22cbce4 in the same session, Goose Creek › Phase II): after zooming in a `.leaflet-gl-layer` canvas mounts, stays mounted through 2 zoom-ins + 2 zoom-outs via the buttons, no raster 0.85 fallback layer, no console error. NOT proven: the glyphs actually drawn (WebGL canvas read back blank without preserveDrawingBuffer), pinch zoom, and the owner's own eyes.
- Verify: live — stopping rule: owner confirms the flashing is gone on his machine. Parked here until he does.
