All three gestures are computed in Leaflet CONTAINER-pixel space, which is why no unit conversion is needed: Web Mercator is angle-preserving and north-up, so a screen-pixel distance ratio equals the real-world scale ratio and a screen-pixel rotation angle equals a true-north rotation — proven, not assumed, by a dedicated test (`overlayGeoref.test.js`'s 90°/180°-rotation cases, which assert the top edge turns toward east and never toward a mirror).
