- **⛔ TWO REFUTATIONS FIRST, because both were named in the brief and both are wrong.** (a) **`interiorFitter` is EXONERATED** — it runs 312×/gesture and costs **0.5 ms total**; B221761's memo hits exactly as designed, so the brief's landing zone was not the cost. (b) **THERE IS NO SUPERLINEAR PER-VERTEX LAW.** §5.5 decimated the rings and preserved the *bounding box* — but a bounding box is not a shape, and decimation removes **concavity**, which is what loads clipper's intersection sweep. **Collinear midpoint insertion** (same area, same perimeter, same bbox, same drawn path — only vertex count rises) measures an exponent of **0.20**: 16× the vertices for 1.75× the cost, and `maxInwardOffset` gets *cheaper* (−0.21) because clipper's `CleanPolygon` drops collinear points for free. §5.5's "920 ms per vertex" was `49,806 ÷ 54` — a whole recurrence attributed to the one variable that arm happened to name. **§5.5's headline needs re-reading exactly the way it re-read §5.4's Finding 3.**
- **CAUSE, counted rather than timed.** A JS self-time profile put **55,631 of 55,760 ms** in clipper-lib's intersection sweep; an invocation count on the probe build said why — **`offsetInward` ran 275,184× in ONE pan** (137,592 per pond), `maxInwardOffset` 8,736×, `pondElevations` 5,616×, **`pondStageModel` 312× = 156 per pond**. Two stacked defects, neither a formula: **(1)** `drainFacts()` in `SitePlanner.jsx` is *deliberately* GATING rather than memoising, so with the Yield panel docked the whole bundle rebuilds every render — 156 rebuilds per pond per pan, 155 of them bit-for-bit identical to the first; **(2)** `pondElevations` was re-derived **inside its own consumer** — `stageTable` asks once and twice more *per band* via `areaAtElev`, so a 7-band pond paid the 29-clipper-execute pinch-off search 15 times for a constant. That is the term that *looked* superlinear: a constant re-derived a linear number of times.
- **FIX — memoisation only, NO formula touched.** `offsetInward` / `maxInwardOffset` (`pondOffset.js`) and `pondElevations` / `pondStageModel` (`pondStageModel.js`) keyed on the ring's **identity** (the planner replaces `points` wholesale on edit, never mutates in place) plus det/opts by **value**. The key IS the inputs, so the cache cannot serve a stale engineering number — which is the exact property `drainFacts`'s gating-not-memoising comment exists to protect. `identityCache` gained a `cap` parameter (default 16 unchanged); one ring is legitimately asked for ~28 offsets by the binary search, and an under-sized cap clears on every call and pays the keying for nothing.
