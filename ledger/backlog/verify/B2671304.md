### B2671304 (×2) — Changing a wall parking field's Stall depth / Stall width / Drive aisle left the buffer / road / trailer row beyond it floating (detached landscaping buffer) `[Site Planner / wall layers]` (bug) #site-plan
- **Report (owner, 2026-11-23, Goose Creek › PII - TAS R2, Building 2, 746×500 cross-dock):** the landscaping buffer on an end wall "detached" from the parking.
- **Measured on the real plan (read-only):** end wall chain = sidewalk 5 ft, parking 64 ft, buffer 5 ft. Buffer sat 18 ft past the parking's outer edge. Draw order was ruled out (the gap is geometric, not a covering road). #2994 (stall width), #2995, #3003, #2998 do not touch layer offsets.
- **Root cause:** `SitePlanner.jsx` `setParkCfg` (Car Parking Properties: Stall depth / Stall width / Drive aisle) resized the field outward but never repacked the flush layers beyond it; `growParking`, the row-count setter and wall-layer depth edits all call `repackFlushBeyond`.
- **Fix:** `setParkCfg` now runs `relayoutWallKids` + `repackFlushBeyond` for a wall-bonded field. Guard: e2e `side-park-cfg-repacks-buffer` (red on main, green with the fix).
- **Verify:** sandbox for the rule; live check pending on a throwaway duplicate. Existing already-detached buffers (like this plan's) heal on the next depth edit or "+ / −" of that wall; not auto-healed on load.
- **Stopping rule:** closes when a signed-in live check on a throwaway duplicate shows buffer flush after a Drive-aisle edit.

- **Recurrence (owner, 2026-11-24):** "pushing out or expanding the building will move the buffer out, but then shrinking the building doesn't bring it back." The 2026-11-23 fix only covered a parking cfg edit; the real cause was one level down.
- **Root cause (×2):** `lib/dogEar.js` `wallStripBoxFor` placed a flush buffer with a ONE-DIRECTIONAL clamp, `perp = max(current, inboard + depth/2)` ("pushed out … never pulled in"). Every building resize (`refitChildrenIn` → `relayoutWallKids` → `sidewalkFullRunPatch`) and the load heal (`siteModel.normalizeWallKids`) went through it, so a grow pushed the buffer out and a shrink left it stranded at the pushed-out position. `repackFlushBeyond` (exact, both ways) only ran on a few panel edits.
- **Fix (×2):** one shared pure pack `lib/dogEar.js` `repackFlushLayers` / `repackAllFlushLayers`: a wall's flush layers (landscape buffer without `sidewalkSide`, wall road, wall trailer row) are placed exactly where the inboard layers end (`inboardReach`), keeping their outward order, perpendicular axis only; a wall carrying a dock-chain member is left to `relayoutSide`. Called at the end of `relayoutWallKids` (every refit: building edge/corner drag, typed dimension, sidewalk/parking edits, rotation), at the end of `normalizeWallKids` (load heal: already-detached buffers snap back on open, reported via onHeal kind `flush-layer`), and `SitePlanner.repackFlushBeyond` is now a thin wrapper over it (no second implementation). Same array returned when nothing moves.
- **Guards:** `test/flushLayerFollowsEdge.test.js` (86 cases: rot 0/33/90/180/247 × every wall × with/without parking × road beyond, grow then shrink on the same and opposite wall; load heal and refit of a 48 ft detached buffer; idempotent; dock chain untouched) — 84 red on main, all green. e2e `buffer-follows-building-resize` (rotated 30°, real edge-handle drag: open heal, grow, shrink, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, reload) — red on main exactly at the shrink, green with the fix.
- **Stopping rule (×2):** closes when a signed-in live check on a throwaway duplicate of the owner's plan shows the Building 2 buffer flush after open and after growing then shrinking the building.
