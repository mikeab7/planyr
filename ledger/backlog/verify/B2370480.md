**What he gets.** Open the Land panel, or "Pick county lots", or Draw / Split / Combine / Edit corners, and the parcel geometry becomes the TOP of the drawing: the county lots on offer (outline + lot numbers) and the plan's own lots (fill, outline, selection weight, setback ring) paint over buildings, roads, markups, callouts and text boxes, the "draw above the plan" references, lifted GIS fills and every GIS line layer (flood boundaries, contours, pipelines…). A press follows the draw order: in Pick county lots a click on a lot covered by a building / markup / reference / measurement picks the LOT (not the thing over it). Close the tool and everything is back exactly where the fixed model puts it.

**How (one decision, three moves — `lib/mapStack.js` "PARCELS IN FRONT").**
- `parcelsOnTop({leftPanel, floating, identifyMode, tool, mergePick, boundaryEdit})` is the ONE function; `SitePlanner.jsx` reads it once (`parcelsFront`). It is a pure function of state — nothing is stored, no layer's saved order / lift changes, no new control.
- **(1)** the plan's setback rings + parcels are built ONCE (`parcelTierNodes`, moved verbatim out of the render) and rendered in exactly one of two places: the normal slot, or `<g data-parcel-tier="top">` after the annotations and before the chips / drafts / handle layer (document order is paint order in SVG, so hit-testing follows it for free — their grab strokes are above too).
- **(2)** the county lots on offer mount in new panes (`parcelPickPane`) hosted in a `foreignObject` INSIDE the plan SVG directly under that group (the lifted-GIS band's shape: mirrored transforms, registration shift undone, `pointer-events:none`). `parcelDisplay.js` / `parcelLotLabelLayer.js` take an optional `pane` (default unchanged, so the Map tab is untouched); a missing host logs loudly and falls back.
- **(3)** the GIS LINE band (a host OUTSIDE the SVG, above it) sinks to the backdrop's level while the tool is up (`lineHostZ`), so no stroke can draw over a parcel.
- **Click-through:** `handleIdentifyCapture` (capture phase, after the Alt stack-picker, before vertex capture) hands every primary press in Pick county lots to `onBgDown` — the function a press on bare ground reaches — so the ack ring, click-vs-drag pan, Space-pan and attach/align flows behave as on empty canvas. Left alone: the handle layer, text fields. A double-click opens nothing drawn over a lot (`featureDoubleAction`).
- **PDF-PARITY:** the sheet shows the NORMAL order — the export clone puts the moved group back in its slot and strips the screen-only markers (asserted on the real built sheet; mutation-checked red without the re-slot).
- **Not changed (owner constraints):** parcels still never move outside Edit parcels (`startMoveParcel` untouched — a press on a lot's line selects, a drag pans); the "zoom in to see the lines" hint and the view do not move; #10 (creation-order stacking, selection lifts) is untouched — this is a mode-gated exception for the PARCEL layer only.

**Stated trade-offs.** (a) While the Land panel is open, a lot's line is above a building, so pressing a building's edge exactly where it coincides with the lot line selects the lot — that is "hit-testing follows the draw order", asked for. (b) A callout the user forced to "absolute front" (B806080) stays above the parcel outlines — it is the user's own explicit front-most choice. (c) A reference or GIS layer sits UNDER the plan's lots only while the tool is up.
