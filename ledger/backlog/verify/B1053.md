- Verify: live — parked in ⏳ Verify as **V486** (`Blocker: real-data`: the owner's signed-in Tsakiris / Bain plans). Sandbox + headless proof is recorded on the V486 entry.
- Origin: filed 2026-07-28 from chat
- **Cause — confirmed, not assumed (the item's prime suspect was right).** The inspector mounted its single ⚡ Optimize button with `statusCards.findIndex((c) => tone !== "ok")`, i.e. on the FIRST NON-GREEN verdict row. B1031 deliberately kept an over-dug ledger GREEN ("a surplus must never out-shout a shortfall") and B1032/B1036 demoted the remaining amber states, so on an all-green panel there was no row for it to ride and it never mounted. Reproduced headless before editing.
- **A SECOND half nobody had spotted:** `designPond()` opened with `if (!needsDet && !needsMit) return;` — so even had the button mounted on a covered pond, clicking it would have done nothing and said nothing. A silent no-op is the exact LOUD-FAILURE prohibition.
- **A THIRD, found by the live harness after the fix:** the right-size search passed the SCREENING groundwater ceiling (a 5-ft Gulf-Coast seasonal-high estimate) as a HARD constraint against a depth ladder starting at 6 ft, so every candidate was rejected on a basin the owner had already drawn 12 ft deep — the optimizer would have proposed nothing anywhere on the Gulf Coast. The ceiling can no longer veto the depth already on the plan (the inspector treats that as a SOFT geotech warning, and the optimizer must not be stricter than the panel).
- **Fix — decoupled from tone permanently.** New pure `lib/pondOptimizeAffordance.js`: availability is POSSIBILITY only (drawn ring · known requirement · resolved usable/dead split) and takes no tone/over-provision input at all, by construction. The verdict now picks the JOB (`solve` when short, `rightsize` when covered), never the availability.
- **Owner amendment, same day (folded in): no button to hunt for.** The affordance is ONE apply-gated suggestion line, present only when a materially smaller basin exists, stating the delta plainly (depth → land freed, buildable sf, dirt $). Nothing materially better → renders NOTHING (silence is information, and it serves PANEL-BREVITY). Never auto-applies and never reshapes drawn geometry — drawing is authorship.
- **Guards:** `test/pondOptimizeAffordance.test.js` (16 cases — the all-green and over-dug states asserted explicitly, plus a test that passing tone-shaped keys changes nothing); `ui-audit/verify-pond-optimizer-affordance.mjs` drives the REAL app logged-out into the all-green state and asserts the affordance renders. The superseded tone-coupled assertions in `pondV3PrE` / `pondV3PrG` / `pondInspectorLayout` / `pondVerdict` were rewritten to forbid the coupling rather than require it.
