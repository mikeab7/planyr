- **THE FIX: the floors are gone and the page grows on all four edges.** `moveAnchorPoint` (`max(edgePad,x)`/`max(0,y)`), `placeAnchor` (same pair) and `resizeBox`'s west/north walls all dropped; `moveSelection`'s `min: 0` default opened. They return a POSITIVE DISTANCE rather than a negative coordinate, so no call site has to negate a sign.
- **GROWTH IS EXTRA PADDING ON THE SHEET, NOT A REWRITE OF ANYBODY'S COORDINATES.** The page reaches outward; the document's own x/y never move. That is what makes this migration-free — every stored note, including his `y:-21` scratch anchor, keeps its numbers — and it is also what makes **B1370545 (NEW-5, placing something level with the title)** fall out of it rather than needing a coordinate change.

Its own note in `notesAnchorNode.js` records why, so it is not restored from a stale comment.

A `useLayoutEffect` keyed on the three growth values folds the MEASURED change in the body's own `offsetLeft`/`offsetTop` into the scroller in the same frame, before paint. An assumed constant would not do: the sheet's alignment flips from centred to left the first time anything grows.
