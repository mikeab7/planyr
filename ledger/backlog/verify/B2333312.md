### B2333312 — NEW-1: Grading is a per-plan switch, OFF by default, inside the Grading panel `[Site Planner / grading]` (bug) #site-planner #ui #performance  *(owner chat block 2026-10-19 — amends B2315856, B2315857, B2319137; PR #2380)*
`[~]` **⏳ merged — V1763840 PENDING (Verify: live — real-project-data: Goose Creek › PII - 2026.09.15 Expeditors).** What he saw (build 2fb9e79): Cut & Fill bands, "35/40/37 ft past the setback" pills, "fill 3.9" corner pills and a Grading panel full of suggested FFEs on a plan he never graded. Cause: the controller was mounted for every plan the owner opens, solved an auto FFE per building, and `gsEls.padElevFt` took that suggestion as the finish elevation the plan's earthwork and drainage price off — so a suggestion silently became the plan's numbers.
- **Fix:** `settings.grading.enabled` (stored with the plan; syncs; a duplicate inherits it). `grPlanOn = gradingOn && enabled === true`; `useGradingBase`, `grOn`, the controller mount, `gsEls.padElevFt`, the grading pack/host ids and the canvas/Layers rows all hang off it. The owner gate (`gradingEnabled()` over `is_admin()`) is untouched and still decides whether the dock item and the switch exist.
- **Off:** no controller (no DEM fetch, sweep, auto-FFE solve, rock fetch, alternative pricing or fix search; the model is null); `gsInputs` equal a non-owner account's; no bands, daylight line, flag pills/dots, corner pills or level-pad line; the two Layers rows are hidden; the panel shows the switch and one line, "Turn this on to suggest finish elevations and show cut and fill for this plan." Turning it off clears everything in the same render; typed FFEs are never touched.
- **Proof (sandbox):** `test/gradingPerPlanSwitch.test.js` (cfg default, grOn wiring, controller mount, non-owner inputs, panel markup off/on) + `ui-audit/verify-grading-harness.mjs` §switch (switch off → no layers, no pills, no cards, model null, one line; on → the round-2 picture returns). Existing grading suites updated for the new default.
- **Stopping rule:** closes when V1763840 records a dated PASS on the owner's account against the served build.
