### B2448816 — Crop drawing spills outside its box when zoomed in and covers the Polygon switch, Cancel / Done and the zoom toolbar `[Shared / crop tool]` (bug) #site-planner #ui #comps  *(owner report 2026-10-29, demoing: "if I zoomed in too much, then the crop expanded outside of the box to where I couldn't even hit the polygon button… obviously that needs to be fixed to where it stays within the box." Minted from this branch's reserved block B2448816–B2448831 · V1892320–V1892335. DEDUPE-FIRST: no existing item — B2254626 / B1924320 are the crop dialog's SIZE, B2268672 its redesign; none covers the drawing overflowing the box. Net-new.)*
**Stopping rule:** closes when V1892320 records, signed in on planyr.io against the deployed build, that a deep zoom on the crop leaves the shape switch, Cancel / Done and the toolbar clickable on the Site tab and Map/Comps surfaces; a failure there re-opens THIS item with `(×2)` (STANDING RULE #2 — a null is a finding, not a disposition).
**Cause (read from the code, then reproduced red in a real browser on main before any fix):** `ImageCropTool`'s chrome layer (crop outline + move surface + grips) was deliberately `overflow: visible` so a grip on the crop's edge would not lose half its grab area. In Rectangle mode those are plain absolutely-positioned divs placed from the picture's current view, so on a near-full-sheet crop zoomed in (≈900%) the move surface was ~10,000 px wide, started thousands of px above the canvas, and — painting after the header row in the same dialog — covered and took the presses of the Rectangle | Polygon switch, Cancel and Done (measured: `elementFromPoint` at each control's centre returned the move surface; the outline's box reached −8,244 px above `crop-canvas`). The grips left their own hit areas the same way. The floating toolbar survived only because it is a z-indexed sibling. Polygon mode was NOT affected (one outer `<svg>`, clipped by the browser) — checked, not assumed.
**Fix — nothing the crop draws may EXIST outside the drawing area (not merely be hidden):** new pure `shared/sitePlans/lib/cropChrome.js` (`visibleRect`, `gripBox`, `dotCenter`, `clipSegment`). The rectangle's outline / move surface is cut to the part that is on screen and a side that ran off is simply not drawn (the rule-of-thirds lines are positioned against the cut box); a grip is shown only while its point is on screen and **slides inward so it is always whole** (that is what the unclipped layer was protecting — now kept, with the clip as a backstop only); an edge-midpoint grip rides the middle of the part of its edge that IS on screen, so a zoomed-in crop can still be resized from any visible edge; a corner / vertex that is off screen shows no grip until panned to (no ghosts); a polygon's edge hit-strips are clipped to the area; the chrome layer is now `overflow: hidden`. The floating toolbar still wins a press over a grip it sits on (unchanged, intended).
**Verified (sandbox):** `ui-audit/verify-crop-contained.mjs` — three surfaces (Site tab Overlays → Crop; Map/Comps Adjust → Edit crop; Map/Comps add-a-site-plan → Crop…), each wide and narrow, Rectangle and Polygon: every control answers its own press, no box of the crop's drawing reaches past `crop-canvas`, every grip shown is whole and grabbable through a 70-step zoom sweep, a real click on Polygon switches the mode, a real drag on the visible grip still resizes. **RED on main (all three surfaces: Polygon switch / Cancel / Done blocked, boxes thousands of px outside), GREEN after.** Known-good arms (toolbar answers before zooming; deep zoom reached; a grip sampled at the canvas edge) — a run that cannot see its subject says VOID. `test/cropChrome.test.js` (geometry + source guards: chrome layer never `overflow: visible` again). Existing crop suites unchanged and green. Screenshots: `docs/pr-shots/`.
**Owner crop rules kept:** one reset only, no "Trim by feet", the editor fully modal, right-click add / delete point, Edit crop works on a locked overlay.
