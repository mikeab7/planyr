### B2515969 — In emulated iPhone WebKit a picture of real weight is sometimes NOT PAINTED (an alt-text pill where it belongs) until the next repaint — Notes "Picture" and an ordinary pasted PNG alike `[Notes]` (bug, unconfirmed on a real device) #notes #mobile #export  *(found by B2494096's phone pass, 2026-10-29)*

**What was measured (all in Playwright WebKit with the iPhone 15 descriptor, 8 trials each unless stated).** Notes ▸ Paste options ▸ Picture, signed in on planyr.io and on a local build: the image decodes (`complete`, `naturalWidth` 904, a canvas read finds ink), is stored, is undone by one tap — and on screen is an alt-text pill ("Pasted table") in about 6 of 8 trials, until something repaints (a blur, a tap on blank paper). **It is not Picture-specific:** a plain PNG inserted the ordinary way (`insertNoteImages`) paints 4/4 when it is tiny and solid, and flakes the same ~2/8 once it is a real-weight image (a 40-KB noisy PNG). **Chromium with the same phone descriptor painted 6/6.** A fresh `<img>` carrying the same data URL painted fine outside the editor.
**Ruled out (so nobody retries them):** decode-ahead via `new Image().decode()`, `img.decoding="sync"`, hiding the figure until decoded, `img.decode()` then an opacity flip, a box-background flip, a transform / `will-change` nudge, re-setting `src`, cloning the `<img>`, blurring right after insert (4/8), Escape (1/8) — none moved the rate; this is not the image's node view and not a DPR-3 artefact (also flakes at DPR 1). Nothing shipped for it.
**Why it is not closed on that null (STANDING RULE #2).** An emulated, headless WebKit is not Safari: a lost repaint here may be this engine's compositor and may not exist on a phone, and the instrument cannot say which.
