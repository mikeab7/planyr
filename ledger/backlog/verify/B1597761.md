- **NO PARALLEL SELECTION SYSTEM WAS BUILT, which the brief named as a requirement.** The band still writes the same `selection` Set that a single click writes, painted by the same transaction-driven `paint` effect, released by the same `clearSelection`. Nothing in `notesKeyScope.js` or the anchor selection path was touched — the two-stage click model (B434416, repaired in #1665) is byte-for-byte unchanged, and `ui-audit/verify-notes-box-selection.mjs` runs clean on this branch.
- **⛔ THE BAND'S BASELINE IS FROZEN AT THE PRESS, and that IS a behaviour change worth naming.** The old additive path read the live selection on every move, so the previous frame's answer was the next frame's input: a box swept up and then swept back OUT of a shrinking band stayed selected. Against a frozen baseline the band is honest in both directions. With nothing selected — the ordinary case — a Shift-drag is simply a replace.
- **Building a union across two bands is still reachable**, so nothing was lost by the modifier moving: Shift-CLICK on an individual box toggles it in or out of the selection (`toggleSelection`, unchanged).
- **Move together, delete together, release together** — all pre-existing (B421494/B590018), all re-proven on this branch rather than assumed: three boxes swept, dragged by one member, all three moved by the identical delta while the box outside the band did not move at all; one Delete removed the whole set; Escape and a click on empty grey each released all three.
- **The old harness was updated, not left to rot.** `ui-audit/verify-notes-marquee.mjs`'s five band gestures now hold Shift; its boundary section deliberately does NOT, because a press that does not travel is a PLACE with or without the modifier. **58/58 green**, including its zoomed-in window.
