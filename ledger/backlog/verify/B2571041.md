### B2571041 — NEW-2: the grading corner cut/fill tags never sit under the selected building's purple "+" / dock-wall buttons `[Site Planner / grading paint + building chrome]` (bug) #site-planner #ui  *(owner chat block 2026-10-30 11:40 AM CT, Georgia Test Site › Concept A, zoomed to the south end at about the 50-ft scale: the "fill 22.1" / "fill 26.0" tags at the two south corners read "fil⊕22.1" under the purple bump-out "+". DEDUPE-FIRST: no earlier item.)*
`[x]` **IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-30 — ⏳ merged — V2019696 PENDING (Verify: live — zoom-dependent rendering, owner-only grading).**
- **Cause (measured):** the pad-corner tag was placed 26 px toward the building's centre and the corner "+" button 20 px toward the same centre — the same ray, so they stacked. Screen-px on both sides, so no zoom avoids it.
- **Fix:** `lib/padCornerTag.js` — `placeCornerTag`. Nothing selected: exactly the old spot. Building SELECTED: the tag slides along the building's longer wall, away from the corner, until its box clears every button disc (corner "+", and the wall-middle "+ / −" pairs), with a thin leader + dot tying it to the EXACT corner point; a wall too short to carry it between the buttons (a small, nearly square building) falls back to the outside of the wall, then the other wall. The buttons never move. The tag layer is `pointer-events: none` (grading root + each tag group), so a tag can never take a press from a button.
- **No drift between the two:** `SitePlanner.jsx`'s `sideAddNodes` now places the corner "+" and the wall pair THROUGH `bumpButtonAnchor` / `sideButtonAnchor` (same numbers as before, 20 / 24 px), and the placer avoids the discs those same functions produce.
- **Found by the harness, not by reasoning:** the corner labels memo in `GradingController.jsx` did not depend on the selection, so on a one-building plan select → deselect left the tags in the selected spot; `selBuildingId` is now in its deps.
- **Tests:** `test/padCornerTag.test.js` (zero box/disc intersection across 7 buildings × 4 rotations × 7 zooms × 4 corners × 4 texts, >500 cases; the owner's 1,566 × 650 ft building at every zoom the buttons render; RED PROOF that the pre-fix placement collides at the same corners; deselected = the old spot exactly; non-interactive; source guards that the planner uses the shared anchors). Real browser: `ui-audit/verify-grading-corner-tags.mjs` (known-bad arm collides, selected = zero overlap, every on-screen button answers a hit-test at its centre, deselect restores) → PNGs `ui-audit/screenshots/grading/corner-tags-{selected-south,deselected-south,selected-building}.png`.
- **Follow-up 2026-10-30 (same session, after #2735 merged):** the drive-in ramp PR (#2736, B2566544–B2566547) merged just ahead of this one and added a small slate ramp "+" beside every wall pair and beside each purple corner "+" — discs the placer did not know about, so a tag could have landed on one. `lib/padCornerTag.js` now owns `rampWallAnchor` / `rampCornerAnchor` / `RAMP_BTN_R` (the planner draws the ramp buttons through them; the one inline `RAMP_BTN_R` and its two offset formulas are gone) and `buttonDiscs` includes them (worst case: a ramp on every wall, one toward each wall's middle at every corner). Same sweep, same zero-overlap result with the ramp discs in.
- **Known limit, stated:** the harness draws the buttons through the shared anchors rather than mounting the real planner (the grading layer is owner-gated and the sandbox account is not the owner). The real-planner arm is V2019696. Hover: with NOTHING selected the buttons also show for the building under the cursor; the tags stay at the corner then (the owner's rule: "when nothing is selected they return") — so a hover-only overlap is possible and is NOT claimed fixed.
