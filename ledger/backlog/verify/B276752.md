- **THE PORTFOLIO SHAPE IS THE FINDING, and it is why this was never a Bain oddity.** Queried live at all 28 Texas origins: **8 inside city limits · 16 unincorporated inside the Houston ETJ · 4 unincorporated with no ETJ.** Twenty of twenty-eight — 71% — are unincorporated, and **17 of those 20 have a city polygon within a kilometre**, so the frontage-sliver trap is loaded on nearly all of them.
- **ROOT CAUSE, and it is not the one that was filed.** The header identify reduces a site to `representativeRing` — the single LARGEST active parcel — and asks which city THAT lot's centroid is in. **Twelve of the 28 sites are assemblages** (Goose Creek 16 parcels, 8 South 19, Martini 14, Tsakiris 9, Schiel 9, Will Clayton 6…), so on those the governing jurisdiction was decided by a coin flip weighted by lot size. Measured: at **Tsakiris** two of the nine parcels sit inside Katy's limits, the biggest happens to be one of them, and the pill printed a bare **"City of Katy · Waller County"**. At **Goose Creek** the opposite: the biggest lot is outside Baytown so the boundary query returned NO city at all, while **six of the sixteen parcels are inside Baytown** — a fact the app could not see from one ring.
- **FIXED — containment is asked of EVERY active parcel** (`parcelProbePoints`, largest first until the tested lots cover 98% of the drawn area, hard cap 16), and the answer is now FOUR states rather than two: **`in`** (a city holds the whole site → it leads, unqualified) · **`partial`** (it holds some parcels and not others → *"Part in City of X / part unincorporated"*, ⚑ straddle) · **`none`** (→ **Unincorporated**, the answer 20 of 28 sites need) · **`unknown`**. Cost is unchanged for the common case: one MULTIPOINT query replaces the one centroid query, and the per-parcel attribution pass only runs when a city is actually involved.
- **AND THE HOLE THE OWNER PREDICTED, closed exactly as he stated it.** With containment `unknown`, `formatJurisdictionBadge` fell back to leading with the RING UNION — so a failed containment lookup rendered as a positive containment answer, with no qualifier. That is the reported *"City of Baytown"* shape. A city may now lead ONLY on a positive containment answer; an unknown says **"City limits · couldn't check"** and every ring city is demoted to **"· touches"**, never the site's jurisdiction. The test that asserted the old behaviour (`B793 — NO centroid answer … keeps the pre-B793 behavior`) was the assertion that let it through and is replaced.
- **⚠ ONE CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF, and it is load-bearing.** Goose Creek and Tsakiris were filed as *"unincorporated, no ETJ, city within 1 km"*. At the site ORIGIN that is right, and the sweep confirms it. Across the DRAWN SITE it is not: Baytown holds 6 of Goose Creek's 16 parcels and Katy holds 2 of Tsakiris's 9, per TxGIO. The origin is one point and the site is many — the same confusion that produced the bug. Both now read as genuine splits, which is neither "City of X" nor a flat "Unincorporated", and in both the city is never presented as the site's jurisdiction — the property the owner asked for.
