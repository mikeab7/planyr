### B2244018 — Two B978273 read functions trusted owner-writable pointers: a team member could read a teammate's private raster or review by naming it `[sitePlans / database]` (bug) #security #comps #doc-review
`[x]` Shipped with B2244017 (same migration, section 4) — found by that item's adversarial pass.
- `can_read_shared_site_plan_raster` matched `raster_key` without binding it to the overlay owner's folder; `can_read_review_via_site_plan_overlay` matched `review_id` without binding the review to the overlay owner. Both columns are writable by the overlay's owner, so a member of a team could share their own overlay naming a teammate's private raster path / review id and read it (the B491 confused-deputy shape).
- Fixed by adding the owner binding to both. **Measured on production first: 0 rows rely on either mismatch**, so every real reader keeps exactly what they see today — review visibility is unchanged for every real row (the owner's "leave reviews as they are" holds); only the forged case closes.
