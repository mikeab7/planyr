`[ ]` **⏳ merged — V455 PENDING (Verify: live — `Blocker: real-data` + PDF/export parity).** Four fixes; the third is the one that matters.
- **Pavement under a building — measured, not inferred.** Sampling the RENDERED road-network path with `SVGGeometryElement.isPointInFill` over each building footprint found ~100 sf of curb-return pavement under EACH of the two dock dog-ears (`e62sascnh`, `e64cglxqv`). B959's own comment admitted the design — "it never reaches the building — that's also z-order-guarded" — i.e. it relied on the building PAINTING OVER the pavement. That hides an overlap instead of preventing it: the pavement still exists, still counts in the paved-area total, and reads off the plan as a drive running under a dock. **Fixed:** `buildingRunLimit` clamps each junction's along-edge reach to stop short of the first building footprint on the junction's OPEN side (with a small clearance), folded into `throughAvailPos/Neg` for BOTH `driveJunctionsOf` and `teeJunctionsOf`. Pavement can no longer be under a building by construction.
- **The target rect's outline drawn across the drive mouth.** Road↔road junctions dissolve (B1006/NEW-2), but a road teeing into a PARKING FIELD or TRUCK COURT can't — the target is a different element with its own fill. So its outline was still ruled straight across the opening. **Fixed:** `roadNet.outlineCuts` maps each drive target to the dissolved pavement that crosses it; `renderElPx` then paints that rect's FILL with no stroke and draws its outline as four edges trimmed by `clipPolylineOutside`. One continuous curb around the entrance, no line across it.
- **A corner clamped below its civil minimum, silently — the root cause of "that shape is wrong".** `arcCorner` feasibility-clamps a corner's radius to half the shorter adjacent leg. That clamp is geometrically required (without it two corners overrun and the strip self-intersects) but it happened with NOTHING said: on the owner's plan a fire lane whose corner carries the 28 ft inside radius its class requires was DRAWING AT 11 ft, and a truck stub at 23 ft against a 50 ft class minimum. Not cosmetic — a compliance defect, and the visible cause of the shape he flagged. **Fixed:** new pure `roadCornerRadii` / `roadRadiusConflicts` expose requested-vs-drawn per interior vertex, and a `road-radius-flags` layer marks the offending vertex ON THE CANVAS (amber, `<title>` explaining that the leg is too short to hold the turn) instead of hiding the conflict behind selecting the road. `data-export="skip"` — review chrome never rides into a consultant's PDF. The geometry is NOT silently re-cut: resolving it is a layout decision (move the corner / lengthen the leg), which is the owner's call, not the renderer's.
- **Curb return sized by the DESIGN VEHICLE OF THE DRIVE.** `DRIVE_RETURN_SEED[kind]` keyed the return off what the drive was driving INTO; it now derives from the drive's own road class via `classReturnRadius` — the same source road↔road tees already use — with a truck-court floor. A fire lane entering employee parking still has to turn an apparatus; an auto aisle into the same field does not. The old per-junction stored seed also went stale: a value stamped onto `driveTee` at connect time kept driving geometry long after the default changed.
- **Sandbox-verified:** `ui-audit/verify-junction-obstacles.mjs` drives the REAL canvas against the owner's real element set and asserts all three through the rendered SVG (zero building overlap by point-in-fill sampling, the target outline emitted as interrupted segments, both sub-minimum corners flagged) — 4/4 green. `test/roadNetwork.test.js` +5 pure cases for the clamp exposure.
