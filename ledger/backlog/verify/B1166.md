- **Report.** Michael: *"fix how big this pop out is when I search by address, I don't think we need the legal description. Owner, parcel #, and acreage is plenty."*
- **Cause.** The card printed `Account / ID` and `Acreage (measured)` explicitly and then spread `apprRows(attrs)` — every remaining curated field. The last of those is **Legal**, and a county Legal is unbounded metes-and-bounds call text (`W2NW4 2-4-68 EXC PT LYING WITHIN COMM N4 SEC COR TH S06D25E 30.118 TPOB…`). In a fixed-width card that wraps to ten-plus lines, so the card outgrew the map controls beside it. The width was never the problem; the row count and one unbounded value were.
- **The fix.** Exactly three rows by default — **Owner · Account / ID · Acreage**, in that order. The situs address is the card's TITLE, so it no longer repeats as a row. Land value, Improvement value, Total value, Land use, Zoning, Year built and **Legal** move behind a **"More details"** disclosure, closed on every search: a real `<button aria-expanded>`, so it is keyboard- and screen-reader-reachable. **Nothing is deleted** — the honesty-stays-reachable rule; the fold is the tool, not the delete key.
- **Bounded even when opened.** The details body is height-capped and scrolls, and the Legal value is capped again inside its own row — so no county's blob can stretch the card, and the two caps are asserted in both the unit test and the browser harness. The card is keyed on the parcel in `MapFinder`, so a new search always re-mounts it CLOSED.
- **Unchanged, deliberately and byte-for-byte:** the statewide-backup and cached-copy warning banners (raw hex included — repointing those at the `--warn-*` tokens is a different change, and doing it here would silently alter how a cached-copy notice reads in dark mode), the **Plan this site** button, the narrow-viewport branch, and the no-parcel / service-unavailable states.
- **Where it lives now.** The card moved out of `MapFinder.jsx` into `components/ParcelInfoCard.jsx` (MODULE-SCOPE-COMPONENTS), and the row split is a pure exported function in `lib/appraisal.js` (`parcelCardRows`). That is what makes the height claim testable at all: the component has no DOM or Leaflet dependency, so it renders in a unit test AND mounts in a harness.
- **Verified here (sandbox — ATTEMPT-BEFORE-YOU-PARK honoured, twice over):** `test/parcelCard.test.js` (14 cases, real component output via `react-dom/server`) — three rows in order, the situs never repeated, a Legal blob 40× longer producing a **byte-identical** collapsed card, the fold absent when there is nothing to fold, and expanding revealing all seven remaining fields with both caps present. Then the real thing: **`ui-audit/verify-parcel-card-height.mjs` (new, 13/13) MEASURES it in Chromium** — collapsed 196 px with a monstrous Legal and 196 px with a one-line Legal (the blob adds no height at all), a real click on the fold reveals the Legal text, expanded stays bounded at 386 px, and the phone branch still renders three rows full-width. lint 0 errors · full suite 6,708 · build green · bundle budgets within ceiling (+1.7 KB total, attributable to the new component).
