- Verify: live — V121985 (3DEP is ERR_CONNECTION_RESET from Chromium in this sandbox, so no contour line can be made to paint here at all)
- Origin: owner chat block, 2026-08-09 — filed and shipped the same session.
- **THE CAUSE, DIAGNOSED RATHER THAN ASSUMED — and it is neither of the two candidates the brief listed.** New instrument `ui-audit/diagnose-layer-gate-flash.mjs` samples the backdrop map's zoom continuously from plan-open to settle, stamps every terrain DEM request with **the map zoom at the moment it was issued**, and counts contour polylines on the same clock. Measured on the pre-fix build, twice: the map holds **17.25**, then **15.08**. Four DEM grid pulls went out at **+290 ms, at map zoom 17.25**, while the plan settles at **15.08 — below the z16 gate**.
- **STATED PLAINLY: the gate was answered at a zoom the plan was never at.** A plan opens on the default view (ppf 0.35 ≈ Leaflet zoom 17.25 at this latitude, comfortably past the gate), and the whole-site framing — `requestFit`, 120 ms after the workspace becomes active — then drops it to whatever the tract needs. Every layer admitted in that window fetched, traced and painted, and the terrain pipeline then **CORRECTLY** cleared it on the first post-fit `moveend`. **Nothing was broken and no paint was wrong; the TIMING OF THE QUESTION was.** The brief's two candidates were "painting at the initial map zoom before the view settles" (right in shape, wrong in mechanism — it is not the map's zoom that is stale, it is that nothing waited for the framing) and "cached tiles drawing ahead of the gate" (refuted — the cache is what makes the paint INSTANT on his machine, but a cold run reproduces the wrong-zoom question with no cache involved at all).
- **THE FIX — one latch, not a timer.** No layer is ADMITTED until the backdrop map has committed the FRAMED view. `viewFramed` alone is not enough (it goes true one render BEFORE the commit lands), so the test is that the committed zoom has caught up with the drawing's own; `layerGateReady` is a one-shot latch, which is what keeps a zoom gesture from re-entering the overlay sync every frame. It gates ADDS only — a removal, an opacity change and a lift are untouched, exactly as with the staging gate it composes with. The backdrop map is also now born at the drawing's own zoom instead of a hardcoded 17.
- **MEASURED BOTH WAYS on the real app: six wasted DEM grid pulls at the wrong zoom → ZERO.** The same harness reports `the gate was answered ONCE, at the plan's real opening zoom`.
- **⛔ AND WHAT THIS SANDBOX COULD NOT SEE, reported as a blocker rather than as a pass.** Every `elevation.nationalmap.gov` request from Chromium here returns **ERR_CONNECTION_RESET** (curl succeeds — it rides the agent proxy), so the terrain layer can be observed ASKING but never ANSWERING and **no contour line can be made to paint**. The harness's third pass says exactly that and refuses to score itself. A harness that could not have seen an effect must never report its absence as a pass.
- **THE GUARD.** `e2e/layer-zoom-dormant.spec.js` asserts ZERO terrain grid pulls on opening a plan that settles below the gate — a blocked request is still an OBSERVED request, so it holds here exactly as it would with 3DEP reachable. **The reload in that case is load-bearing and is commented as such:** opening cold from the site list happens to mount the map AFTER the framing, so the gate is answered correctly by accident and a spec written that way **passes on the broken build**; re-entering on a remembered plan — how the owner arrives at a site he has been working on — mounts the map first. Proven red pre-fix (4 pulls) and green after.
- **Sandbox result:** as B323424 above; plus the diagnosis harness red on pre-fix and green on the fix.
