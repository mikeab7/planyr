- **The gap it closes.** Drawing a branch by hand meant starting a road in open ground, landing its FAR END on the through road, and hoping the connect caught. This starts FROM the junction — which is how a designer thinks about it — and removes the guesswork.
- **What makes the junction real (the non-obvious half).** A tee is recognised where a road's endpoint sits on another road's **INTERIOR VERTEX** (`teeJunctionsOf`), so the branch point must BE a vertex on the parent. `startRoadBranch` finds the nearest point on the parent's SPARSE centerline to the right-clicked world point, splices a vertex there via `insertRoadVertex` with a width-scaled `collapseFt` (reusing a vertex already essentially there rather than stacking a new one — the B1052 lesson), then seeds `draftRoadPts` at exactly that coordinate.
- **Everything downstream is the ORDINARY road-draw path** — ✓ Done / Enter / Esc / Backspace all behave as usual, and the junction geometry solves itself on the next render via the B1011 arms model. The branch inherits the parent's **class, travel width and curb** (`branchSeedRef`, consumed and cleared by `finishRoad`; also cleared on Esc/cancel so a later free-drawn road can't inherit a stale seed).
- **`onElContext` now records the WORLD point** of the right-click (`typeMenu.w`), so "from here" means the spot pointed at, not the element's centre.
- **Verified on the OWNER'S REAL PLAN** end-to-end through the real canvas (`ui-audit/verify-road-branch.mjs`): right-click the truck loop → the item appears → one click + ✓ Done → a new road that **inherits truck/40** and **resolves as a real tee (2 wedges, R=50)** on the parent, with the parent gaining its junction node. `e2e/road-branch-and-label.spec.js` covers the same path in CI.
