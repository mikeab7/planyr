- **ROOT CAUSE.** `NoteEditor.jsx`'s page-growth effect computed `growTop = max(0, anchorExtentTop(blocks) − padTop − bandH − TITLE_BAND_GAP)` — crediting the title band's own rendered height as "free" room for a box reaching above the document's origin, on the reasoning that padding-top growth is extra space the sheet already has to spend. That reasoning has a hole: `note-title`'s `<input>` is `width: 100%`, always, regardless of what the title says, so "free" space inside the band is not actually empty — a box landing there renders literally behind the title's own input. **And padding-top growth cannot fix this by construction, proven rather than argued**: padding sits BEFORE the band, so growing it shifts the band and a box measured from the document's origin DOWN TOGETHER, by the same amount, for any credit subtracted — the two never separate no matter how large the credit or the multiplier.
- **THE FIX.** The one distance that is NOT shared between the band and a box is the GAP AFTER the band, before the document's own origin (`TITLE_BAND_GAP`, previously a fixed 16px `marginBottom`). `growGap = max(0, anchorExtentTop(blocks) − TITLE_BAND_GAP)` now grows THAT margin instead of the sheet's padding-top (`sheetGrowGap`, replacing `sheetGrowTop`) — the band's own div auto-sizes to its real content (title alone, or title + the "Edited …"/project chips row), so the fix is correct for a tall band or a short one without reading its height at all. No coordinate migration: the document's stored `y` is untouched, same as B1370545's own promise.
- **PROVEN BY CONSTRUCTION, not by one lucky measurement.** Extending the growth math this way makes the invariant hold for ANY negative `y`, not just the reported one — verified across `y` of −10 / −30 / −60 / −90 / −260 in `ui-audit/verify-notes-free-placement.mjs` §6 (rewritten; its OLD assertion literally required the overlap and is corrected in the same commit, with a note explaining why the old version was the bug). All five clear the title's own rect; the red-proof (a real click at the geometric intersection point) is confirmed to fail on `origin/main` (`document.activeElement` reads `BODY`, matching the owner's own measurement) and pass on the fix (focuses `note-title`).
- **ADJACENT CASES, driven live rather than assumed, §6b of the same harness:**

  | Case | Result |
  |---|---|
  | Title short → long → short again | No overlap at any point — the fix does not read title length at all, so there is no dead zone to "clear." |
  | A note level with the CHIPS row ("Edited …"), not just the bare title | Renders below the chip row's own bottom, not merely below the title text — confirmed geometrically, not just below the title. |
  | The same, in dark mode | Unaffected — pure layout, no theme-token dependency. |
  | A title long enough to "wrap" to two lines | **N/A, confirmed rather than assumed** — `note-title` is a plain single-line `<input>`, which cannot wrap at any length; the harness asserts the tag itself rather than inferring it from the source. |
- **THE PLACEMENT PATH, not just the growth path.** A fresh press landing in the band's own row (before anything is placed) is unaffected — `focusFromMat` already excludes `input, textarea, select, button, a` from its own logic, so a press on the title's own hit-box (which spans the whole row) still focuses the title, never places a note under it. A press that DOES land on genuinely blank space (the margin between the band and the document, or the grey mat beside the sheet) still places a note as before; the very next layout pass (the same effect, keyed on `docTick`) re-measures `growGap` including the freshly-created anchor and pushes it clear before paint, so there is no visible frame where a newly placed note could sit inside the band.
