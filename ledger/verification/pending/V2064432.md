### V2064432 — B2619760: Sign in, Forgot password and Sign up all work behind the captcha on planyr.io, and on an iPhone-size WebKit

Sandbox-proven: `test/captchaGate.test.js` (13), `test/authCaptcha.test.js`, `test/authPanelTurnstile.test.js`, `e2e/auth-captcha.spec.js` (Chromium 9/9; mobile WebKit 45/45 over 5 repeats) — all with Cloudflare and Supabase MOCKED, so they prove the wire shape and the panel's behaviour, not that Cloudflare accepts a given browser. **Steps** (signed out, planyr.io; read `/version.json` and the served chunk hash in the SAME observation as each result; use the test account `e2e@planyr.test` with `E2E_EMAIL` / `E2E_PASSWORD`; delete anything created, without asking):
1. Open Sign in (any module — Food, Site, Schedule, Notes use the same panel). **Expect:** nothing new on the form (no widget box, no "verifying" text) unless Cloudflare asks for a challenge.
2. Enter the test email and a WRONG password, press Sign in. **Expect:** the plain server message "Invalid login credentials"; never the words "captcha", "disallowed" or "token".
3. Without reloading, enter the RIGHT password, press Sign in. **Expect:** the panel closes and the account email shows (the typo did not burn the retry).
4. Sign out, open Sign in, fill both fields and press Sign in immediately. **Expect:** it signs in (a brief "Verifying…" is fine); one press only.
5. Open Forgot password, enter the test email, press Send reset email. **Expect:** the normal reset confirmation, no captcha wording.
6. Switch Sign in → Forgot password → Sign in → Sign up → Sign in. **Expect:** exactly one check widget at any time; Sign up shows its visible box; signing in afterwards still works.
7. Open Sign up, fill it with a throwaway address, press Create account only as far as the first server answer. **Expect:** the form renders with its check and the request is accepted (signed in — email confirmation is OFF) or refused with a plain sentence. Delete the account it created.
8. Repeat 1–5 in Playwright WebKit at the iPhone 15 size, portrait and landscape. **Expect:** nothing clipped, the panel scrolls, the Sign in button reachable. (A real on-screen keyboard and the collapsing Safari toolbar are NOT provable in WebKit-on-Linux — say so.)
- **If Cloudflare refuses the headless browser:** record exactly which step it refused; that is not a pass and not a blocker for shipping; the live check on a real browser (the owner's iPhone / a signed-in Chrome) stays owed.
- **Stopping rule:** closes on a dated pass of 1–8 (8's keyboard note excepted), or a failed step filed as a recurrence on B2619760.
