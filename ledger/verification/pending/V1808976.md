### V1808976 — B1067 (×2): right-clicking a road gives the FULL menu, "Add control point" then "Add a T-intersection here" on top `Blocker: real-data`

**Why live:** the red-proof and fix were driven in the sandbox on a blank plan; this checks the same on a real plan with real roads (curved, multi-point, each class), on the owner's own account.

Steps (use a throwaway duplicate of a plan, never a real one):
1. Open a plan with a straight two-point road. Click the road to select it. Right-click mid-line. **Expect:** a full menu whose FIRST row is "Add control point" and SECOND is "Add a T-intersection here", followed by Arrange / Properties / Copy / Duplicate / Lock / Delete; no separate "Road" heading repeating the T.
2. Click "Add a T-intersection here", click where the new road should end, press ✓ Done. **Expect:** a new road tee'd to the host at the clicked spot, same class and width as the host, two curb returns.
3. Click empty ground to deselect, right-click the road again. **Expect:** the identical menu (selected and unselected must match).
4. Right-click mid-line, choose "Add control point". **Expect:** a control point appears exactly where you clicked, on the line.
5. Repeat on a multi-point curved road (each class: truck court / drive / 100 ft aisle). **Expect:** same two rows on top; the T inherits that road's class and width.
6. Right-click directly on a corner of a selected road. **Expect:** the small vertex menu (Delete control point / Roundabout at an end) — unchanged.
7. Lock the road, right-click it. **Expect:** the full menu with neither "Add control point" nor the T.
8. Right-click a selected easement strip, a selected polyline markup and a selected measurement line. **Expect:** each opens its own full menu with "Add control point" on top.
9. Delete the throwaway duplicate.
