What WAS done: (1)-(3) the original stale-origin fix, the fade-freeze fix, and the (×3) duration cap were each root-caused and verified as recorded in this entry's own history (unchanged, see the BACKLOG.md item for the full trace). Confirmed by reimplementing `flyTo`'s own zoom curve and finding the minimum zoom each jump sweeps to — Katy (~48km) barely dips, Austin/Maui dip 2+ and 7+ levels respectively, matching the owner's own capture. A "Loading imagery…" pill now ties to the tile layer's own loading state instead of leaving grey unexplained. Separately investigated, per the owner's explicit "say so plainly, do not assume": the reported mobile grey-BAND (clean horizontal split, sheet open) could NOT be reproduced from the bottom sheet mounting — `BottomSheet.jsx` is `position:fixed`, which Flexbox gives no layout space to, confirmed by instrumenting the real `FoodMap`/`VisitPanel` pair through the real mount sequence: Leaflet's own `_size` and the container's real bounding box stayed identical throughout. Shipped a `ResizeObserver`→`invalidateSize` binding anyway (nothing previously told Leaflet about a container resize outside of a flyTo/setView — a genuine gap regardless of this specific symptom's mechanism), verified live (`.scratch-repro/verify-resize-observer.mjs`) that a real viewport resize with no flyTo/setView call updates `map.getSize()` correctly.
