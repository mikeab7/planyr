### V445488 — B800848: the Contour lines layer no longer lags a real signed-in plan while panning/zooming `Blocker: live-GIS`
**What was verified here, extensively, without a live 3DEP round-trip completing.** This sandbox's Chromium cannot reach `elevation.nationalmap.gov` at all (`net::ERR_CONNECTION_RESET`, confirmed again this session — Node's own `fetch` reaches it, Playwright's Chromium does not), so no contour line has ever been paintable here through the real network path. To still measure something real rather than reason about it, `ui-audit/diagnose-contour-pan-perf.mjs` (new) intercepts every `exportImage` request and answers with the REAL captured LERC tile `test/fixtures/dep-katy-463x400.lerc`, which let contours actually render, repaint and be paced through a genuine pan sequence in a headless build for the first time. Two builds were compared — a `dist-before` from the pre-fix source and the fixed `dist` — driving an identical, real, continuous 14-step pan (never revisiting ground) at working zoom 18, with the per-step ground shift calibrated from the lattice tile's own real span rather than guessed in screen pixels. Measured via CDP `Performance.getMetrics`: **106.3 ms → 102.6 ms median main-thread work per pan step (−3.5%)**. Separately, and more rigorously, the exact mechanism the fix depends on was proven against the same real captured tile in Node-level unit tests (`test/terrainLattice.test.js`): repainting an identical view is a total no-op for the new diff (zero Leaflet objects added or removed), and dropping one tile from the cover (simulating it panning out of view) retires only the geometry that actually touched it while an untouched tile's own contours keep their identity and are never rebuilt.
**What is NOT proven, and why it parks here.** Nobody has opened a real, signed-in (or logged-out) plan on `planyr.io`, turned on "Contour lines (1 ft)" at a working zoom, and panned/zoomed around to confirm it *feels* less laggy — that needs the real 3DEP service, which this sandbox cannot reach. The measured −3.5% end-to-end improvement is honest but smaller than the owner's report would suggest is needed; profiling why turned up a SEPARATE, larger cost (`composeContourPaint`'s full per-level recomposition, ~24 ms/repaint, unrelated to this fix) that is filed as its own follow-up (**B800849**) rather than rushed into this same change. A possible redundant tile-refetch pattern was also observed and filed, honestly, as unconfirmed (**B800850**) — it may be a defect, or it may be an artifact of this session's specific test harness; either way it needs a real network path to settle.
**Steps, each with a named expected result:**
1. On `planyr.io` (signed in or out), open or start a plan near a real US location with 3DEP LiDAR coverage (most of the continental US). Zoom in to site/neighborhood scale (roughly the zoom you'd draw buildings at). **Expect:** the Layers panel is reachable and "Contour lines (1 ft)" is listed under GIS layers.
2. Turn on "Contour lines (1 ft)". **Expect:** after a brief load, thin brown 1-ft contour lines appear, with heavier lines and elevation labels every 5 ft — no change in appearance or precision from before this fix.
3. Pan around continuously (drag the map) for 10–15 seconds, covering new ground rather than panning back and forth over the same spot. **Expect:** noticeably less stutter/freeze than before — the owner's originally reported lag should be reduced. (It may not be eliminated — see the honest caveat above; if it still lags badly, that's evidence for B800849/B800850, not a regression in this fix.)
4. Zoom in and out a few times with contours on. **Expect:** same as step 3 — smoother than the pre-fix build, not necessarily perfect.
5. Compare a "before" feel if possible (the previous deployed build, or ask whether it felt different before this shipped). **Expect:** an improvement is perceptible, even if modest.
