### V1011856 — B1395568: the "per year / per month" toggle survives a reload and follows the account to a second device, and the map's Comps rail never disagrees with the card `Blocker: auth`

**Why this needs a real pass, and exactly how far the sandbox already got.** The toggle's own mechanics — flipping the featured rate, the peer scale, both tick labels and the sentence together, without also opening the comp underneath it — are driven live in this session against the REAL `CompsCard` component in a real headless browser (see below), not just asserted in a unit test. What a sandbox cannot do is sign in: the proxy CORS-blocks the Supabase auth handshake, so the one thing left is whether the choice actually round-trips through `profiles.prefs.compsRatePeriod` — surviving a reload, and following the account to a second device/browser — and whether the map's Comps rail (a signed-in-only surface: comps are account data) reads back the identical choice the card wrote. This is a narrow residual, not an unverified feature.

**What was verified here (this session, real headless Chromium, real clicks, logged out).**
1. A scratch harness (`scratch-comps-check.html`/`.jsx`, deleted before commit — not part of the shipped diff) rendered the real `dashboard/components/CompsCard.jsx` with `dashboard/lib/compsCardModel.js`'s real `buildCompsCardData`, fed a fixture shaped like Michael's own production comps (a featured lease recorded ANNUAL, its Harris County peers a mix of annual and monthly). Clicking the real "per month" button: the featured figure went `$6.00/SF/yr` → `$0.50/SF/mo`, the scale ticks went `$5.76`–`$6.20` → `$0.48`–`$0.52`, and the comparison sentence ("tied for second highest of the four, and right at the median…") was byte-identical before and after — correct, since rank/median are period-invariant. All four moved in the one click; zero console/page errors.
2. **The toggle never also opens the comp.** Clicking the toggle button left an `onOpenComp` counter at 0; clicking elsewhere on the same card ("this one") incremented it to 1 — confirms the toggle's `stopPropagation` actually stops the card's own whole-card click handler from also firing.
3. The Dashboard was driven signed-out at `#/dashboard`: renders cleanly, the Comps card shows its ordinary empty state ("No comps recorded yet.") with NO toggle (correctly — there is no rate to convert), zero console errors.
4. `test/comps.test.js` (12 new), `test/compsCardModel.test.js` (5 new), `test/compsRatePeriodPrefs.test.js` (new, the pure `_normalizePeriod` half) — full repo suite green, 16,240 tests. `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm run perf:bundle`, `node ui-audit/design-drift-audit.mjs`, `node ui-audit/doc-pointer-audit.mjs` all clean.
